# Ogo

A stepped stone pyramid rising just high enough to sit beneath the forest canopy, south of Gulg in the deep Crescent Forest. Home of the halfling chief **Urga-Zoltapl** and the seat of forest-halfling authority.

## At a glance

- **What it is** A pyramid perhaps seventy-five feet tall with the chief's palace on top and a dozen stone houses for his servants and wives scattered around the base.
- **The people** Forest halflings — the original inhabitants of the wood, older than any city on the ring, and not remotely like the halflings of other continents.
- **Politics** Urga-Zoltapl supplies **two hundred halfling scouts** to Urik under a standing arrangement, paid in obsidian. This makes Ogo the only forest power with a formal treaty on the ring.

## Traveler's notes

- Ogo is unique among halfling settlements in that an outsider may reasonably hope to visit and leave again. This is a real distinction and it is worth understanding why the qualifier exists.
- Travel in the company of halflings from Urik, who have safe conduct. Alone, you are relying entirely on the chief finding you more interesting as a conversationalist than as a guest at dinner.
- Bring a gift, speak plainly, and do not laugh at anything you are told.
