# Mind Meld

*The cat gives you one of its memories and takes one of yours.*

## The vision

A camp at night. Not yours. Tents of hide, a fire banked low, people sleeping around it whose faces you do not know and whose names you do — that is the first wrong thing. You know the name of the woman nearest the fire. You know she snores. You know she gave you meat when there was not enough.

You are lying at the edge of the firelight with your chin on your paws.

That is the second wrong thing, and by the time you notice it, it is not wrong anymore.

## The beast

When you stand up you are yourself again, and the cat is beside you. A [tagster](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#tagster): long, low, tawny, with a spined ridge down its back and eyes that hold the firelight a little too long. The free peoples raise them from kittens, and a tribe's cats share the tribe's Will. This one has been sharing it with you for the last hour, to see if you could carry it.

It looks at you. There is no boundary where it is looking from.

It gives you something. A memory, whole: a morning, cold, the smell of a particular person's hands, the sound the tents make in wind. It is not important. It is just *true*, and it is now yours, and you will never be sure it did not happen to you.

Then it takes one. You feel it go — a small thing, a morning of your own. Not stolen. Traded. The cat will carry it back to a fire you have never sat at, and someone there will remember a thing they did not do.

The cat yawns, which is the only goodbye a cat gives, and lies down at the edge of the light.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your guide is looking at you with an expression you cannot read, and then — for just a moment — you can. It is *relief*. Then it closes again.

Blue at the edge of your eye. There is a morning in your head that is not yours.

## The gift

**Mind Meld.** *As a bonus action, you and one willing creature you can see can speak telepathically until the end of your turn, and it shares with you one memory of its choosing.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a tawny shape at the edge of a fire that is not yours.
