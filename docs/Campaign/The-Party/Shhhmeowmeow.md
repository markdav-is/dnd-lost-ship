## Backstory

Two threads are established; the rest is the player's to tell.

- **The northern continent.** Shhhmeowmeow hails from the north — the land of the tower journey in the previous campaign, where the party found the **Black Dragon** (named **Essos** on Eustace's sheet) and Shhhmeowmeow bonded it under the Prophecy of the Red Dragon ([The Dragons](../../World/The-Dragons.md)). The dragon sleeps now; the Orb of Dragonkind in the pack is the other half of that bond.
- **The fire city under Eldorado.** The player has a backstory reason to reach the **fire city** — one of Vermoon's buried elemental cities, a Titan at its heart, lying under the rim town of Eldorado on Aerun ([The Elemental Cities](../../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md); [Eldorado](../../Aerun-Players-Guide/Gazetteer/Eldorado.md)). **This has nothing to do with the infection, Rajaat, or the Lighthouse** — it is a personal thread, and the wiki keeps it separate from the cure plot. It is the second reason the party goes to Aerun ([Beneath the Lighthouse](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/Beneath-the-Lighthouse.md)): Erleena, an archaeologist who has dug there, is the one who names the place. *What* Shhhmeowmeow wants in the fire city, and why, is still the player's story — ask before the arc reaches the rim.

## The dragon bond

Shhhmeowmeow hails from the **northern continent** — the land of the tower journey where the party found the **[Black Dragon](../../World/The-Dragons.md)**, their paired dragon of the Red Dragon prophecy. The Black Dragon now sleeps with the rest; the dragons only stir for a world-scale crisis, and whether the old bond can rouse it if the infection becomes one is one of the campaign's held cards.

## DM hooks

- The party's scout and trap-finder; the plant-magic advantage makes them the natural point character for garden and mold terrain.
- **The Nine Lives Stealer** is a decapitation threat (crits vs. low-HP creatures can kill outright) — but its victims' souls feed the blade. Against *infected people the party means to cure*, that bow is a moral hazard worth playing: the best shot in the party holding the one weapon the [Counteragent](../../Game-Mechanics/The-Counteragent.md) doctrine says not to use.
- Outlander background pairs well with the Frostwind Crossing and Barrier Peaks survival play.
- Homeland hook: the northern continent — dragon tower country — is unexplored territory in the current arc.
- **Eldorado.** When Erleena says the name beneath the Lighthouse, this is the player whose reaction matters. Let it land; don't explain it for them.
