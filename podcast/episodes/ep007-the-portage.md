## scene: welcome [dry]

WANDERER: [warmly] Good white hours, friends, wherever the gray finds you — Wandering Aerun, channel forty-three, and tonight we go down the stair. The portage! The Cochlea of Balic, where everything the silt carries meets everything the road carries, and several hundred porters move a sea's worth of cargo between them twice a day. For six tendays it has been the slowest hundred feet on the continent — and friends, as of this morning, it isn't anymore. Something happened at the foot of that stair last night, and I was standing on the second turn when it did. I've got a drover on the band, I've got a letter I'll be keeping, and I've got a story. Pour the tea.

## scene: interview [radio]

WANDERER: My guest tonight is calling from the queue itself — Harl, twenty years a drover on the Ring Road, argosy eleven of fourteen. Harl, you've been parked at that stair for a tenday and a half. How are the beasts taking it?

DROVER: [easy, unbothered] Better than the factors, Wanderer. Nothing bothers those animals when I'm on the line — twenty years and never one spook. [modest chuckle] It's a knack. My old da had it, and his da. And honestly, a parked argosy's no hardship: feed the team, walk the harness, check forty feet of strap an inch at a time. And then there's the tanks, at the heart of everything. Enough water for the crews, and then some — strictly off limits to the likes of me. [beat] You don't go down there, and you don't knock. That's not a rule anybody wrote down. That's just how a hull stays lucky.

WANDERER: [quietly delighted] Every trade on this continent keeps one door shut, friends. I've stopped asking what's behind them; I've started collecting the doors. [beat] Harl — the porters. You sat in the longest queue the Cochlea has ever produced because of them. Where does a drover land?

DROVER: [no hesitation] With them, and loud. Those gangs move a sea twice a day, Wanderer. I haul freight four hundred miles; they haul it the last hundred feet, straight up, in the heat, on city rations, and the city keeps the rate. [beat] We weren't delayed, friends. We were waiting. There's a difference, and every hull in that queue knew it.

WANDERER: [softly] We weren't delayed, we were waiting. Hold that, friends — and Harl, stay on the band, because you were there for the rest of it.

DROVER: [warm] Top of my own hull, Wanderer, with the team fed early. Wouldn't have missed it for a clear road.

## scene: the-performance [radio]

WANDERER: Last night, at the foot of the Cochlea, on a stage built of freight pallets between argosy nine and the first turn — the Salt View Principal Troupe performed the Fall of Yaramuke for the porters of Balic. The factors paid for it. They booked a distraction, friends. Madame Ossia does not sell distractions. I stood on the second turn and watched three hundred porters watch that play, and when the tower came down — the real tower, the real smoke — the silence that came off those gangs was not grief. It was recognition. And underneath it, the thing Ossia has been saying from a stage her whole life: what you carry matters. What you are matters. Every turn of that stair heard it.

PAUSE: 0.9

WANDERER: And the Schools came. All five, friends — no banners, no speeches. They walked the turns while the play ran. A Mender binding stair-hands at the second landing, no charge, the mark plain on her brow. A Reckoner reading the city's rate card aloud — just reading it, slowly, with an eyebrow — which at the Cochlea counts as inflammatory literature. Kenaz watching footing. The Academy at the rail, hooded, saying what the Academy always says, which is nothing. And when the play ended, seven porters walked off that stair in School cloaks. Seven. Contracts bought from the city's ledger and burned before the smoke cleared — the Schools don't buy people, friends; they buy the paper. Where other lands raise temples, Aerun raises schools, and last night I finally understood the saying from underneath.

PAUSE: 0.9

WANDERER: And this morning, the stair moved. Same porters, same rations, same city — and the baskets flew, friends, because the gangs decided they were done, on their own count, in their own time, the way the whole thing began. The city posted a water increase by noon and called it "seasonal." [dry] Seasonal. Nobody was bought last night, friends, and nobody was freed, and I won't pretend otherwise — the stair belongs to Balic tonight same as it did a tenday ago. But three hundred people heard out loud what they're worth, seven of them walked out of the ledger entirely, and the factors learned what a queue costs. [beat] One more thing. The booking for last night came through a Nibenay broker, and I looked into the broker, because looking into things is the job. The broker does not exist. [pause] I have a guess. I'm keeping it.
