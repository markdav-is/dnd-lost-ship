# Mystic Hand

*It lifts a single stone and sets it in your palm without touching it.*

## The vision

The lip of the wall. You are standing at the very edge, where the silt-fall goes over, and the dust is pouring past your boots into two thousand feet of air. The plume rises. The wind off the gray comes up the cliff face and lifts it, and the whole fall hangs there, smoking, going up as fast as it goes down.

Things ride the updraft. You have heard that. You had not thought about what it meant.

## The beast

It comes up the face on the rising dust: a [floating mantle](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#floating-mantle), a ray the width of a cart, flat and fringed, rippling the way cloth ripples. Nothing holds it up. It does not flap. It simply does not fall, and the dust goes around it.

It hangs in front of you, at the edge, level with your chest. Close enough to see the eyes along the leading fringe, small and black and many.

There is a stone at your feet. A chip of ochre rock, smaller than your thumb. The mantle looks at it.

The stone lifts.

Slowly. No wind takes it; the wind is going the other way. It rises as if the air under it had thickened, turns once in the light, and drifts across the gap between you and the mantle and back, and settles into your open palm with the weight of exactly one small stone.

The mantle did not touch it. You did not touch it until it arrived. And in the drift of it across that gap, you felt — very faintly, like a thread pulled across a finger — the *holding*. Where it began. What it felt like to be the thing that did it.

The mantle tilts, catches the updraft, and goes over the lip into the gray, and is gone.

You close your hand on the stone.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your hand is closed. When you open it there is a chip of ochre rock in the palm, and the nearest ochre rock is two thousand feet down.

Blue at the edge of your eye.

## The gift

**Mystic Hand.** *As an action, you move one loose object weighing 5 pounds or less that you can see within 30 feet, up to 30 feet.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a fringed shadow hanging level in rising dust.
