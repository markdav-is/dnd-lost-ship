# The Sandwalkers

A loose confederation of desert survivor tribes on [Aerun](/World/Aerun) — the people who watched the world die once and chose to move rather than fight what they could not stop.

## Culture

- **Witnesses, not scholars.** Oral histories stretch back to the Green Age, carried in song, scar, and the careful reading of ruins. *"The sand remembers everything. The problem is that most people don't speak sand."*
- Primal shamanism: they listen to the spirits of the land. Their most feared omen is the **Defiling Silence** — the absence of sound that precedes the death of a place.
- They have **never accepted Aura implants** and carry no trackable technology. To the network, a Sandwalker is a ghost.
- Language: Common, a Primordial desert dialect, and Sandwalker sign.
- They trade information across vast distances — word of a scientist dying inside an alien vessel reached the deep desert before the party ever arrived.

## Vocabulary the DM can use

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| *vel-keth* | "The hunger that eats the world" — the force that made Aerun a desert; Kaelen's name for the ship's infection |
| The Defiling Silence | The primal spirits going quiet as the land's living energy is drained faster than it regenerates |
| The Green Age | Aerun before the fall — see [The Green Age](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age) |

## In the campaign

Their emissary is **[Kaelen al-Hajra](/NPCs/Kaelen-al-Hajra)**, chosen to cross the sea and find the source of the Silence. If the campaign travels to Aerun, the Sandwalkers are guides, oracles, and the only people who can walk the party past the network's — and the desert's — dangers.
