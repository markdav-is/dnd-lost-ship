# Eldorado — the Rim Town

Inland behind Tyr, at a high pass where the road climbs toward the cliff country. The last place to buy rope before the high country, and the first place to hear a lie about what's up there.

## At a glance

- **Size** A few hundred permanent residents; the transient population doubles it and turns over constantly.
- **Living** Outfitting. Rope, water-skins, dust-veils, climbing irons, mules, guides — all of it dear, because where else are you going to get it?
- **Reputation** Gold-fever with no gold. Prospectors have been going up from Eldorado for centuries; a few come back with salvage, most come back poorer, and some do not come back.

## What a visitor sees

One long street of outfitters and drink-houses, a chapel, a water-master's office, and a great deal of gear for sale second-hand — which is to say, sold by people who returned, and by the families of people who didn't. The wind off the heights carries fine gray dust that gets into food, eyes, and lungs; locals wear veils without thinking about it.

## Traveler's notes

- Every guide in Eldorado claims to know the high country. Ask how many crossings they have made and who came back; the honest ones give small numbers.
- Sandwalker traders occasionally come down to barter. They are courteous, they do not drink, and they will not guide you up. Not for any price. Do not press it.
- Buy more water than you think you need. This is the standard advice and the graveyard is full of people who found it excessive.
