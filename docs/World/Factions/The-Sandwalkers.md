# The Sandwalkers

The deep desert tribes of [Aerun](/World/Aerun) — the people who watched the world die once, adapted to the cure that scarred it, and have served for a thousand years as the unchosen **wardens of Rajaat**.

## Culture

- **Witnesses, not scholars.** Oral histories stretch back to the Green Age, carried in song, scar, and the careful reading of ruins. *"The sand remembers everything. The problem is that most people don't speak sand."*
- A culture of **harsh religious discipline and survival** in the realm of capricious storms beyond the Rim Wall — entirely off the grid, invisible to the network Jak thinks is the world.
- Primal shamanism: they listen to the spirits of the land. Their most feared omen is the **Defiling Silence** — the absence of sound that precedes the death of a place. The name is not metaphor: their ancestors *heard* it when the defilers unmade the continent's green.
- They have **never accepted Aura implants** and carry no trackable technology.
- They **revere the great worms** as forces of nature that maintain the balance of the world — because, uniquely, they know that this is literally true.
- Language: Common, a Primordial desert dialect, and Sandwalker sign. They trade information across vast distances.

## The wardenship

The Sandwalkers guard **[Rajaat — the First Source](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source)**: the buried origin of the spore infection, contained for a thousand years by the quarantine-desert their ancestors made and the worm ecology that closes the loop. *"Contained only by sand and worms and the prayers of people the coastal cities have never met."* They did not choose the duty; they inherited it. A true cure — the kind the ship might yield — would give them a choice for the first time. This is Kaelen's quiet, personal stake in the party's success.

**Their secrets, in rings:** the Rajaat story (now told at the Council of Frostwatch) · the defiler method and the [College](/World/Factions/The-Defiler-College) (yielded under pressure) · the full worm ecology and **the Spice's true nature** (never — the world's economy runs on not knowing).

## Vocabulary the DM can use

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rajaat** | The First Source — the place where it began; the name is not written on any coastal map |
| *vel-keth* | "The hunger that eats the world" — the infection, in both its outbreaks |
| The Defiling Silence | The primal spirits going quiet as living energy drains faster than it regenerates |
| The Green Age | Aerun before the outbreak and its cure — see [The Green Age](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age) |

## In the campaign

Their emissary is **[Kaelen al-Hajra](/NPCs/Kaelen-al-Hajra)**, sent to track the Defiling Silence to its new, second source. If the campaign travels to Aerun, the Sandwalkers are guides, oracles, and gatekeepers of the Deep Desert — and the only people who can broker an audience with what lives beneath it.
