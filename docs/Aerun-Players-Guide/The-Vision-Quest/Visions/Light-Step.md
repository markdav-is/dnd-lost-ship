# Light Step

*Weight is a decision.*

## The vision

Gray. Nothing but gray, flat to every edge of the world. You are standing on it.

You should not be. You know what the silt does. A person sinks; an animal sinks; there is no swimming in it and no bottom. But you are standing, and the dust is soft under your boots, and it is holding — just — the way thin ice holds, with a feeling of *for now*.

You take a step. You sink to the ankle. You stop. You sink to the shin.

Somewhere out on the flat, something small is laughing at you. It is not a kind laugh. It is not a cruel one either. It is the laugh of a thing that has watched a hundred people do exactly this.

## The beast

It comes skimming. A [silt runner](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-runner): a reptile the size of a child, long-toed, ridge-backed, running across the dust as if the dust were a floor. It does not sink. It does not *consider* sinking. It circles you twice, close enough to touch, and the silt under its feet barely dimples.

It stops. It looks at your buried shins, and at your face, and makes a sound you will think of afterward as a question.

Then it does the thing. Not a trick of the feet — you watch the feet, and there is no trick there. It simply decides, somewhere behind the eyes, how much it weighs. And the answer is: *not enough to matter*.

You feel the shape of the decision. It is smaller than you thought. It is not *I am light*. It is *I have not agreed to be heavy*.

You lift one foot. The silt lets it go. You put it down, and it does not sink.

The runner is already gone, a line of dust toward the horizon.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. You are on your feet before you know you meant to be, and your guide, who was halfway to helping you up, stops with a hand out and nothing to do with it.

Blue at the edge of your eye. Dust on your boots to the shin, and none above.

## The gift

**Light Step.** *As a bonus action, your speed increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn, and you can stand up from prone without spending movement.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: long toes on the gray, not sinking.
