# Beacon

*The sun is a beast, and it is not angry.*

## The vision

Noon. You are lying on your back in the white hours, the way everyone on Aerun lies still, except there is no shade and no wall and no roof, only the sky.

The sun is looking at you.

That is the whole of it for a long time. Not heat — you are not burning. Attention. The kind a very large animal pays to a very small one that has wandered into its field, not hungry, just noticing. The sky is not blue up there. It is white, and then gold, and then it has a shape.

## The beast

The shape comes down out of the glare, and the glare comes with it. A [fire drake](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#elemental-drake): a reptile the length of an argosy, scaled in slag and ember, the air around it running like water. Every valley knows its drake by name. This one does not tell you its name.

It lowers its head until one eye is over you, and the eye is the sun, and you look into it because there is nothing else to look at.

It is not angry. That is the thing you will remember. You had always thought of the sun as an enemy, the way everyone on the coast does. It is only awake. It is only *looking*.

It breathes out, once, gently, and a single spark lands on your chest. It does not burn. It sits there, warm, like a coal in a hand that knows how to hold one.

*Carry that,* the drake does not say. *It is a small part of my looking. Put it where you need it.*

It lifts its head and the sun goes back to being the sun.

## The waking

Gray light. The ring of stones. Cold, for the first time since the climb. Your guide has a hand cupped over something and when you sit up the guide opens it, and there is nothing there, and the guide nods as if that were the answer.

Blue at the edge of your eye. Warm, somehow.

## The gift

**Beacon.** *As a bonus action, you shed bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light 20 feet beyond, in any color you choose, for one hour.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: one eye, open, the size of the noon sky.
