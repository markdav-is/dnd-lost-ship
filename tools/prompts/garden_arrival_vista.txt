First-person view from the top of the garden, just stepped out of the round north gate chamber onto a flagstone landing on the highest terrace. The whole garden falls away below and ahead: jungle stepping down in 5-foot terraces toward the centre, a second lower grassy wall with its own round gate ring enclosing the inner garden, and in the exact middle, far off, a still black lake with a small wooded island. On the island stands the same slender white lighthouse as the reference, lower half wrapped in russet growth, its lamp turning slowly, a pale beam sweeping through the orange spore haze that pools thick over the lake. To the left and right the outer 20-foot grass-covered wall curves away in long straight octagon sides until it disappears into the haze on the far side, 500 feet away. Overhead the vast pale ribbed ceiling and the blazing sun-lamp. Four-winged birds wheel far out over the canopy. Bright warm daylight, enormous sense of scale — a whole world under a roof. Ultra-wide establishing shot.
