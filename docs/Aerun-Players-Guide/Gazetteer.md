# A Traveler's Gazetteer of Aerun

*Being a plain account of every place marked upon the atlas, set down for merchants, pilgrims, and fools — with such warnings as the compiler judges useful and such rumors as he judges entertaining.*

**How to use this:** these entries are **player-facing and spoiler-free** — what an educated traveler on the Ring Road could learn in a tavern or from a Guild factor. Hand these out freely. Start with [Aerun — A Primer](Aerun-A-Primer.md) for the shape of the continent and its powers; for the five psionic academies — what everyone knows and what a lore check turns up — see the companion primer, [The Great Schools of Aerun](The-Great-Schools.md). The DM-side material lives under [World](../World.md) and [Campaign](../Campaign.md).
