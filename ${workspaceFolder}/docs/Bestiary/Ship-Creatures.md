## DM patterns

- The menagerie doesn't coordinate — but the mold is starting to network parts of it. An escaped creature (the cloaker precedent) is a ready-made "the ship is leaking" beat.
- Lure-predator-lure chains: globe palm scent → predators; squealer's distress calls → rescuers; the bunny path → the maw. The garden *hunts by kindness*.
