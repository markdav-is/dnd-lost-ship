## Backstory

The sheet is blank, but Andy's 2023 campaign board (`assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json`, card *Shhhmeowmeow* and the Taldea cards) is not. The pitch, in three lines from the card: **Tabaxi Ranger. Looking for the mythical city of Eldorado. Motivated by gold and hates dragons.**

- **Nepeta.** Shhhmeowmeow comes from **Nepeta**, a city of tabaxi in the forest around Lost Spire on [Taldea](../../World/Taldea.md). **A dragon came at night and destroyed it** — nobody could say what color in the dark. The survivors tried to rebuild, but the ground turned on them: landslides, then swamp, then a swamp that burned the hands of anyone who worked it. They took it as a sign from **Zaltec** to leave. The land is dead and toxic now; acid seeps up and warps or kills whatever grows.
- **The survivors** live in an encampment outside the town of **3-Way**. **YawnPurPur** sells their wares in Shimmerhill and drives his wagon back every week; he is the one who told the party what happened after the attack. Other names from home, off Andy's list: *SHHHPftPftPft, SHHHPur, YeowalMeow, GrowlPft.*
- **The dragon was Essos.** Under the Bolt Mountains the party walked into the lair of the Black Dragon who destroyed Nepeta — and he turned out to be the Earth Titan's sworn protector, the one who told them about the prophecy, and then Shhhmeowmeow's own paired dragon ([The Dragons](../../World/The-Dragons.md)). His answer to the anger: *we all have our part to play, and fate's weave has never been easy.* Had Shhhmeowmeow attacked, he would have left rather than fight. Afterward, Shhhmeowmeow **guilting Essos** into things was a standing mechanic: advantage on the ask.
- **What neither of them did.** The poison in Nepeta's ground is not dragon acid. The attack ruptured **Antari batteries** buried under Lost Rock, and Lost Rock itself is the horn of the Earth Titan, writhing. Whether Shhhmeowmeow ever learned that is *ask the table*.
- **Hates dragons; bonded to one.** That is the character as of the last campaign, and the board never resolves it. Andy's to-do list ends with ***"Shhhmeow's Dragon Orb"*** unchecked — though an attuned Orb of Dragonkind is on the current sheet.
- **The Antari sigil.** Shhhmeowmeow found the sigil that Erleena identified as the mark of [the Antari](../../World/The-Antari.md).
- **Faith.** The card notes the "Newla, Mastikian pantheon" — the sheet's **Nula**, Maztican. (Nepeta's people at large looked to Zaltec.)
- **The fire city under Eldorado.** The quest the character was *made* for — and the one city the previous campaign never reached; "Eldorado exploration and outcomes" was still on Andy's loose-ends list at the end. It is one of Vermoon's buried elemental cities, under the rim town of Eldorado on Aerun ([The Elemental Cities](../../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md), which now holds everything Andy prepared about it; [Eldorado](../../Aerun-Players-Guide/Gazetteer/Eldorado.md)). What Yorrem's library told the party years ago: the Fallen's city, *gold buildings and many riches*. **This has nothing to do with the infection, Rajaat, or the Lighthouse** — it is a personal thread, and the wiki keeps it separate from the cure plot. It is the second reason the party goes to Aerun ([Beneath the Lighthouse](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/Beneath-the-Lighthouse.md)): Erleena, an archaeologist who has dug there, is the one who names the place. Gold is the stated motive; whether there is more to it is still the player's story — ask before the arc reaches the rim.

## The dragon bond

Shhhmeowmeow hails from the **northern continent** — the land of the tower journey where the party found the **[Black Dragon](../../World/The-Dragons.md)**, their paired dragon of the Red Dragon prophecy: **Essos**, protector of the Earth Titan Kaia, and the dragon that destroyed Nepeta. The Black Dragon now sleeps with the rest; the dragons only stir for a world-scale crisis, and whether the old bond can rouse it if the infection becomes one is one of the campaign's held cards.

## DM hooks

- The party's scout and trap-finder; the plant-magic advantage makes them the natural point character for garden and mold terrain.
- **The Nine Lives Stealer** is a decapitation threat (crits vs. low-HP creatures can kill outright) — but its victims' souls feed the blade. Against *infected people the party means to cure*, that bow is a moral hazard worth playing: the best shot in the party holding the one weapon the [Counteragent](../../Game-Mechanics/The-Counteragent.md) doctrine says not to use.
- Outlander background pairs well with the Frostwind Crossing and Barrier Peaks survival play.
- Homeland hook: the northern continent — dragon tower country — is unexplored territory in the current arc. The Nepeta survivors are still camped outside 3-Way, and their old home is still an acid swamp over a leaking Antari battery bank.
- **Eldorado.** When Erleena says the name beneath the Lighthouse, this is the player whose reaction matters. Let it land; don't explain it for them.
