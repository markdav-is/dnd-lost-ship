# Hollowstorm

Out on the western promontory, where the coast juts into deep water and the weather arrives first. A fishing town with a name it earned.

## At a glance

- **Size** Around a thousand souls, more in fishing season.
- **Living** The western fisheries — the richest waters on Aerun, because the ocean here is cold, deep, and untroubled by anything the continent does.
- **The name** For the sound. Winter gales come off the open sea and funnel through the rock arches below the headland; the whole promontory hums. Locals sleep through it and visitors do not.

## Traveler's notes

- Hollowstorm eats better than any city on the ring and knows it. Ask for the smoked catch.
- The town is the wettest, greenest spot on the continent — everything the westerly winds carry falls here and on the mountains behind. A traveler from the interior may find the damp genuinely unpleasant after a lifetime of dust.
- Ships put in for water and repairs, not cargo; the Guild has no yard here, and small independent captains like it that way.
