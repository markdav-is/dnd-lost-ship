# Taldea

The northern continent of tinkerers, mines, and mountains — Drytide and its Great Race, Shimmerhill on its metal shelf, the buried earth city of Dirtroit, the Black Dragon's lair, and the poisoned swamp of Lost Rock that used to be Shhhmeowmeow's home.

Source: Andy's 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (lists *Restoring Power* and *Travel Back to Yorrem*). Prep notes, not a transcript. Placement on the continent follows the [world map](../World.md).

## Drytide

A **port city of the [Tinkerers of Thingamajigs](Factions/The-Second-Age-Guilds.md)** — mixed-use streets, a shop below and a home above, inventors building from salvage or from scratch. Greasy shorts and shirts, garages instead of bars.

- **The Rusty Cog** — 15 sp a night with food. The innkeeper is a warforged named **Mangle**.
- **The Artificer** — adds magical-mechanical improvements to equipment, "for a price. And it's quite a price."
- **The Kart Shop** — rent a kart instead of a mount: twice as fast, roads and smooth ground only.
- **[Rico](../NPCs/Rico.md)**, the young upstart with the engine.
- **Ships out:** to Yorrem, to Hollowstorm (the midway stop for everything crossing the world), to Blackguard on [Aerun](Aerun.md), to Direport or Fairstone on Malandara. Airships run to Hollowstorm and the Holy Citadel, about weekly.

### The Great Race

Six miles, no rules, and most years somebody doesn't finish. The field the year the party ran it, with Rico's scrap-built lifted truck:

| Team | Ride |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The Holy Ones** (Devout Coalition) | A bulky, shiny, lifted golden truck etched with the symbols of many gods |
| **Tinkers Thinkers** | Every idea anyone had, bolted together |
| **Geeee Force** (Gleaners) | Small, natural, perfectly camouflaged — for a forest. On sand it stands out |
| **Shimmerhill's Shineys** | Big, metallic, crewed by attractive people in jewels; the distraction *is* the strategy |

Rico and the party **tied for second**. Drytide partied in the streets at its garages, opinions of Rico turned around, and that night a cloaked man was found reading Rico's engine notes — see *Burl* on [The Dragons](The-Dragons.md).

*(Andy's race rules, if it is ever run again: twelve rounds. Each round every car rolls Dex — success takes the **Lead**, partial stays in the **Pack**, failure drops **Behind**. Then one action each: **Attack** to hang a condition on a rival, **Maneuver** to climb a position, a car-specific **Special**, or **Repair** by dropping a position to clear a condition. Three conditions knocks a car out. Most rounds in the Lead wins 3,000 gp; second place 1,500.)*

## The road to Shimmerhill

Five days on foot, two and a half by kart. Bandits with a once-a-day blasting artifact work the road; so does a blink dog with a harengon accomplice who speaks Sylvan and steals your things.

**The Shrine of Skerrit.** Off the road, a great stone centaur among columns, water, and plants, with stairs going down sized for centaurs. **Skerrit** is the god of centaurs, fauns, and satyrs; beside him stands his champion the **Forest Walker**, a cloaked figure hard to make out. The two are said to inhabit wild animals to lead the faithful to their destiny. Among the offerings: a **broken bow** marked with an oak, its string cut, and a note — *"Skerrit, I rebuke thee, take back your gifts I have no further need for them."* Andy's planning list names the thread — *"the Fallen Paladin of Skerrit — Venat?"* — and it was never resolved. It was laid for [Musty-Jo](../Campaign/The-Party/Musty-Jo-Mustang.md).

## Shimmerhill

A **Gleaners Guild mining city** on the flank of the **Bolt Mountain Range**, directly under **Shelf Mountain** — named for the long **metal protrusion** jutting from it, which the city is built on and around. The hills shimmer with minerals; airships lift ore out to other continents.

- **On top of the Shelf:** homes, shops, offices — the Gleaners' head office, the G4CE office, the Academy of the Fettered Mind (on the edge, overlooking the valley), the Devout Coalition's temple.
- **Under the Shelf:** mines, smelters, workshops, warehouses, the Tinkerers — and the **Tarnished Tankard**, the seedy bar over the [Titan Fighters'](Factions/The-Titan-Fighters.md) hideout.
- **Why all four factions are here:** the miners broke into caves and **ruins under the mountains**.
- **People:** **Esmerelda Underchin**, the Gleaners' no-nonsense Head of Blasting and Burrowing · **Magrath** of *Magrath's Maps*, a dwarf mapmaker who charts the new-dug caves · **YawnPurPur**, a tabaxi of Nepeta who sells his people's wares here and drives a wagon to 3-Way every week.
- **Odd thing on an Academy bench:** a lump of **earth that moves** rhythmically, soft as liquid clay, humming with power — found in an Antari room in the caves.

## Under the mountains

The miners noticed that the ores here lie in the rock "like they were stirred into an earth slurry and set." The route under the range, by Magrath's map: the working mines; an underground lake (black-pearl clams, and something that dissolves people); a collapsed tunnel with a folding boat in it; a **forgotten goblin temple to Maglubiyet**, where a fight turned up the broken head of a double-headed axe; a cavern of **desecrated ground** around a trapped metal chest holding an **Orb of Dragonkind**; and at last smooth metal — the Antari facility the Titan Fighters use as their mountain base.

### Dirtroit

Past the base the caves open on a cliff over a black chasm. Across it: ruined buildings, windows, dead trees — a city that once stood on the surface and was **buried under the mountains**. Following the great cables down, the party reached intact streets with the lights on and a library where Jak read the name aloud: **[Dirtroit](The-Elemental-Cities.md)**. People sheltered here after the calamity; some never left their beds.

Farther along the chasm it opens wide, and there is the torso of a colossal being sunk in the earth, unconscious, a **horn rising from its head up through the rock**. This is **Kaia**, the [Earth Titan](The-Titans.md). The groaning the party kept hearing in the dark was hers.

### The Black Dragon's lair

East and upward the caves turn wet and begin to stink of rot. Pools of acid. Kobold attendants and the dragon's pets — centipedes, snakes, scorpions. An Antari door sealed between two statues that open it when handed the right flail and the right *gun*, from a cache of rusted weapons. Behind it lairs **Essos** (first written *Ovess*), the Black Dragon — see [The Dragons](The-Dragons.md).

## Lost Rock

*Also "Lost Spire." The locals' name for a rock that is never where it was.*

In the forest on the far side of the Bolt Mountains stood **Nepeta**, a city of tabaxi — [Shhhmeowmeow's](../Campaign/The-Party/Shhhmeowmeow.md) home. A dragon came at night and destroyed it. The survivors tried to rebuild: the rains came, with no trees to hold the soil the hills slid into a swamp, and then the swamp began to **burn the hands** of anyone who worked it. They took it as a sign from their god **Zaltec** to leave, and they live now in an encampment outside the town of **3-Way** (a day and a half from Shimmerhill by wagon).

What actually happened: the "rock" is the **tip of Kaia's horn**, which the Antari used as a **communications tower**. The dragon's attack ruptured Antari **batteries** buried near the surface, and what seeps up is battery acid. It killed the forest, made the swamp, and is slowly eating the tower. The tower **moves** because the Titan underneath is writhing in what little pain she can express.

Today it is a fogged, acid mire under permanent cloud — shambling mounds, a bagiennik, vegepygmies in a ruined house, the stakes and fire pits of Nepeta. The spire is smooth metal with a card-reader door. Inside: dormant automatons, an elevator, a map room showing the world as it was before the calamity with the four lost cities marked, and the **orb sockets** that power each continent ([The Aura Network](The-Aura-Network.md)). Once power returned, the tower began circling the swamp slowly, as if on a track.
