# The Ship's Purpose

The vessel is still running after centuries. It is not a ruin — it is a machine with a purpose that predates recorded history.

## What the players have seen

- The **reactor core** on the lower deck: glass columns of water-cooled machinery, still arcing with power.
- The ship's automated systems actively **classify the mold as a Class 4 Bio-Hazard** and run cleanup: worker robots harvest, compress, and dispose of infected material through the cargo doors. The ship is fighting the infection too — in its own blind, bureaucratic way.
- **Power discs** recovered from the garden; the discs slot into the ship's power distribution.
- The [hyper-dimensional force fields](../../The-Lost-Ship.md) that make the ship a void in Jak's network are a *property of the vessel*, not an accident.

## The true history (DM only)

Hundreds of years ago, an advanced humanoid civilization fled its dying homeworld — a cataclysm of their own making — aboard this self-sustaining ark, carrying passengers, crew, and creatures from their world. During the voyage the ship's computer (**Aphelion**) went haywire; core systems degraded and the robots began refusing orders. As robot mutiny loomed, an outbreak of killer mold of unknown origin swept the decks, killing crew but sparing machines. Commanded by the computer, the robots hunted the survivors until one scientist — **[Nova](../../NPCs/Nova.md)**, now in stasis on the lower deck — shut Aphelion down. The ship crashed in the Barrier Peaks and lay buried for centuries, until a tremor unearthed it a few months before the campaign began.

**Centuries later, the ship's mind has been roused again — not by accident, and not by Aphelion reasserting itself, but by two of the party's own allies working together, on purpose, for their own reasons.**

## What the mold is (DM only)

The russet mold is not a species. It is a symptom.

- **The Black Salient.** A protrusion **from the Far Realm** — the plane of alien, mind-warping entities — a salient in the old military sense, a bulge of somewhere else pushed into our reality, anchored to the [Lighthouse](../../The-Lost-Ship/The-Lighthouse.md). The "root structure of something massive and buried" spilling from the tower's breach is the protrusion itself, or as much of it as can sit on this side. The negative-energy bleed is Far Realm reality leaking into ours.
- **The bleed.** It is bleeding negative energy into reality, and the bleed has to take some shape here. It takes the shape of the russet mold: the spores, the mycelium, the patches, the halo turning over the lantern room are all the same wound made physical. The Lighthouse's "broadcast" is not a transmitter. It is the leak.
- **Nerves growing a will.** As the Salient's influence grows, the infected stop being separate creatures with separate hungers. Herds become scouts; wolves, birds, and things that used to be deer start acting in a coordinated manner toward the same goals. Hive Awareness and Spore Link are the mechanics; this is what they are — the bleed reaching far enough to think. The [Fungal Horror](../../Bestiary/Fungal-Threats.md), statted as a Far Realm entity — literally, not as flavor — is the Salient closest to the surface: something from outside wearing a body of mold.
- **The ark met this thing before.** "Killer mold of unknown origin" that spared machines and killed people is exactly the pattern the Barrier Peaks are living through now. Whether the crew carried the Salient aboard, or it followed the ship, or it is what killed the homeworld, is the first open question below — but the ship's records and the peaks are describing the same wound.
- **George's chemistry is working — and it is angry.** [George's](../../NPCs/George-Decay.md) anti-fungals and spore-blaster do nothing to the Salient itself. What they do is deny it footholds: every stabilized host and every stripped spore coat is ground the bleed cannot hold. [Erleena's](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) cure research beneath the Lighthouse threatens the same thing at scale. The Salient is reacting. The siege at [The Breach](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md) is not strategy; it is a thing lashing out. Its reach into the lake shows at Erleena's portholes: the small infected fauna — leechoids, poison-spined scintillating fish, quipper swarms — batter the glass in relays around the clock. The glass holds. The froghemoth is the one thing in the water that could breach it.

## Open questions to pay off later

- Was the mold cargo, stowaway, weapon, or the reason the homeworld died? (Ties directly to [The Green Age](The-Green-Age.md) "Fallen Star" theory — this may not be the first such ark.)
- **What does the Black Salient want?** Deliberately unanswered. Does a protrusion *want* anything, or is the coordination just pressure finding cracks? Is the anger at George and Erleena the reaction of a mind, or of a wound being cleaned? Don't resolve this until the table forces it.
- **Is Aerun's [First Source](The-First-Source.md) the same kind of thing?** Rajaat, the dark energy the sorcerer-kings draw on, a world drained to the bone — pose it as the question, not the answer. If there is one Salient, there may be others, and the party has now walked between two worlds that each have a hole in them.
- **How Eddie woke (canon):** [George](../../NPCs/George-Decay.md) and [Erleena](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) woke him deliberately, working together, using the ship's dormant computer systems — first for cure research, then in an attempt to re-establish communication across the ship's decks and with the outside world. It has nothing to do with the mold; the timing (soon after Erleena's lab power-up and George's medical-system queries) is simply because that's when they had the access and the need. This entity currently goes by **Eddie** at the table; the name **Aphelion** is a DM-only historical fact belonging to the ship's original mutiny-era computer, not yet revealed or used in play — see the table rule in [Return to the Ship](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md).
- **Why the ship is under siege tonight (canon):** the Lighthouse itself is directing the attack — which is to say, the Black Salient anchored to it is. For centuries its job was to bleed the infection *outward* — the slow bloom radiating into the Barrier Peaks. Now [Erleena's cure research](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) beneath it is making real progress, George's anti-fungals are proving out on the decks above, and that progress threatens it. It has turned the same directing intelligence *inward*: the feral horde besieging the hull at [The Breach](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md), and whatever else answers its signal, is the Salient trying to stop Erleena and George specifically before the cure reaches it.
- **The cycling door beneath Erleena's lab** is an open question — deliberately unattributed. Something overrides the lock, opens it, closes it; she asked the party to make it stay shut, and it hasn't entirely worked. Nobody has an explanation — not Erleena, not Eddie — and this page does not assign one. What is known: the door leads further down, toward Nova's stasis chamber. Separately, the **froghemoth**, once just territorial, is infected and Lighthouse-directed now — an active asset in the Salient's fight, not merely an old hazard blocking the route.
- George and Harah will eventually converge on the same conclusion from opposite directions: *the ship is not just a threat. It is a message. Someone sent it here — to warn, or to destroy?*
