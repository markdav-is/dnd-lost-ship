# Distances and Days

*Copied faithfully from the Caravan Guild's posted rate boards, which are never wrong and never generous.*

## Reckoning your days

The Guild reckons in **tendays**, like everything else on the continent, and its rate boards assume you do too.

- **A walking caravan** makes 20 to 25 miles a day: call it two hundred-odd miles to the tenday. Divide the miles by twenty and add your misfortunes.
- **A loaded argosy** makes 12 to 15 miles a day, a hundred and thirty-odd to the tenday, and stops for nothing smaller than a mountain.
- **A Guild skimmer convoy on the silt** makes sixty miles a day or better, and no one aboard can tell you how.
- The western legs are another matter. Between Urik and Tyr there is a great deal of coast and very little forgiveness.
