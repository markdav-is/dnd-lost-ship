## Rejuvenate — castable now

At cleric 10, Gobbledegook has **5th-level slots** — which means **[Rejuvenate](../../Game-Mechanics/Dark-Sun-5e-Rules.md)** (Athasian added cleric list) is preparable *today*. With him gone, the party's only Rejuvenate-capable caster is out of reach; the restoration half of the Ash-and-Green road ([The Three Roads](../../Adventures/The-Three-Roads.md)) now leans on [Harah](../../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md), who always has it prepared.
