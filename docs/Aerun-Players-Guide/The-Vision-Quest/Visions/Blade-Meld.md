# Blade Meld

*It asks which of your hands is the weapon.*

## The vision

You are holding something. You look down. A knife — bone, chipped, the one you own, or one like it. Your fingers are around the grip. You open them. The knife stays.

You open them wider. You shake your hand. The knife is still in it, and it is not stuck; it is simply that there was never a place where your hand stopped and the knife began, and you had not looked closely before.

Around you is a road. Tablelands, stone, a long straight leg of the Ring Road with no argosy on it. Something is coming up the road, and it is making the stones click.

## The beast

A [dune reaper](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#dune-reaper-warrior). Mantis-shaped, horse-sized, the color of the stone it stands on. Its forelimbs are not holding blades. Its forelimbs *are* blades, scythes of chitin longer than your arm, and it folds them the way you would fold your hands.

It comes close. It looks at the knife in your hand. It looks at the other hand, the empty one.

Then it lifts one of its own limbs and holds it up beside your arm, and the question is perfectly clear without a word in it: *Which one?*

You do not know. The reaper waits. It has nowhere to be.

You lift the empty hand. And the reaper — this is the gift — nods, the way a mantis nods, all head and no neck, and lays the flat of its blade against your open palm, and it is cold, and it does not cut.

*Either one,* it means. *Both. The weapon is where you say it is.*

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your hand is closed around nothing, and it hurts to open, and when you do there is a line across the palm that was not there, pale and straight and already healed.

Blue at the edge of your eye.

## The gift

**Blade Meld.** *As a bonus action, a one-handed weapon you hold cannot leave your hand for one minute: not dropped, not disarmed.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a folded blade, laid flat against your palm.
