# Altaruk

*Fifteen-foot walls, five hundred paid spears, and no interest whatsoever in your business.*

A walled caravan fort on the southern Ring Road, **35 miles east of Balic**, on the way to Amber Valley.

## At a glance

- **Size:** a fort with a town inside it. A few hundred people live here, plus whoever is sheltering that night.
- **Held by:** three merchant houses jointly, House Wavir chief among them. The shared ownership is deliberate: no single family can close the gates against the others.
- **Living:** tolls, stabling, water, and the mercenary contract. The garrison is professional, well paid, and bored, which is the ideal combination.

## What you will see

A blunt square of wall with a single gate, a yard big enough to hold two caravans, cisterns, a smithy, and a drinking hall that never quite closes. Nothing decorative anywhere.

The commander is a preserver of some reputation (a **Gleaner**, which on Aerun is the same thing), and that is unusual enough that people mention it. There is a **G4CE office** inside the gate: two desks, a contract board, and a bunk for anyone carrying the medallion.

## Visitor's notes

- Altaruk keeps an unwritten rule: wizards of the gentler tradition are welcome, and the other kind are turned away at the gate. Nobody puts this in writing. Everyone knows.
- It is the safest bed on the southern road, and priced accordingly.
- Giants come down out of the highland now and then to raid the fields west of here. The garrison handles it. That is the entire reason the garrison exists.
