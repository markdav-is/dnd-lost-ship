# Amber Valley

A caravanserai town on the southern Ring Road east of Balic, in a shallow valley of tan grass that turns gold at evening — hence the name, which is the least sinister on the continent.

## At a glance

- **Size** Small and entirely commercial: yards, wells, warehouses, and inns.
- **Living** Servicing the southern run. Every caravan between Balic and the eastern forest stops here, and Amber Valley takes a modest cut of all of it.
- **Character** Neutral ground by long habit. House factors from rival families drink in the same room and behave, because the alternative is finding another well.

## Traveler's notes

- The best place on the ring to **join a caravan** if you are traveling alone: everything passes through, and hiring is done openly in the yards each dawn.
- News arrives here before anywhere else in the south. So do rumors, in roughly equal quantity.
- The valley's wells are deep and reliable, but the town charges for water by the head — including for animals, including for yours.
