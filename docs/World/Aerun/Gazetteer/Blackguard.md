# Blackguard

A walled waypoint on the north coast between Draj and Raam, where the Ring Road runs close under the cliffs. Grim, useful, and not fond of questions.

## At a glance

- **Size** Small — a few hundred, mostly caravan trade, plus whatever the season washes in.
- **Living** Water, stabling, wheel repair, and beds for the Draj–Raam run. It exists because that stretch is too long to do in one push.
- **The name** Nobody agrees. The polite version is that it was named for the black cliff behind it. The other version is that the town was once cut off by the Guild for a season, and that Blackguard is what was left. Locals will tell you either, depending on how the evening is going.

## Traveler's notes

- Prices are fair and the beds are clean. This is not hospitality; it is a town that knows exactly how badly you need it.
- Blackguard sits between two cities that go to war with each other regularly and takes no side, ever — which is why it is still standing.
- If a Guild caravan is in the yard, eat where its drivers eat.
