# The Counteragent

The cure as combat kit: how the party arms up with Erleena's serum and fights to *heal*. Core design rule: **the cure is loud** — the mold is a network, every inoculation screams through the spore-link, and healing generates the counterattack. Mercy and combat are the same activity.

For the module using these rules, see [Return to the Ship](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md).

## The serum

Brewed by [Erleena](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) (with Nova's archive and, eventually, Aphelion's pharmacopeia) in **limited batches — every mission has a dose budget**. The first dose ever administered goes to [George](../NPCs/George-Decay.md), on-screen. It works.

## Delivery kit (the cure arsenal)

| Weapon | Base | Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Serum darts** (needler pistols ×10, Level 1 armory) | Needler | 15-ft cone, DC 15 DEX to avoid — but infected targets *hit* are dosed (1 dose per burst) |
| **Cure-tipped arrows/bolts** | Any bow | On hit: half damage, target is dosed. Shhhmeowmeow's precision build shines |
| **Dart grenade** | Sleep-grenade chassis | 20-ft radius burst inoculation; every infected creature in the area is dosed (3 doses per grenade) |
| **Aerosol sprayer** | Refilled defoliant tank | 15-ft cone, doses all infected in the cone (2 doses per spray); short range, crowd tool |
| **Syringe lance** | Lance (Musty-Jo) | On a charge hit: normal damage *or* zero damage + auto-dose (rider's choice) |
| **Field syringe** | Melee, any | An action; **automatic against a grappled, restrained, prone, or willing target** — the teamwork meta: pin, then cure |

## Dose resolution (one roll)

When an infected creature is dosed, roll **d20 + stage modifier vs. the serum DC (14)** — the *infection* is making the save; the person inside wants to lose:

| Target stage | Modifier | On infection's failure (the good outcome) |
| :--- | :-: | :--- |
| Latent / Symptomatic (most villagers, livestock) | −2 | **Cleansed instantly**; drops out of the fight, dazed |
| Active (visible growths; most infected wildlife) | +0 | Stunned 1 round, then cleansed |
| Recently transformed (vegepygmy/spore servant, ≤ ~10 days) | +3 | **Reverts to a person** over 1 minute — the campaign's miracle, rationed by the DM via the 10-day window |
| Long transformed / network-grown (fungal horrors, thorny hunters, elder vegepygmies) | immune | Beyond retail reversal — only the Lighthouse retune can reach them. Fight normally |
| Machines (Aphelion's robots) | immune | No biology to cure. **Full-damage, guilt-free combat** |

On the infection's *success*, the dose is spent but the creature is **staggered** (disadvantage on its next attack) and takes a cumulative −2 on its save against the next dose.

## The alarm track (why healing is dangerous)

Every successful cure **pings the network**: +1 heat on the mission's alarm track. At thresholds (suggested 3 / 6 / 10):

1. **Scouts** — infected wildlife converges; spore-screamers start degrading exposed serum (dose budget pressure).
2. **Enforcers** — a heavy responds: fungal horror, mycelial construct, or a robot squad if Aphelion's reach extends here.
3. **The wave** — a full counterattack to retake the site. Hold or withdraw.

Stealthy triage keeps heat low; blitzing a village square is a siege invitation. Player choice, every mission.

## The mold adapts (escalation between missions)

First village is the tutorial; then the network learns. Rotate counters: **armored hosts** (dose needs a called shot at −5 or a pinned target) · **anti-medic units** that focus whoever carries the doses · **hostage tactics** (infected shields around the relay) · **serum-rot spores** (open dose containers spoil in the area — use grenades or lose them).

## Village mission template (three fights in one)

The three quarantined foothill villages — two silent — are the ready-made ladder, and **Sigrid & Toren's village** (the mill survivors, the taken families of [Think of the Children](../Adventures/Think-of-the-Children.md)) is the emotional summit: the kids get their parents back.

1. **Scout** — count the infected, find the **relay bloom** (the local mini-lighthouse the mold raised; the village is not safe while it stands).
2. **Cure-fight** — subdue-and-dose through the streets; alarm track running.
3. **Demolition** — destroy the relay (a straight fight against its wardens; the bloom itself has AC/HP and a spore-burst death throe).
4. **The wave** — survive the counterattack. Then, and only then: the village is *saved* — eyes clearing, families waking, a bell ringing somewhere.

## Why the finale still matters

Retail curing cannot outpace the Lighthouse broadcast — the math is visible to the players (dose budgets vs. an infinite transmitter). The village campaign buys lives and proves the cure; **the source still has to be sung different** ([Return to the Ship](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md), beats 6–7). The handheld doses are retail. The Lighthouse is wholesale.
