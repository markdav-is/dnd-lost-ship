# The Red Dragon Campaign

The previous campaign (table dates October 2023 – August 2025; about two years ago in the fiction) — Andy's game, in which the party — **Wayward, the Friends**, or WTF — switched the Aura network back on, restored the world's power, freed the dragons, took Airlantis, and ended on Eros Station. Everything the current campaign calls "last time" is here.

Source: Andy's Trello board, exported to `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` — 71 cards of world notes and session prep. **This is what Andy prepared, not a record of what the table did.** Where the notes offer the party a choice, the choice taken is mostly not recorded; those are marked *ask the table*. The board stops mid-boss-fight; the ending comes from Andy's later interview ([Timeline](../World/Timeline.md)).

**Throg'Mar**, for the record: chaotic neutral "goodish" half-orc, an orphan from a big city with not the strongest moral compass, a bounty hunter and odd-jobber who loved food, treasure, alcohol and a good fight; fought with a beloved maul named **Shatterskull**; "believed" his zealot's power came from Gruumsh; never fit with orcs or humans.

Andy's three candidate arcs for the campaign, from the first week: *save the world and become heroes · save the world but stay unknown · take many small adventures and become local heroes.*

## 1. The dream and the festival — Yorrem

Every hero has the same dream on the same night: standing in a **red dragon's lair** beside trusted companions of many years — people they have never met. They wake in [Yorrem](../World/Caria.md) on the day of the harvest festival. That evening the [Titan Fighters](../World/Factions/The-Titan-Fighters.md) raid the city and carry off a festival vendor, who throws the party an **"evil eye" pendant**. **Elmor Kimem** of the Gleaners' guard hires them at 100 gp a head to bring the raiders in. At the Academy, **[Professor Erleena Riser](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md)** identifies the pendant as belonging to a city of the Fallen called **the Void**, where a new entrance has just been found.

## 2. Outside the walls — Oldspire, Titans Rest, the Void

Three days to **Oldspire** (passing the **Seventh Son door** that opens only for Jak). In the bar, the Titan Fighter **Zarrth**; in the same town, **Eustace** joins. Out in the Forest of Silent Boughs the party takes the tree fort **Titans Rest** and rescues **Dr. Methuselah Nye**, Erleena's friend, whom the Fighters had been questioning about the eye and the mythical cities. At the new dig, the eye opens a desk, the card in the desk opens the doors, and on the dais beyond the server banks a voice says *"Unknown user recognized… I am Aura… my data banks have not been accessed in 721 years."* **Jak becomes Aura's administrator** ([The Aura Network](../World/The-Aura-Network.md)) and decides what access Erleena gets. Weeks of downtime follow: G4CE contract work, helping Erleena with the Antari language. *(Level 3–4.)*

## 3. Restoring power — Taldea

Aura is failing on her last reserves. Erleena reads enough to place the power source on [Taldea](../World/Taldea.md), at **Lost Rock**. The party sails to **Drytide** (meets **Rico**), walks to **Shimmerhill**, and goes **under the Bolt Mountains**: the goblin temple, the Orb of Dragonkind on desecrated ground, the Titan Fighters' illusory aboleths and their base, the battery room Jak reconnects. Then **Dirtroit**, the sleeping **Earth Titan Kaia**, and the lair of the Black Dragon **Essos** — the dragon that destroyed Shhhmeowmeow's village, who tells them about the **Prophecy of the Red Dragon** ("there is always one more added") and tells Eustace the ball he carries is worth more than gold. Topside, through the acid swamp, into the tower of **Lost Rock**: red, purple, and blue orbs socketed to the right cities, and the world's power comes back on. *(Level 5.)*

Along the way Eustace begins **dreaming of a green dragon** — first a nameless voice fishing for information, later a vision of the dragon dying under machines, its orb taken by human hands, its body carried to a bright room under the sea, its eyes opening red.

## 4. The road home — the race, the pyramid, the Administrator

On the way out of the Lost Rock country Musty-Jo uses a bean from the **Bag of Beans** on a troll and a gleaming **stepped pyramid** erupts on a hillside — trapped first floor, a gynosphinx offering *Strategy, Wisdom, or Strength*, a pharaoh's tomb at the top. Back through Shimmerhill (Esmerelda's offer — *ask the table*). In Drytide the party crews **Rico's** truck in the **Great Race** and ties for second. That night a cloaked man reads Rico's engine notes: **Burl Grives**, who says the engine was his idea, answers nothing, and leaves by *dimension door*. The same night Aura tells Jak the power controls have been switched to automatic by someone with higher permissions than his — **"the Administrator."** Erleena is locked out too. Reports come in worldwide: strange tides, dark fast storm clouds, tremors at Shimmerhill, heat out of the **Amber Valley**. The party rides Essos back to Yorrem. At the Farmers Table, Erleena names the sigil Shhhmeowmeow found as the mark of the **[Antari](../World/The-Antari.md)**; an elf woman listens from another table and slips out.

## 5. Oceana

**Barghests** come for Gobbledegook in Yorrem — hunters of goblin souls for the General of Gehenna, with whom Maglubiyet broke a deal. A week by sea to **Summervalley** (sahuagin, a burned fishing boat, merrow), then north past a gnoll den *(level 6)* and the machine-torn coast, out to the center of the wrong-way currents, and down the elevator with the **Staff of Whirlpool** to **[Oceana](../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md)**. A color-mixing puzzle restores power to the dead domes and frees a trapped man — **Burl Ives**, who shows an Antari badge to an automaton (*"They are with me"*) and walks out through a portal. In the labs the party fights a mechanized **adult green dragon** while Erleena uploads **Undaloth's** soul back into his body. A second, shabbier bean-pyramid — a centaur pharaoh's — yields the **Flail of Tiamat** and an **Enspelled Staff**. Erleena finds the ocean-current controls set to *manual* and shuts them down; she and Undaloth stay in Oceana for his rehabilitation. The party learns the **portal symbols** and steps through to a ruined room in Dirtroit and a locked, overheating room in Eldorado.

## 6. Airlantis

Through the portal to **[Airlantis](../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md)**: abandoned after continual dragon attacks, out of fuel, and falling. Two problems stand between the city and a safe landing — a battery puzzle in the electrical room and a gravity/balance fault. Behind the last doors: the **Silver Dragon Ball**, 200 platinum, and three suits of armor. Upstairs in the weather-research decks the dragon **Claire** has torn a nest into the hull; she knows them as the heroes of the Red Dragon, demands they prove themselves, and attacks. The party wins the city and the dragon. **Rico** comes up, recognizes the engines as cousins of the one he built with Burl, and adapts his fuel — elementals fed on a recipe needing **fossilarium** from Shimmerhill. Land it or keep it flying is left to the party; per the interview they kept it, and Eustace eventually parked it at home. *(Level up, July 2025.)*

## 7. Dildro and Eros Station

Erleena has been reading. The Antari won the peoples of Vermoon over by arguing that the Titans could not have made all life here — that **the Titans lied** — and if that was once common knowledge, the dragons must know it. She asks them. One does not feel as the others do: **Dildro**, the Blue Dragon, who walked away from his Titan — and who is **Burl** ([The Dragons](../World/The-Dragons.md)). He wants off the planet. The party agrees to help; he offers his orb to Gobbledegook and keeps his human shape.

The way out is **Eros Station**: habitation decks to the south with belongings still on the shelves, stasis pods on the west side full of sleeping Antari, labs and journals to the east, and in the north the one person awake — **the Administrator**.

The board's last entry (2025-08-20) is a fight in progress: *Shhhmeowmeow dominated — does what she is told unless it is self-destructive; Jak and Eustace paralyzed; Musty-Jo frightened; eleven toadstools where the fight began.*

## How it ended

From Andy's interview rather than the board: the First Administrator died aboard the station; the Blue Dragon stayed, bound outward; the dragons went back to sleep; and Jak took the empty chair ([Timeline](../World/Timeline.md)).
