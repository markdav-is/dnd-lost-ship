# The Titans

Colossal elemental entities — "the Work" — that powered Vermoon's great cities in the age before the apocalypse. Worshipped as benevolent gods. Possibly something much worse.

Source: Andy's world-building interview, `assets/Vermoon World-Building Session_...transcript.txt`; names, myth, and the enslavement from his 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (cards *Titans*, *Dragon Lore*, *Fire Titan – SAVITA*, *Lost Rock*, *A View of Dirtroit*).

Elsewhere the board settles it: Undaloth was not one of the prime dragons and **had no Titan** ([The Dragons](The-Dragons.md)). Cities on [The Elemental Cities](The-Elemental-Cities.md).

## The myth, as the board tells it

- The **Primordial Titans created Vermoon** — shaped the land, made its inhabitants, and ruled as beneficial gods, "revered for their love of their creations and how well they took care of them."
- They made the **dragons first**, to keep the peace and protect the world, then made more beings to fill it. Keeping the peace was easy — until a ship of the stars arrived.
- **Other gods came with the newcomers.** At first in peace, working alongside the Titans; then more gods, more creatures, and things got out of hand.
- **[The Antari](The-Antari.md) enslaved them** — Savita first, held hostage against the others' retaliation, then each in turn — using science to draw power from them without killing them. The dragons' power was tied to the Titans, and went down with it.

## Kaia and Savita — the two the notes describe

- **Kaia, the Earth Titan.** The party saw her under the Bolt Mountains beyond Dirtroit: a colossal **torso sunk in the earth, unconscious, a great horn rising from her head up through the rock**. The tip of that horn is **Lost Rock** — the Antari used it as a communications tower, and it moves because she writhes ([Taldea](Taldea.md)). Rico's guess at how she was used: tectonic movement for geothermal heat. Her name is the [Titan Fighters'](Factions/The-Titan-Fighters.md) password.
- **Savita, the Fire Titan — "the furnace of Eldorado."** After her capture her flaming body and her **heart of molten lava** kept Eldorado's forges burning night and day. Andy's Eldorado note describes what is down there now as **the body of the now-dead Titan and its still-active core**, spewing fire and lava, with the city built around it — and a later note refers to **"the sacrifice of Savita"** as the turning point that brought the Antari down. How she died, and whether the sacrifice was hers or the Red Dragon's, is not written.

## What they are

- Vast elemental colossi with a **cosmic quality** — so big and so different they are not really relatable; inexplicable, powerful, *"don't get too close"* dangerous. Each takes the character of its element: the Earth Titan golem-esque, stone and shifting earth; the Water Titan aqueous and amorphous; wind and fire likewise. (Exact forms are deliberately open — Andy left size and shape to interpretation, from mountain-sized to "a teeny, tiny, super-powerful elemental.")
- The ancient cities **used the Titans as power sources**, each under a city's control — the engine behind the elemental city-themes ([The Elemental Cities](The-Elemental-Cities.md)). **Each buried elemental city still has its Titan at its heart** — the fire city under Eldorado on Aerun among them.
- The **dragons were meant to protect the Titans** — a duty entangled with the Red Dragon's apocalypse ([The Dragons](The-Dragons.md), [Timeline](Timeline.md)).
- The party has directly seen only the **Earth Titan** (Kaia) in the previous campaign; the others are known through their cities' use of them.

## The central ambiguity — gods or captors?

The people of the old world loved the Titans as benevolent providers. Andy's intended wrench, never fully played out and **deliberately unresolved**:

> *"What if they were actually kind of your captors — and they brought you to this planet, and you were just here because of them, held against your will. And you just didn't know it."*

**Where the idea comes from (2023 board):** it was the **[Antari's](The-Antari.md) argument** — all life on Vermoon could not have been made by the Titans, so *the Titans lied to you* — and it is how they swayed enough of Vermoon's peoples to help bring the Titans down. So the captor theory arrives pre-poisoned: it may be true, and it was certainly useful to the people who said it first. The sleepers on Eros Station still mean to finish the job — their journals plan to **combine the Titans into a super-weapon**. [Erleena](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) dug the argument back up, asked the dragons about it, and found one who believed it.

This is the belief that drove the **Blue Dragon** off-world: *"I don't think I'm supposed to be here."* If true, the peoples of Vermoon are a transplanted population that has forgotten its origin — which resonates uncomfortably with [the ark-ship's own story](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose.md): an Antari **terraforming ark** that reached Vermoon before the refugees did. **Open hook, nothing decided:** the refugees argued the Titans could not have made all life here; a terraformer with a biosphere and a cargo of living things, arriving first, sits right beside that claim. Whether the ark ever did any of its work before it fell is unset.

## Hooks into the current campaign

- **The fire city's Titan, under Eldorado:** **Savita** — a dead Titan with a live molten core, in a city that is a ruin: all is lost down there, and the town above lives off what it can mine out ([The Elemental Cities](The-Elemental-Cities.md)). The Titan the party is likeliest to reach next — because [Shhhmeowmeow's](../Campaign/The-Party/Shhhmeowmeow.md) backstory points there, and [Erleena](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) knows where it is (though she has only ever seen its gate room). **Keep it separate from the infection plot**: the fire city has nothing to do with Rajaat or the Lighthouse. It is a place the party wants to go for its own reasons.
- **Aerun's Titan:** Sandwalker tradition speaks of the Titan ("the Work") slumbering in the deep desert — cause of the devastation, or the last attempt to stop it ([The Green Age](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age.md)). If the ship's energy drain spreads, does it wake — or corrupt — what sleeps there? Whether that deep-desert Titan and the fire city's Titan are the same is open; the fire city is under the rim, not under the silt.
- **The Lighthouse question at Titan scale:** the ship drains the land's living energy the way the cities once drew on the Titans. Harah's *vel-keth* may be the same hunger wearing different machinery.
- **Revelation delivery:** Harah's Primal Rite of Remembrance or Nova's recovering memory can each confirm — or complicate — the captor theory when the campaign is ready to answer it.
