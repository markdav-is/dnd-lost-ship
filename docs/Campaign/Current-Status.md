# Current Status

Snapshot of the campaign state for session prep. **Last updated 2026-10-06, after Return to the Ship session 2 (played 2026-09-24): the ropers are dead, the party has reached George at S42, has the doses and the spore-blasters, has spoken to Erleena on the comm, and has long-rested in George's med bay.**

- The **Council of Frostwatch has happened as played**: Valerius severed her implant, revealed the true spread, and [Harah](../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md) **named Rajaat** — the thousand-year quarantine, the second source, planetary triage.
- **The party chose the Cure path** and set out for the ship **by dogsled convoy on the glacier route behind [Korrin's](../NPCs/Korrin.md) lead sled** (the spore-choked ravines are still impassable — the sledmaster's route avoids them). **Five went**: Shhhmeowmeow, Eustace, Musty-Jo, Beyond-Ce, Robin ([The Party](The-Party.md)). **Harah stayed at Frostwatch**; Gobbledegook is no longer with the party.
- **Played so far:** [The Whiteout](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Whiteout.md), [The Breach](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md), and [The Lab at S42](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Lab-at-S42.md) ([Adventure Log](Adventure-Log.md)). All three S38c ropers are dead, the dance-club androids are on the party's side, and the party is **rested at S42**. Erleena already made her two asks over the comm (the door that keeps opening; the froghemoth at her lab), and the party plans to **wash the froghemoth rather than kill it**. Route down not yet chosen: Eddie offered the drop tube, George the lift. **Next:** [The Awakened Ship](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship.md), Eddie's list of three goals in any order: the frog, the big door, and the antenna. Route: the way down → [Beneath the Lighthouse](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship/Beneath-the-Lighthouse.md) → optionally [The Jammed Override](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship/The-Jammed-Override.md) / [The Antenna](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship/The-Antenna.md) → **[The Send-Off](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Send-Off.md)** (the closing scene: player handouts, *what you know about Aerun*).
- **Conditions carried into next session:** none. Long rest in George's med bay at the end of session 2: full HP and slots, no poison (table ruling: roper poison ends when the tentacle lets go), Beyond-Ce back in her own form. **Doses not yet taken.** George advised waiting until the last minute, and each holds for 24 hours from when it's taken.
- **Erleena is unreachable from outside** (but the party has now spoken to her on the internal comm from George's console) — *"No signal. I'm sorry, your number cannot be completed."* The ship's hyper-dimensional fields filter RF — the same void that blinds Jak's network — so nothing reaches her sealed levels from outside the hull. Inside the hull she is in daily contact with George and Eddie. The party carries her a month of outside news, the council's above all; she asks for it before they can offer.
- **Where the arc lands:** Erleena hears about Rajaat and the defilers from the party, makes her two asks (a baseline sample from the other source; a defiler wizard for George's surgery), says the cure is on Aerun — **and goes herself**, with the party. **George stays** on the ship with Eddie: more anti-fungal, the robots washing the decks, and the Lighthouse kept busy fighting him so it stops spreading outward, *for now* ([George Decay](../NPCs/George-Decay.md)).
- **The second reason to go:** the **fire city under Eldorado** — a buried elemental city Erleena has seen only from its gate room and never dug, and a backstory thread of Shhhmeowmeow's. Unrelated to the infection ([The Elemental Cities](../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md)).
- **Defiler containment is in motion — through Harah, uneasily.** The council wants the [Order of the Sere](../World/Factions/The-Order-of-the-Sere.md) to keep the spread tamped down during the mission. But reaching them means travel, and **they will not talk to Harah**: *"They wouldn't necessarily trust me… It's a long story. Meet me at the bar later."* Erleena's second ask now makes that meeting unavoidable.
- **Robin recruited** — the occupation's infirmary medic ([her page](The-Party/Robin-Wood.md)) — now a full **PC** (new player).
- **The party is withholding the network-corruption theory.** After Copper's warning and the infected-wildlife descent, they deliberately kept the invisible-infection details out of official channels — *"the people we're talking to will have a glowing ear"* — and debated reaching Jak directly with information for him alone.
- **Antitoxin: one dose left.** The whole party was infected at some point and cured by the ship's antitoxin — fast-acting, not long-lasting. The scarcity is felt. George's six stopgap doses (five for the party, one for Erleena) are the bridge.
- On the ground: **one town fully shut down, two quarantined**; the Spásistren are recruiting heavily in Grønnfjord.

## The question on the table

The direction is chosen — now it has to survive contact: get Erleena and her lab off the ship and across the sea before the spread outruns everyone, with [Eddie awake](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md) and the Lighthouse fighting back. The council framing stands as triage, not a vote: seal/purge remain the fallbacks if the cure fails ([The Lighthouse Dilemma](Plot-Threads/The-Lighthouse-Dilemma.md)). How the party gets to Aerun is the next module's problem.
