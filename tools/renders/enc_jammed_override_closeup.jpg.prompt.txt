Above the dial, two small round indicator lamps: the one over LOCAL glowing amber, the one over REMOTE green and dark. Behind the dial, through a gap in the panel, a corroded relay furred with greenish-white crystals. A gloved hand of an adventurer reaches into frame from the bottom edge, thumb and fingers closing on the knob, about to turn it. In the soft-focus background: the skeleton's bony hand resting on the console next to it, and the warm white light of the bay beyond the booth glass.
