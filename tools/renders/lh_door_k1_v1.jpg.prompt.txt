A wide 16:9 painting in exactly the same painted style, colours and lighting as the reference images. Close-up at the base of the same lighthouse, standing on the ground a few paces from its door, looking slightly up.

The base of the tower fills most of the frame: old white concrete almost hidden under thick, pale, root-like mycelium cords that glow softly where they knot, shelf fungi, amber caps, the old metal railing and ladder half-swallowed. Dead centre, the open door: a dark weathered metal door standing ajar, and the doorway behind it glowing a deep warm orange, spore haze spilling out of it at ground level. Thick roots spread across the mossy russet-gold ground toward the viewer. Above, the tower rises out of the top of the frame; the golden spore glow from the lantern room spills down from above. Glittering spores drift in the air. Orange haze rolls low.

No people, no rabbits, no water, no bridge. No text. Hushed, sacred, beautiful, and quietly wrong: the threshold of something.
