# Anticipate

*The thing that is already where you will step.*

## The vision

Forest. Not the gray — the eaves of a wood, at dusk, the light coming sideways through leaves the size of shields. You are walking. You have been walking a long time and the path keeps bending the same way.

Every time you put a foot down, something has just been there. A print in the leaf-litter, still closing. A branch still swinging back. You are not following it. It is ahead of you by exactly one step, and it has been ahead of you by exactly one step since before you noticed.

You stop. The forest stops.

You step left, off the path. The leaf-litter there is already pressed flat.

## The beast

It lets you see it when it decides to. A [kirre](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#kirre): a tiger that is not a tiger, striped in dusk-colors, with eight legs that move like four, and the horns of something that was never a cat. It is sitting where you were going to stand next. It has been sitting there all along.

It does not threaten. It does not need to. It looks at you, and then, slowly, it looks at a place beside it — a patch of ground, empty — and you understand that this is the lesson. Not *where the kirre is*. Where it *isn't*.

You step there. For the first time, nothing was there first.

The kirre yawns, and the forest is gone.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your guide is watching you and has been for a while, and as you open your eyes the guide's hand moves, very slightly, and you see the hand move before it moves.

There is blue at the edge of your eye. You can feel it more than see it, like a cold coin on the lid.

## The gift

**Anticipate.** *As a reaction when a creature you can see acts against you, force it to make a Wisdom save; on a failure it has disadvantage on that action.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: eight legs, sitting where you were about to be.
