# The Ringing Mountains

The great spine of the west coast, running unbroken from the northwestern headlands above Tyr down past Osgaker and Hollowstorm to the volcano beside Urik. Snow on the high crests — the only snow on Aerun — and the reason the western coast is green.

## At a glance

- **Height** Four to six thousand feet, standing taller than the highland country behind them.
- **What they do** The prevailing winds always blow from the west. They strike this wall, rise, and drop everything they carry on the seaward slopes. **That rain is Tyr's valley, Hollowstorm's fisheries, and Urik's plantations.** What crosses the crest arrives hot and empty, which is why the interior is what it is.
- **Wealth** Iron at the northern end above Tyr, obsidian at the southern above Urik, and gold hidden at Walis in between. Three fortunes in one range.
- **The name** For the sound: certain high canyons sing when the wind is right. Travelers hear it before they see anything.

## Traveler's notes

- Passes are few, guarded, and closed by weather more often than the coastal cities like to admit.
- The seaward slopes are farmed in terraces; the landward slopes are bare rock and scree that runs when you stand on it.
- Hejkin and worse things live in the deep tunnels beneath the mining camps. Miners will tell you about it at length and will not go back down alone.
