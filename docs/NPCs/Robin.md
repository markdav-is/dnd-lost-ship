# Robin

The occupation's infirmary medic at Frostwatch — a healer-cleric who arrived "a few days ago with everybody" (the Spásistren deployment), ran the hold's very well-stocked infirmary, and got recruited by the party on the spot. *"You want to save the world?" "Sure, I guess. It's an adventure."*

**Status: party companion** — joining the return expedition to the ship.

## What's known (from the played sessions)

- A **cleric "of sorts"** with real medical skill; the party sought her out for supplies and healing after the descent, and invited her along for the cure mission.
- Came south with the military force but **wears no implant** — no blue light. Her view of the Spásistren is dry and unillusioned: volunteers who "join up and become… robo ninjas. Not me personally." She notes they're **recruiting heavily in Grønnfjord**.
- Asked if she'd been infected: "Not that I know of" — delivered right after the party explained they'd *all* been infected and cured by the ship's antitoxin.
- Well-traveled, vague about her origins ("I'm from… around"); not strong on geography; spends a lot of time outdoors.
- Shared the local picture: one town completely shut down, two more quarantined, everyone nervous.

## DM notes

- **Deliberately underdefined** — the table met her cold and liked her; her background is open design space. Options worth weighing: exactly what she says (a competent, kind medic who wanted an adventure) — the hopepunk read; or a thread to somewhere (the Spásistren vetting her, a Veiled One, or simply the person who keeps the party honest about triage).
- The party recruited her *while actively suspicious of everyone at Frostwatch* — she passed their sniff test when almost nobody else did. That trust is now a stake on the table.
- Mechanically: the party's dedicated healer for the return expedition ([Return to the Ship](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md)) and a natural operator for [the Counteragent kit](../Game-Mechanics/The-Counteragent.md).
