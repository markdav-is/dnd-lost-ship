# The Ship's Purpose

The vessel is still running after more centuries than anyone has counted. It is not a ruin — it is a machine with a purpose, and the purpose was **terraforming**: this is the **first ship of the [Antari](../../World/The-Antari.md)**, down in the Barrier Peaks since long before the refugees, the cities, or the Titan war.

## What the players have seen

- The **reactor core** on the lower deck: glass columns of water-cooled machinery, still arcing with power.
- The ship's automated systems actively **classify the mold as a Class 4 Bio-Hazard** and run cleanup: worker robots harvest, compress, and dispose of infected material through the cargo doors. The ship is fighting the infection too — in its own blind, bureaucratic way.
- **Power discs** recovered from the garden; the discs slot into the ship's power distribution.
- The [hyper-dimensional force fields](../../The-Lost-Ship.md) that make the ship a void in Jak's network are a *property of the vessel*, not an accident.

## The true history (DM only)

**The ship is Antari — and it got here first (canon, 2026-09-21).** [The Antari](../../World/The-Antari.md) — "the ancients" whose ruins, portals, and network the party spent the last campaign digging up — sent **two** ships to Vermoon, a long time apart.

- **The ark came first.** It is a **terraforming ship**: a self-sustaining vessel sent out from the Antari homeworld carrying crew, passengers, a garden biosphere, and a menagerie of creatures from a dozen worlds, to make a world ready. It **crashed in the Barrier Peaks long before anyone else arrived** — how long before is not set, only that it is older than everything else Antari on the planet.
- **The refugee ship came later.** That is the one in Andy's notes: broken-down, barely made it, allowed to land by the Titans and dragons. Its people settled, enslaved the Titans, built Dirtroit, Eldorado, Airlantis, Oceana, and Eros Station, and were buried by the Red Dragon about 720 years ago. By then the ark had already been under the mountain for ages.

What happened aboard is unchanged. During the voyage the ship's computer (**Aphelion**) went haywire; core systems degraded and the robots began refusing orders. As robot mutiny loomed, an outbreak of killer mold of unknown origin swept the decks, killing crew but sparing machines. Commanded by the computer, the robots hunted the survivors until one scientist — **[Nova](../../NPCs/Nova.md)**, now in stasis on the lower deck — shut Aphelion down, **wiping the computer core as she did it**. With no one flying it the ship came down in the peaks and lay buried until a tremor unearthed it about two years ago.

**Centuries later, the ship's mind has been roused again — not by accident, and not by Aphelion reasserting itself, but by two of the party's own allies working together, on purpose, for their own reasons.**

### What follows from it

- **Eddie has never heard of the other ship.** He went dark as the ark fell, before the refugees ever reached Vermoon. The cities, Aura, the portals, Eros Station, the Titan war, the Red Dragon — none of it is in his records, and none of it is a lie when he says so. Ask him about "the Antari on the ground" and he has nothing; as far as he knows, his was the only ship.
- **Eddie's blind spot for Nova (canon, 2026-09-21).** Nova wiped the computer core on her way to the tank, so the mind George and Erleena woke has no record of her — not the shutdown, not the stasis run, not that anyone is still alive down there. **His internal map shows S64b as nothing special: machinery.** He is not hiding her and he is not avoiding her; as far as he can see there is no one to mention. It is also why the name *Aphelion* means nothing to him. How much else went with the wipe is not set.
- **Nova is Antari, and she doesn't know either.** She went into the tank in the same hour. She is the only Antari awake on Vermoon, no part of the Eros Station sleepers' plan, and older than all of them: what she remembers of home is the homeworld *before* whatever sent the refugees running.
- **The "alien technology" is old Antari technology.** The family resemblance was always there — color-coded **[key cards](../../Game-Mechanics/Key-Card-System.md)** and the chipped plastic cards that opened the Void and Lost Rock; **laser pistols** here and in Dirtroit's portal room; Nova's **stasis tank** and the pods on Eros Station; a ship's computer and a world's network ([Aura](../../World/The-Aura-Network.md) — a network with a voice interface, not a mind like Eddie) — but the ark is an **earlier generation** of all of it.
- **Erleena already knows (ruled 2026-09-21).** She is an archaeologist of the Antari and she has lived aboard for months: she made the connection long ago — older script, older design, but plainly the same people. **It seems so obvious to her that it never came up.** She is not hiding it. If pressed she will explain it, a little surprised anyone needed it said. So the reveal is not a twist waiting on Nova; it is a question nobody has asked Erleena yet.
- **Why Aura cannot see it.** The hyper-dimensional fields are a property of the vessel ([The Lost Ship](../../The-Lost-Ship.md)) — and the ark was buried and silent before Aura existed. The ground network was never built to include it.
- **Open hook — the terraformer and the Titans.** The refugees' winning argument was that *the Titans could not have made all life on Vermoon* ([The Titans](../../World/The-Titans.md)). A terraforming ship that arrived first, with a biosphere and a cargo of living things, sits right next to that question. **Nothing is decided here** — not whether the ark ever did any terraforming before it fell, not who made what. It is a hook, not an answer.
- **Also not decided:** how long before the refugees the ark arrived; whether the refugee ship knew the ark had gone ahead (Andy's notes have them setting out for "an unknown destination"); and whether the homeworld was already failing when the ark launched. Nova's returning memory is the lever for any of it.

## What the mold is (DM only)

The russet mold is not a species. It is a symptom.

- **The Black Salient.** A protrusion **from the Black** — in Dark Sun cosmology, the plane of pure darkness that shadows are cast from, the cosmic-horror realm beyond the Gray; and **the place Rajaat was sent**: when the Champions turned on him they sealed him in **the Hollow**, a prison pocket inside the Black, and Borys became the Dragon to keep the lock shut ([Dark Sun](../../World/Dark-Sun.md)). A salient in the old military sense — a bulge of the Black pushed into our reality, anchored to the [Lighthouse](../../The-Lost-Ship/The-Lighthouse.md). The "root structure of something massive and buried" spilling from the tower's breach is the protrusion itself, or as much of it as can sit on this side. The negative-energy bleed is the Black leaking into our world. **On Vermoon it is the same Rajaat.** The Sandwalkers say *"it started with Rajaat"* — the sorcerer. His sending into the Hollow tore a hole from the Black into our world, and the hole never healed: that is Aerun's Salient, and the place carries his name ([The First Source](The-First-Source.md)). The ship's Salient is a second hole; how it was made is one of the open questions below.
- **The bleed.** It is bleeding negative energy into reality, and the bleed has to take some shape here. It takes the shape of the russet mold: the spores, the mycelium, the patches, the halo turning over the lantern room are all the same wound made physical. The Lighthouse's "broadcast" is not a transmitter. It is the leak.
- **Nerves growing a will.** As the Salient's influence grows, the infected stop being separate creatures with separate hungers. Herds become scouts; wolves, birds, and things that used to be deer start acting in a coordinated manner toward the same goals. Hive Awareness and Spore Link are the mechanics; this is what they are — the bleed reaching far enough to think. The [Fungal Horror](../../Bestiary/Fungal-Threats.md), statted as an aberration — literally, not as flavor; it is a thing of the Black — is the Salient closest to the surface: something from outside wearing a body of mold.
- **The ark met this thing before.** "Killer mold of unknown origin" that spared machines and killed people is exactly the pattern the Barrier Peaks are living through now. Whether the crew carried the Salient aboard, or it followed the ship, or it is what killed the homeworld, is the first open question below — but the ship's records and the peaks are describing the same wound.
- **George's chemistry is working — and it is angry.** [George's](../../NPCs/George-Decay.md) anti-fungals and spore-blaster do nothing to the Salient itself. What they do is deny it footholds: every stabilized host and every stripped spore coat is ground the bleed cannot hold. [Erleena's](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) cure research beneath the Lighthouse threatens the same thing at scale. The Salient is reacting — it has become aware of the two of them and turned its effort inward, at the ship. The siege at [The Breach](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md) is not strategy; it is a thing lashing out. Its reach into the lake shows at Erleena's portholes: the small infected fauna — leechoids, poison-spined scintillating fish, quipper swarms — batter the glass in relays around the clock. The glass holds. The froghemoth is the one thing in the water that could breach it.

## Open questions to pay off later

- Was the mold cargo, stowaway, weapon, or the reason the homeworld died? (Andy's notes give the Antari homeworld a large-scale war, which is what the *refugees* fled; whether it had started when the ark launched is open.) (Ties directly to [The Green Age](The-Green-Age.md) "Fallen Star" theory. There were two Antari ships — this ark, and the refugee ship that landed safely — so if a vessel did fall on Aerun, it was neither of them.)
- **What does the Black Salient want?** Deliberately unanswered. Does a protrusion *want* anything, or is the coordination just pressure finding cracks? Is the anger at George and Erleena the reaction of a mind, or of a wound being cleaned? Don't resolve this until the table forces it.
- **Aerun's [First Source](The-First-Source.md) is the same kind of thing (canon, 2026-09-12).** There are **two Black Salients** at work — two places where the Black has pushed through: this one, anchored to the Lighthouse, and Rajaat's, under the Sea of Silt. The ship's mold is **not a copy** of Aerun's and Aerun's is not the original of the ship's — they are two independent bleeds of the same kind of wound, which is exactly why Erleena wants a sample: what the two share is the thing itself. **Aerun's Salient is contained somehow.** The silt ecology on [The First Source](The-First-Source.md) manages its *bleed* (spores, worms, Spice, drought, the Veil); what holds the *Salient itself* in check — whether that is the same machinery, or something the wardens do, or something older — is open and Mark's to decide. At the table Erleena still only has the question; she gets the answer with her own instruments, on Aerun.
- **How Eddie woke (canon):** [George](../../NPCs/George-Decay.md) and [Erleena](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) woke him deliberately, working together, using the ship's dormant computer systems — first for cure research, then in an attempt to re-establish communication across the ship's decks and with the outside world. It has nothing to do with the mold; the timing (soon after Erleena's lab power-up and George's medical-system queries) is simply because that's when they had the access and the need. This entity currently goes by **Eddie** at the table; the name **Aphelion** is a DM-only historical fact belonging to the ship's original mutiny-era computer, not yet revealed or used in play — see the table rule in [Return to the Ship](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md).
- **Why the ship is under siege (canon):** the Lighthouse itself is directing the attack — which is to say, the Black Salient anchored to it is. For centuries its job was to bleed the infection *outward* — the slow bloom radiating into the Barrier Peaks. Now [Erleena's cure research](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) beneath it is making real progress, George's anti-fungals are proving out on the decks above, and that progress threatens it. It has turned the same directing intelligence *inward*: the feral horde besieging the hull at [The Breach](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md), and whatever else answers its signal, is the Salient trying to stop Erleena and George specifically before the cure reaches it. When Erleena leaves for Aerun and George stays to hold the ship, that inward focus is the whole plan: as long as it is fighting George, it isn't spreading — for now.
- **The cycling door beneath Erleena's lab — solved, and it's nothing sinister (canon, 2026-09-12):** the door's **control station is on the lower-deck side, and its override is jammed at LOCAL** — mold in the mechanism, a corroded relay. It keeps re-issuing its last command; Eddie can see the fault and can't clear it from his end. Nobody and nothing is *doing* it. Fixing it is a side quest ([The Jammed Override](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Jammed-Override.md)) — the door is at the southern edge of the lower deck, a DM addition off the published map that the party already knows — and the payoff is what it opens onto: a held route to the deck where Nova's stasis chamber is. Separately, the **froghemoth**, once just territorial, is infected and Lighthouse-directed now — an active asset in the Salient's fight, not merely an old hazard blocking the route.
- George and Harah will eventually converge on the same conclusion from opposite directions: *the ship is not just a threat. It is a message. Someone sent it here — to warn, or to destroy?*
