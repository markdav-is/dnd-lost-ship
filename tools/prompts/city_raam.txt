City map of RAAM — the Silt-Shelf Metropolis.

Atlas context: the northeast, exactly where the plateau's cliff edge meets the ocean — the city is wedged between them. Ocean harbor on the northeast face at sea level; the silt shelf ~1,000 ft above at its southwest back, where the Long Reach lane sets out across the gray (show its first bone channel-markers leaving the quays). The Ring Road passes along the coast below. This is the busiest transfer point on the continent.

The largest and least-governed city on the ring, straddling two levels and built into the cliff between them. Below: deepwater piers, ships, and the Ring Road's Caravan Exchange Yard outside the walls. Above: the Silt Transfer quays on the shelf, skimmers moored on the gray. Between: the Ascent Quarter — switchback freight terraces cut into the rock face, warehouses, porter barracks, toll-houses, and tenement galleries carved into the cliff between the bends, with counterweight hoists (silt-ballast skips, giant-hair rope) working the final ~300-ft band beside zigzag porter-stairs. City plan: the walled Queen's Hill with the Ivory Palace, Royal Barracks, Hall of Conquest, and the Sepulcher of Abalach-Re (the fallen champion's tomb); then the Gleaners' Quarter, Official Quarter, Cons Quarter, Ascetic Row, and the sprawling Low Quarter dissolving into unwalled chaos at the edges — its streets dotted with small shrines to the fallen champion. Mark the Public Armory, Natural Arena, School of the Way, Crematory, and the Office of House M'Ke. ~40,000 inside, as many again in warlord estates outside.
