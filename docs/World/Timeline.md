# Timeline

World history and campaign chronology, oldest first. Dates are relative to **now** (the aftermath of the Council of Frostwatch). Deep-history entries incorporate Andy's world-building interview (canon).

## Deep history

| When | Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| The old age | The **[Titans](The-Titans.md)** — colossal elemental entities — power Vermoon's great cities; the people revere them as benevolent gods. Whether they were guardians or **captors who brought the people to Vermoon** is the world's buried question. The **dragons** stand as the Titans' protectors |
| The landing | The **First Administrator** — an elvish leader with strange summoning abilities — arrives at the head of a group that lands on Vermoon and quietly takes over, ruling through the ancients' networks |
| The apocalypse | The **Red Dragon brings about the apocalyptic event that buries the world's technology** — cities fall, the infrastructure goes dark underground, and the age of laser pistols becomes the age of legends. (How this squares with the dragons' duty to protect the Titans is an open mystery) |
| Ages past | Elsewhere: an advanced civilization on a distant, dying world launches an ark-ship — crew, colonists, creatures from a dozen worlds ([the true history](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose.md)) |
| During the voyage | The ark's computer **Aphelion** goes haywire; robot mutiny looms; a killer mold outbreak sweeps the decks; the scientist **Nova** shuts Aphelion down; the ship crashes in the Barrier Peaks |
| Centuries ago | **Aerun's desertification** — the Green Age ends; the Sandwalkers' ancestors survive by moving ([the theories](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age.md), now shadowed by the Red Dragon apocalypse) |
| Centuries | The ark lies buried; its first victims become the first vegepygmies. The Temple of Nord stands dormant in Grønnfjord |

## The previous campaign (~2 years ago)

- The **Prophecy of the Red Dragon** awakens the dragons and gathers the heroes; each is paired with a dragon ([The Dragons](The-Dragons.md)) and the party collects the dragon orbs.
- The restored power grid brings **[Aura](The-Aura-Network.md)** and the **teleport network** back online; the party ranges worldwide — Dry Tide, the underwater city Ocean, the northern tower — and takes control of the flying city **[Airlantis](The-Elemental-Cities.md)**.
- The arc resolves aboard the **orbital space station**: the elvish **First Administrator** perishes; the **Blue Dragon** — Tarn's aloof string-puller — declares it was never meant to be kept on this world and stays aboard, bound outward. The chaos is quelled; the prophecy fulfilled; the dragons return to hibernation.
- **Jak Bjornsson**, the party's companion, a monk from Grønnfjord, is chosen as the new Administrator — and chooses to rule *openly*, from the ground.
