Executive Summary
-----------------
The party completed their mission to investigate a fungal outbreak source within a ship, returning to Frost Watch to find military forces under a new administration. They learned from Doctor Copper that the infection may spread through a network signal rather than physical spores, with locals acting suspiciously. They plan to report findings to High Sister Valerius and travel to Green Fjord to locate Jack, while withholding sensitive information to avoid alerting potential infiltrators.

Key Themes and Topics
---------------------

The party observed infected wildlife (deer, goats) and mycelial growth across the landscape. Doctor Copper revealed that the infection might not require physical spores but could be propagating via a network signal, with individuals acting differently without visible symptoms.
- Wildlife exhibits unnatural behavior and fungal growth (e.g., antlered mountain goats, motionless elk with white eyes).
  - Copper suspects the infection uses the "aura network" to control minds without physical signs.
  - Military guards at Frost Watch have blue lights in their ears, possibly part of the network.
The ship's infection source was contained but not eliminated; spores continue to spread. Previous attempts to find the source failed, with no returnees.

Frost Watch has been rebuilt with new timber and mortar, but its banners now display the administrator's seal and Temple of Nord symbols. Guards in flowing robes with blue ear lights patrol the area.
- Original Frost Watch symbols (gray wolf on white) replaced by administrator's seal.
  - Guards are calm but unnervingly synchronized; they seem connected via the blue lights.
  - Doctor Copper questions whether the military presence is trustworthy.
The military arrived after the party left, helping rebuild but with unknown motives. Copper has observed villagers acting strangely despite no visible infection.

Important Details and Insights
------------------------------

The infection may not require physical spores but could be transmitted through the aura network, allowing control without visible symptoms. This explains why some locals act oddly without physical signs. Copper's concern is that the network itself is compromised.
This insight shifts the threat from physical spores to a systemic vulnerability in the world's communication network, making containment more complex.

The party has only one dose of antitoxin left, which is fast-acting but not long-lasting. This scarcity forces them to prioritize who receives it and how to handle future infections.

The guards' synchronized behavior and blue ear lights suggest possible network control. The party is cautious about trusting them, especially after Copper's warnings.
