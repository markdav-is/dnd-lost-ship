# Level 2 — The Theater

The ship's entertainment deck, claimed by mind flayers. **Status: explored — the party cleared it in a brutal psychic brawl. [George](../NPCs/George-Decay.md) now lives on this deck, in S42, Medical Storage.**

## What happened

Two **mind flayers** and four **intellect devourers** had taken the theater and were using the ship's technology to study the conquest of other worlds. In the fight, [Musty-Jo](../Campaign/The-Party/Musty-Jo-Mustang.md) was grappled, stunned, and dominated multiple times; [Beyond-Ce](../Campaign/The-Party/Beyond-Ce.md) took heavy psychic damage and was briefly turned against the party before breaking free. The last mind flayer dropped on stage.

## Now — the observation deck with Eddie online

The deck is a ring of rooms around an octagonal overlook: the S34 viewers line the inner rail, with the garden 80 ft below, and four **S31 drop tubes** at the compass points — north, west, south, east — connect up to Level 1. The **Lift** squares beside the S33 cargo holds are cargo lifts *down* to the garden, not up — each hold's lift is a 20-ft cargo platform that Eddie or a violet card lowers to the spot marked on Map 7.3: **S33a (south) → S48 feed storage · S33b (east) → S50 crew room · S33c (north-west, one door from George's S42) → S52 garden maintenance, beside the north tube's garden landing · S33d (west) → S54 climate control.** The S33c platform is the practical way to move a centaur and cargo down — no anti-grav. Since Eddie came online the **run from the north drop tube is lit** — S31 → past the S32 robot station → the yellow door of **S42, Medical Storage: [George's](../NPCs/George-Decay.md) lab**, two doors west. The tempting shortcut is the **south** tube, right beside the S1 airlock on Level 1: it lands at the theater (S35), and from there it's half the ring to George either way — the dance club and cocktail lounge going west, the lounge robots, gym, and cargo holds going east. Eddie doesn't light it. Everything else on the ring is dark and exactly as the module left it. Lit means Eddie is looking; dark means he isn't.

**Same deck as the "Upper Walkway."** The wiki's [Level 3 — Upper Walkway](Level-3-Upper-Walkway.md) is this deck's inner rail (S34) and lounge entrances; its dining servo is the S37 lounge robots by another name. The party ran that gauntlet during the survey only because they came in through the theater and needed the servo's violet cards to go down. On the return, as played, the party took the **south** tube to the theater and walked the ring toward S42, killing a roper among the S38 growths.

- The theater (S35) still holds the mind flayers' apparatus on the stage — the loose end already noted in DM notes below — and the S36a east kitchen's service windows open straight onto it.

## Wandering encounters

Based on the book's **Random Spaceship Encounters** table (QftIS p.164), which covers the whole ship except the garden. The book's rate: once per hour aboard, roll a d12, and an encounter happens on a 1. The book table is split here by [ship state](../The-Lost-Ship.md). **Ship-off** is the start state (Eddie asleep: dark cosmic horror), and on the return it still covers the dark side of the ring. **Ship-on** is the return with Eddie awake (solarpunk *Space: 1999*): the same robots and androids, now on the party's side.

## DM notes

- The drop tubes from this level require a **violet key card** to descend — the gating that pushed the party through the walkway level to find one — Eddie can override the violet gating now, at least on the routes he's lighting.
- The mind flayers' research is a loose end: what did they learn about the ship, the mold, or other worlds? Their notes/apparatus could hold a revelation if the party returns. Were they also drawn by the void — the one place *their* enemies couldn't scry either?
- This level is otherwise thinly detailed in the conversion documents; the original S3 Level II material (and its 'tween-decks service areas) is available in `assets/tsr09033 - S3 - Expedition to the Barrier Peaks (1).pdf` for expansion.
