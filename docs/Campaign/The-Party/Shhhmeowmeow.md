# Shhhmeowmeow

Tabaxi Ranger 9 (Outlander). Player: Shhhmeowmeow. Sheet: `assets/Shhhmeowmeow_109963368.pdf`. Also known as **Shenandoah** — the name from the previous campaign's dragon arc.

- **Saves:** STR +7, DEX +8 proficient. **Passive Perception 16**, Darkvision 90 ft.
- **Key skills:** Nature +9, Stealth +8, Athletics +7, Insight/Perception/Survival +6, Investigation +5.
- **Defenses:** resistant to damage dealt by traps; advantage vs. magically created/manipulated plants (very relevant in the garden).

## Combat

- **Dread Ambusher:** +WIS to initiative, +10 ft speed and an extra weapon attack (+1d8) on the first turn.
- **Favored Foe** (4/long rest), **Feline Agility** (double speed 1/rest).
- Attacks: Arrow of Electric Sting +1 (+11, 1d6+7 +2d6 lightning), Longbow (+10), Scimitar (+8, nick), **Laser Pistol** (+6, 3d8+4 radiant), Primal Savagery (+6, 2d10 acid), Magic Stone.

## The dragon bond

As Shenandoah, Shhhmeowmeow hails from the **northern continent** — the land of the tower journey where the party found the **[Black Dragon](../../World/The-Dragons.md)**, their paired dragon of the Red Dragon prophecy. The Black Dragon now sleeps with the rest; whether the old bond can rouse it if the infection becomes a world-threat is one of the campaign's held cards.

## DM hooks

- The party's scout and trap-finder; the plant-magic advantage makes them the natural point character for garden and mold terrain.
- Outlander background pairs well with the Frostwind Crossing and Barrier Peaks survival play.
- Homeland hook: the northern continent — dragon tower country — is unexplored territory in the current arc.
