# The First Source — Rajaat

The most closely guarded secret on Vermoon: the infection pouring from the Barrier Peaks ship **has happened before**. The Sandwalkers call the original outbreak — and the nameless place that holds it — **Rajaat**. *"The place where it began. The place where it has always been."*

Sources: `assets/Aerun_TheDesertContinent.md`, `assets/Return_to_Frostwatch_v3.md` + table canon (2026-08-03). The Rajaat story is now **council-known**; the containment mechanism and the Spice remain secret.

## The established history

1. **Rajaat was discovered on Aerun during the Green Age** — an ancient, catastrophic origin point of a fungal/spore infection identical to the ship's russet mold. It spread the way the Scarlands outbreak is spreading now: *slowly, then all at once.* The animals changed. The trees changed. The people changed.
2. **Containment was tried first — and failed.** Walls, burned borders, posted guards. The walls held **eleven years**; then the bloom came from *beneath* them. The spores had been in the groundwater the whole time.
3. **Then they unmade the land. On purpose.** *"They killed the land to save the world."* **Defiler magic** destroyed the spores directly (they are plant life) and every green thing with them — no water, no organic matter, no host for the bloom; the continent's heart reduced to the silt itself. The infected creatures survived the casting and starved. Defiling was shunned from Vermoon afterward; the [Order of the Sere](../../World/Factions/The-Order-of-the-Sere.md) keeps it as a failsafe. *(Harah tells the silt-was-made story in open council; the defiler method and the Order she reveals only if pressed or in private.)*
4. **The quarantine has held for a thousand years.** It is the only containment ever found that works.
5. **Rajaat was never destroyed.** It remains beneath the Sea of Silt — and the deep mechanism of its ongoing management is a secret Harah guards.

## The containment (the deeper secret)

What actually keeps Rajaat in check is the Sea of Silt's engineered ecology — plus one thing no ecology provides:

1. **Spawn** — spores rise from the First Source.
2. **Mass** — with nothing organic to attach to in the defiler-killed waste, they gather into dense masses.
3. **Bloom** — the mass eventually explodes into a violent fungal bloom.
4. **Consumption** — the dead spores are eaten by the apex predators of the Sea of Silt: **the great worms**.
5. **The Veil** — see below.
6. **The Drought** — the dryness itself. Water is the breach vector (the Green Age walls fell to spores in the groundwater), so the working unmade the basin's entire water cycle: below sea level, endorheic, rain-suppressing haze dome overhead, moisture-swallowing silt below, and **worm larvae that sequester** any water that penetrates the deeps. Rare rain-years are **bloom years** — the cycle surges, the worms feast, and the coast sees only a bumper Spice harvest. **Wetting the interior at scale — a weather-working, an irrigation scheme, a cracked aquifer, a well-meant Rejuvenate campaign, a breached portage sill — is not terraforming. It is a containment breach.** The wardens' driest law, and the one the campaign will someday test.

The defiling *created the conditions*; the worms *close the loop*; the Veil makes the whole machine **unfindable**. At the council Harah went as far as she ever has: Rajaat is *"contained only by sand and worms and the prayers of people the coastal cities have never met."* **The prayers are not metaphor** — see below. Her people are its **wardens** — a duty they did not choose.

## The Veil (the psionic concealment)

The deep places of the Sea of Silt are **hidden from minds**. The keepers of the Spice-lifecycle secret — the [Sandwalkers'](../../World/Factions/The-Sandwalkers.md) warden-orders — maintain a massed, generations-deep psionic working that veils the interior deeps: not an illusion painted over the silt, but an edit to *perception itself*. A creature can fly **directly over the First Source** and see only empty gray; the knowledge slides off the mind before it forms. Maps drawn over the deeps come back subtly wrong. Expeditions cross the veiled zone and remember crossing nothing.

**Why it works — the campaign's psionic metaphysics** ([conversion rules](../../World/Dark-Sun.md)):

- Psionics on Vermoon behave like **prescience in Dune** in one crucial way: **other psionic minds muddy it**. Sight contests sight; disciplined minds blur what any single mind can perceive; massed disciplined minds can blind a region outright. The Veil is this principle at its ultimate scale — the largest sustained psionic working in the world, humming beneath a thousand years of prayer.
- But *unlike* prescience, psionics are **not internal** — not a private inward sight. The Will is a **mental tool used on others**: reading, veiling, thrusting, commanding. The Veil is not the wardens seeing differently; it is the wardens *reaching into every mind that approaches* and gently taking the Source out of it.

**Consequences worth playing:** a thousand years of Guild crossings, house prospectors, and even the dragons' patrols have found nothing — and never noticed the not-finding. The Veil is also the deepest answer to why the [Aura network](../../World/The-Aura-Network.md) ends at the Rim Wall mattering so little to the wardens: machines can be walled out, but the Veil handles *minds*. And it cuts both ways — a sufficiently powerful psionic (a dragon that grew suspicious; [a certain blue-eyed student](Spicy-Jak.md), someday) represents the first genuine threat to the concealment in centuries.

## The Spice (never disclosed)

The byproduct of worms digesting dead spores is the **Spice**:

| Facet | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| Economy | The most valuable resource on Aerun — the wealth behind the [merchant families'](../../World/Factions/The-Merchant-Houses.md) alliance and the Guild's crews. **An Aerun-only phenomenon: the southern empire has never tasted it** |
| Effect | Enables and amplifies **psionic abilities** (the "Will and the Way") — and extends life. Ambient exposure gives nearly everyone on Aerun a minor wild talent; [any PC who partakes unlocks the same](../../World/Dark-Sun.md) |
| The mark | Heavy sustained use stains sclera and iris a deep, uniform **blue** |
| The lie | Aerun believes Spice is a natural mineral or botanical resource. That it is processed fungal apocalypse filtered through giant worms is the tribes' deepest secret |

## Why the Barrier Peaks changes everything

The ship is a **second Rajaat** — sitting in a valley full of trees, animals, rivers, and people. Spores never need to mass (life everywhere to attach to); there are no worms to close the loop — and **no veil**: the second source sits naked in every mind and every network that looks at it. *"Your silt does not exist yet. And creating it large enough would take generations and kill everything between here and the sea."*

## The new stake — the cure frees Aerun too

The v3 council canon raises the ceiling: a true cure from the ship's medical levels **might work on Rajaat itself**. Not a seal, not a fire — an *end*, for both sources. For the Sandwalkers it would mean release from a thousand-year wardenship: *"We did not choose that. A cure would give us a choice for the first time."* This makes the [cure path](The-Lighthouse-Dilemma.md) a world-stakes proposition — and gives Harah a personal reason to see the party succeed.

## DM levers

- **The defiler option:** the Order of the Sere can kill the spores directly at the cost of all Scarlands plant life — with the starving-fauna aftermath. The proven, unbearable fourth path.
- **Harah's layered secrecy, revised:** the Rajaat story is public; the defiler method and the Order surface under pressure; the worm ecology is half-named; **the Spice and the Veil are never spoken**. Five rings of disclosure, each with a price.
- **The Spice revelation:** if the coastal world — or the southern empire — learns what the Spice really is, the political map redraws overnight.
- **The Veil under strain:** every warden the Silence pulls south is a mind withdrawn from the working. If the second source keeps drawing them, the Veil thins — and the first prospector to *notice the deeps exist* starts a countdown nobody can stop.
- **Rajaat itself:** never destroyed — only contained. Is it a *third* crashed ark? The Titan's wound? Whatever the answer, the precedent stands: sources are not destroyed. They are *managed*. The cure path is the first challenge to that rule in a thousand years.
