# The Crescent Forest

![The forest country](../../.attachments/darksun_ivory_triangle_4e.jpg)

A long bowed arc of true forest on the eastern coast, curving between the highland cliffs and the sea. The last great wood on Aerun, and the most valuable ground on the continent that isn't a city.

## At a glance

- **The shape** It curves because the highland's edge curves — the forest fills the corridor between the cliffs and the ocean, which is where the name comes from.
- **The trees** **Agafari**: iron-hard timber that takes an edge like bronze and is worth its weight in trade goods. Nibenay logs it; Gulg lives in it; the two have quarreled over it for generations, and every so often the quarrel becomes a war.
- **Who lives here** Gulg and its dagadas, the halflings of Ogo, hunters, hermits, and a loose circle of druids who take a dim view of the logging and act on that view.

## Traveler's notes

- The Ring Road runs along the forest's eastern edge and does not enter it. Roads inside the wood are local, unmarked, and belong to somebody.
- Travelers report a stretch of deep forest that feels *wrong* — watching undergrowth, an air of malice — and give it a wide berth. Locals call it the Witchgrove and will not guide you there for money.
- Take a forest guide from Gulg or Ogo. Woodcraft learned anywhere else on Aerun is not woodcraft here.
