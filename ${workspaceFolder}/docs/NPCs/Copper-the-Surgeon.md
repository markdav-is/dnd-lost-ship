# Copper the Surgeon

Expedition medic at Frostwatch — grim, paranoid, and haunted; a battlefield surgeon whose experiences with infection have scarred mind and soul. *"The rot hides in blood. Fire cleanses. If you're hiding something, I'll find out — and I won't hesitate."*

(Early drafts of the Frostwatch module call him "Dr. Veyra" — same character.)

## Personality

Perpetually on edge; trusts no one, including himself. Anger as a shield. Obsessed with purging infection at any cost — fire and isolation are his cures, and he will propose burning supplies, animals, or infected companions. A skilled healer whose hard choices can save the expedition or doom it.

## The theory that matters

After the party's return, Copper delivered his warning in the granary's shadow: some locals are *different* — not physically infected, but making decisions they wouldn't have made, **protecting the infection without knowing it**. His fear: the infection can ride a *signal*, and the Spásistren's network may already be carrying it. *"I'm probably wrong. I'm usually wrong about the worst things. But I'm right about the worst things often enough that you should listen."*

**Copper is not wrong** — or at least, the campaign must never prove he is. See [The Invisible Infection](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Invisible-Infection).

## At the council

Voted for the purge with two words: *"Do it."*

## Infection symptom table (his diagnostic list, d6)

1. Coughing fits, orange fungus in saliva · 2. Chills, patchy discoloration · 3. Whispering-voice hallucinations · 4. Itching, small growths on arms/neck · 5. Extreme fatigue, poor concentration · 6. Violent aggression toward companions

## DM levers

- Fear checks (WIS DC 15) when witnessing mutation are his constant refrain — he *narrates* the horror.
- If an infected PC hides symptoms, Copper finds out. What he does next is a scene, not a die roll.
- His paranoia can be the campaign's canary — or its first tragedy if he purges someone wrongly.
