# Telekinetic Strike

*You stand in front of it and learn where a wall is weakest.*

## The vision

A waystation wall. Packed earth faced with stone, thick as a man is tall, the kind every Guild stop on the Ring Road has and nobody asks why. You are standing with your back to it. Your hands are flat against it. It is warm from the day.

Something is coming across the flat toward the wall, and you are between them.

You can feel it through your palms before you can see it. A tremor. A rhythm. The wall is *listening* to it, and the wall is afraid.

## The beast

A [rampager](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#rampager). The living siege engine — a thing the size of a house, all shoulder and skull and forward motion, the reason waystop walls are as thick as they are. It does not roar. It does not need to announce itself. It comes on at a walk that covers ground like a run and it is looking, not at you, but at the wall behind you.

It stops a stride away. The ground is still ringing.

It lowers its head, and you think: *this is the end*, and it is not. It is looking *past* you. And because you are standing where you are, with your palms on the stone, you follow its look — back through yourself, into the wall — and you find what it is looking at.

A place. A hand's breadth of stone like any other. But *under* it, where the packing was done by someone tired on a hot day, the earth is not quite as earth as the rest. A seam. A lie the wall tells. The rampager has never seen this wall before and it found the seam from a stride away, the way you would find a face in a crowd.

It does not strike. It shows you the seam until you can see it with your eyes closed. Then it turns, enormously, and walks away across the flat, and the wall under your palms stops being afraid.

Every wall has one. Every thing that stands up has one. You will look for it now without meaning to.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your palms are sore, as if you had been pressing them against something hard all night.

Blue at the edge of your eye. You look at your guide and — without wanting to — you see where the guide would break.

## The gift

**Telekinetic Strike.** *As an action, one creature you can see within 60 feet must make a Constitution save or take 1d4 force damage. The damage grows as you do.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a skull lowering, looking past you at the seam.
