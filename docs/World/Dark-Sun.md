# Dark Sun (source setting)

The reference shelf for everything [Aerun](Aerun.md) borrows — and it borrows **heavily**. Dark Sun (TSR's Athas) supplies the continent's bones: the city-states, the merchant houses, defiling and preserving, psionics everywhere, the silt, the arenas, the dying-world ecology. This section indexes the source library and states the campaign's official conversion rules, so future tie-ins pull from one place.

- **[Glossary](Dark-Sun/Glossary.md)** — the triad, the merchant families, the dragons, the Caravan Guild, and the politics that make the world run.

## Campaign conversion rules (canon)

1. **Aerun is Athas-through-Vermoon.** Borrow names, factions, monsters, and politics freely from any Dark Sun source; where a source conflicts with played canon, the campaign wins. The rules chassis is the [Dark Sun 5e conversion](../Game-Mechanics/Dark-Sun-5e-Rules.md) (AthasCon Players Guide + Campaign Guide + Terrors of the Desert).
2. **The Dune triad, not a room full of kings.** To keep the politics table-simple, power on Aerun is three-cornered: **Jak is the Emperor** (the world's one king, in the south — the Administrator; Aerun is his wildest, least-governed continent, left alone because it is **the trading hub of the world**) · **the seven merchant families rule the seven cities** (the Landsraad) · **the Caravan Guild** holds all bulk transport (the Spacing Guild — mekillot argosies and silt-crawlers, neutral, everyone or no one). The old god-kings **kept their names and lost their thrones**: each is now a **psionic dragon** leading one family's armies — Hamanu for Stel, the Shadow King for Shom, the Oba for Inika; the rest dead, broken, or banished ([Glossary](Dark-Sun/Glossary.md)).
3. **Psionics come from the Spice.** On Athas, everyone is psionic by nature. On Vermoon, psionics are the [Spice's](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md) gift: Aerun-born roll a **wild talent** (Players Guide table, p. 112) from lifelong ambient exposure — and **any player character who partakes of the Spice unlocks the same door**: roll a wild talent on first sustained use; heavier use grows the gift (and the blue-within-blue stain, and the dependency). *The Will and the Way* is the deep reference for what trained minds can do; [Spicy Jak](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/Spicy-Jak.md) is the arc that takes this all the way. **How psionics behave (canon):** unlike Dune's prescience, the Will is not an inward sight — it is a **mental tool used on others**: reading, veiling, thrusting, commanding. Like prescience, though, it is **muddied by other psionic minds** — sight contests sight (opposed psionic checks when talents clash), and massed disciplined minds can blind whole regions. The proof is **the Veil**: the wardens' generations-deep working that hides the deep silt — and the First Source beneath it — from every mind that approaches, even one flying directly over it ([The First Source](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md)).
4. **The Sea of Silt replaces the Deep Desert.** Aerun's interior is not sand — it is **silt**: a continent-heart ocean of powder-fine gray dust that flows like water, drowns like water, and carries nothing heavier than a silt-crawler. The Green Age defiling didn't leave a desert; it left *this* — land unmade to its mineral ghost. The [First Source](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md) lies beneath the silt; the **great worms swim it**; the [Sandwalkers](Factions/The-Sandwalkers.md) read its currents and walk its shoals. Everything the wiki previously called the Deep Desert is the Sea of Silt. The approved **Aerun Atlas** (`assets/Aerun_Atlas.png`) now depicts this correctly.

## How the borrowing maps

| Dark Sun / Dune | This campaign |
| :--- | :--- |
| Athas, the dying world | **Aerun** — one continent's worth of the same story, as Vermoon's warning |
| Rajaat, the First Sorcerer (inventor of defiling) | **Rajaat, the First Source** — the name migrated from the man to the thing his art was used against; the weapon and the disease share a name |
| The Cleansing Wars' scoured wastes | **The Green Age defiling** — the working that unmade the interior into the Sea of Silt |
| The Emperor (Dune) | **Jak** — the one king, far to the north; his temples are embassies |
| The Landsraad / the merchant dynasties | **[The seven families](Factions/The-Merchant-Houses.md)** — Wavir, Tsalaxa, Inika, Shom, M'ke, Vordon, Stel — **ruling their cities outright** |
| The Spacing Guild | **The Caravan Guild** — mekillot argosies and silt-crawlers; all transport, perfect neutrality |
| The sorcerer-kings | **The dragons** — names kept, thrones gone: psionic army-leaders bonded to the families |
| The Veiled Alliance | The same, quietly working toward Aerun's restoration |
| Universal psionics | **The Spice** — psionics as ecology, not birthright |
| The Dragon / the Avangion metamorphoses | The ascension roads behind the dragons themselves and [Spicy Jak's](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/Spicy-Jak.md) third path, the Psionic Avatar |
| Silt horrors, thri-kreen, muls, elven runners | Imported wholesale — see Terrors of the Desert for stat blocks |
