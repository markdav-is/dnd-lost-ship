Eye-level view wading into the south-east corner of the inner garden, where burst pipes have flooded the ground into a swamp: knee-deep green scummy water, gnarled trees sinking into the morass, vines drooping from low branches, bright sunlight coming down in long beams through the canopy gaps and glittering on the water. A rusted, broken ship pipe juts out of the bank, still trickling. Off to the left through the trees, the edge of the open lake and, far across it, the small wooded island with the same slender white lighthouse as the reference, lower half wrapped in russet growth, in the orange haze. The russet spore haze lies low on the water. Near a sunken log, a long flat dark shape lies just under the scummy surface, only a faint wake and two tiny eyestalks breaking the water — barely noticeable, a giant leech waiting. The long, dark, unknown way round.
