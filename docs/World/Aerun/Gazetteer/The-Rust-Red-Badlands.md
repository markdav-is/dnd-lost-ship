# The Rust-Red Badlands

Broken mesa country on the east of the continent, stepping down from the highland's shoulder toward the Crescent Forest and the sea. Red rock, deep gullies, flat-topped buttes, and very little water.

## At a glance

- **The look** Iron-stained stone in every shade from ochre to blood, cut into canyons and standing mesas. Striking at dawn, punishing by noon.
- **Where** Behind and above Nibenay, filling the gap between the eastern forest and the cliffs of the interior.
- **Travel** The Ring Road skirts the Badlands' foot rather than crossing them, and this is not cowardice: the canyon country is a maze, water is scarce, and the mesas are climbable only in places that must be known in advance.

## Traveler's notes

- Nibenese quarrymen work the near edges for building stone; beyond their camps, the country belongs to raiders, herders, and whatever lives in the gullies.
- Flash floods are real here despite the drought. A dry canyon can run chest-deep for an hour after rain nobody in the lowlands saw fall.
- Guides out of Nibenay are good and expensive. Guides who approach you in a tavern offering a shortcut through the Badlands are neither.
