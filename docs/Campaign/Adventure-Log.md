# Adventure Log

What has actually happened in play, in order. Each chapter links to the runnable module in [Adventures](../Adventures.md) where one exists.

## Chapter 1 — The Summons

A year after Wayward, the Friends' victory over the First Administrator, each PC received a summons by **dis-trans bat**: Jak's voice sealed in a glass tube. They arrived in a transformed [Grønnfjord](../Locations/Gronnfjord.md) — the Temple of Nord awakened, Spásistren patrolling, the harbor tense beneath the prosperity. In the colossal black-stone throne room, [Jak Bjornsson](../NPCs/Jak-Bjornsson.md) revealed **the void**: a place in the southern Barrier Peaks that his world-spanning Aura network cannot see at all, from which unnatural creatures emerge. He begged his old friends to be his eyes inside it. *(Module: [Chapter 1 — The Summons](../Adventures/Chapter-1-The-Summons.md))*

## Chapter 2 — Grønnfjord

The party outfitted in the harbor district (Bjorn's Blades & Bows, Sigrid's Traveling Goods, the Harbor Provisioner), gathered rumors at the **Salty Serpent Inn**, and learned of missing expeditions, metal-fleshed monsters, ships hauling material to **Nordkrist's Hold**, and children coming back "different" from temple ceremonies. *(Module: [Chapter 2 — Grønnfjord Shops](../Adventures/Chapter-2-Gronnfjord-Shops.md))*

## The Voyage South — Foggy Fjord

The party's ship lost its rudder and beached on a cold fjord below cliffs riddled with caves. Two days of dread: strange humming, oversized claw marks, frozen rain. *(Module: [Foggy Fjord](../Adventures/Foggy-Fjord.md))*

## Think of the Children!

On day two, siblings **Sigrid and Toren** arrived by dogsled in pot-and-pan armor, warning that their village had been "overrun by elves" — actually spore servants. The camp was attacked by the horde, then a spiked ice ball crashed down the slope and split open to reveal the **Mycelial Frost Giant Skeleton**. The party survived; among the treasure were six axe beaks. *(Module: [Think of the Children](../Adventures/Think-of-the-Children.md))*

## Frostwatch Horror

The party crossed the **Frostwind Crossing** by dogsled with [Korrin](../NPCs/Korrin.md) and reached [Frostwatch Hold](../Locations/Frostwatch-Hold.md), meeting expedition leader [McReady](../NPCs/McReady.md) and the paranoid medic [Copper](../NPCs/Copper-the-Surgeon.md). That night two **Fungal Horrors** and infected wolves burst through the walls. The party survived the siege — killing Korrin's infected sled dogs was one of the hardest moments — and pressed south to the ship the next morning. *(Module: [Frostwatch Horror](../Adventures/Frostwatch-Horror.md))*

## The Ship — Level 1

The party entered the exposed airlock in the hillside, learned the [key card system](../Game-Mechanics/Key-Card-System.md), fought webbirds, intellect devourers, and the malfunctioning **surgeon android** — interrupted by the theatrical arrival of [George Decay](../NPCs/George-Decay.md) ("MED-2, authorization code Decay-Gamma-Seven! Stand down!"). George, infected but lucid, joined as guide and revealed that **Erleena Riser** was also aboard, missing in a containment suit. *(Reference: [Level 1](../The-Lost-Ship/Level-1-Crew-Quarters.md))*

## The Ship — Level 2: The Theater

In the ship's theater the party defeated **two mind flayers and four intellect devourers** who were using the ship's technology to study the conquest of other worlds. **Musty-Jo** was grappled and dominated; **Beyond-Ce** took heavy psychic damage and was briefly turned against the party. Loot included the red key card, sleep grenades, and a laser pistol. *(Reference: [Level 2](../The-Lost-Ship/Level-2-The-Theater.md))*

## The Ship — The Garden Level

George briefed the party in the crew room (S50), then stopped at the garden threshold — the spore air would kill him. The party crossed the outer jungle, the fungal inner garden, and the black central lake, fought past the **Froghemoth** (driven off, not killed — it still rules the lake), survived the **wolf-in-sheep's-clothing** on the bunny path, and entered the mycelium-consumed **[Lighthouse](../The-Lost-Ship/The-Lighthouse.md)** — finding **Erleena Riser alive**, straining at the floor hatch in her intact containment suit. In the sealed levels beneath the Lighthouse she declared her intent to build her cure lab, and asked the party to secure the cycling door further down. *(Reference: [The Garden Level](../The-Lost-Ship/The-Garden-Level.md))*

## The Ship — The Lower Deck

Beneath the lake: the **reactor core** (glass columns of arcing water-cooled machinery), decontamination corridors with wheely sleds, cargo holds of compressed mold cubes. The party solved the control booth puzzle, initiated the **manual purge cycle**, fought the bipedal security bots that tried to throw them onto the trash pile, and were **flushed out of the 40-foot cargo doors** into the snow — alive, and outside. *(Reference: [The Lower Deck](../The-Lost-Ship/The-Lower-Deck.md))*

## Return to Frostwatch (two sessions, played 2026-08-04)

**Session 1 — the descent and the occupied hold.** The journey down showed the infection loose in the world: fungal-antlered goats, motionless white-eyed elk, spore-choked ravines that closed the ground routes. Frostwatch was repaired but re-flagged — Spásistren everywhere, blue lights behind ears. Copper delivered his **invisible-infection warning** in the granary's shadow, and the party took it fully to heart: they resolved to **withhold the network-corruption theory from the council** (*"the people we're talking to will have a glowing ear"*) and debated how to reach Jak directly. At the infirmary they met **[Robin](The-Party/Robin-Wood.md)**, the occupation's medic, and recruited her. Inventory sobering note: one antitoxin dose left.

**Session 2 — the Council of Frostwatch.** Played per the module: Valerius's three questions, **the Severing**, the revelation of the true spread (one town gone dark, two quarantined) — and [Harah](../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md) **naming Rajaat**: the thousand-year quarantine, the second source, the walls that held eleven years. The paths were weighed (the Airlantis purge rejected as destroying the cure research; avalanches and sealing judged insufficient for a green valley) and **the party chose the Cure**: fly back to the ship, find the medical levels. Two complications landed at the table: **Erleena's comms are dead** (*"your number cannot be completed"*), and containment cover requires the **defilers — who will not talk to Harah**. (*"It's a long story. Meet me at the bar later."*) *(Module: [Return to Frostwatch](../Adventures/Return-to-Frostwatch.md); next: [Return to the Ship](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md))*

## Return to the Ship — session 1 (played 2026-09-08): the Whiteout, the Breach, and the roper

**Five went**: Shhhmeowmeow, Eustace, Musty-Jo, Beyond-Ce, and [Robin](The-Party/Robin-Wood.md) (her player Bella's first session at the table). Harah stayed at Frostwatch. The party long-rested after the council, took the parkas Frostwatch issued (Musty-Jo noting he has only chain mail under his), and left at dawn behind [Korrin's](../NPCs/Korrin.md) lead sled — the hoversled left where they abandoned it by the spore ravine, not retrieved.

**The Whiteout.** Run on the Glacial Crossing tracker as prepped: Survival, Animal Handling, and Perception each leg, Korrin's sled sliding from *clear* to *obscured* and back as the rolls went. Beyond-Ce **polymorphed into a wolf** to run with the dogs; Robin spent a 5th-level **Enhance Ability** on three drivers and kept *guidance* going; frostbite CON saves landed each leg, and Beyond-Ce was the only one to fail one. No exhaustion. Korrin was never lost, and the convoy crested the last ridge with the storm breaking.

**The Breach.** The ship under siege exactly as described — infected wolves, birds, and things that used to be deer crushing against the hull like zombies, police robots wading through with their tentacle-arms, turrets, and flyers thick in the air. The party's plan: hold outside the maelstrom while **Robin's *minor illusion*** — a five-foot something moving erratically, thrown thirty feet ahead with Eustace carrying her — drew the flyers off. Perception 24 found the **upper airlock door standing open** with a straight line to it. They ran: DEX saves against the turret lines (**Musty-Jo was clipped by friendly fire** — *"keep to the left, friends — no, my left!"* … *"Sorry! That one was mine. Nearly had it, though. Keep going, you're doing wonderfully."*), Athletics and Acrobatics through the horde, and the airlock code **1-3-5-7-9** with Sleight of Hand and Arcana. The door sealed; the corridor lights came up warm, section by section.

**Eddie.** *"Hey guys! I'm Eddie, the shipboard computer! Here to help! Sorry about the laser out there — that one was me! George is waiting for you on the next floor. I'll light the way!"* The table's first reaction was Hitchhiker's Guide jokes and Beyond-Ce asking, *"Are you perhaps going to kill us, Eddie?"* Eddie said George woke him during his research and that he's been working with George and Erleena on the cure and the antifungal. He advised going *down a level, then north-west* — and the party dropped straight down the nearest tube, the **south** one beside the airlock, and came out on the **observation deck by the theater, the bar, and the dance club: the dark side of the ring**, with George's lit doorway visible across the deck to the north-west.

**The roper.** Walking the ring toward the light, Eustace — who can't be surprised — caught tentacles coming along the ceiling: a **roper**, one of the plant growths that wall off George's pocket. Shhhmeowmeow put arrows and the laser pistol into it and was **grappled and poisoned**; Beyond-Ce ringed it in a **wall of fire** (a table discovery: the spell can be cast as a ring), dropped a **lightning bolt** through the flames, then — grappled and poisoned herself — **polymorphed into a fire elemental myrmidon**, which is immune to poison and burns whatever holds it; Eustace, hovering at the ceiling, finished it with **25 lightning damage from his storm cloud**. Three rounds. *"The roper is dead."*

**Where it stopped.** On the observation deck's dark side, halfway round to George's door, with **more plant growths visible ahead** and a second fight looming — the table called it there. Beyond-Ce is still a fire elemental (the poison comes back when the polymorph ends); Shhhmeowmeow is poisoned; Musty-Jo took a laser hit on the run. Nobody has reached S42 yet. *(Module: [Return to the Ship](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md) — [The Whiteout](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Whiteout.md), [The Breach](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md); next: [The Lab at S42](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Lab-at-S42.md). ## Return to the Ship — session 2 (played 2026-09-24): the last ropers, George, and a call to Erleena

**The ropers.** The session opened mid-fight on the observation deck's west walkway, with two S38c ropers between the party and George's door. Eddie brought the corridor lights up in a rolling wave (*"Hey, guys. I thought you could use some light."*), and the **aerialist androids** came out of the dance club to help, hauling on strands and flanking. Robin and Musty-Jo were both grappled and Musty-Jo was reeled in and bitten. Eustace dropped lightning on the tentacle holding Robin. Fire did most of the work: Beyond-Ce, still a fire elemental myrmidon, set the ropers burning, and one burned to death before she immolated the last one with a natural 20. Ruling at the table: **the roper poison ends when the tentacle lets go**, so Shhhmeowmeow came out of the fight clean. Beyond-Ce dropped the polymorph afterwards. Digging in the burnt remains turned up a **charged energy cell (10 laser-rifle shots)**, given to Musty-Jo, and a crew member's bones with a corroded, unreadable name badge. When the party asked the androids where George was, Eddie answered for them.

**George at S42.** Played from [The Lab at S42](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Lab-at-S42.md): the yellow door, the cream room, George walking to meet them on his own feet with the growths half what they were. He explained that he and Erleena had been talking over the ship's network and **woke Eddie together**. He handed over **six doses** of his anti-fungal (one each, the sixth for Erleena, which Shhhmeowmeow is carrying). A dose halts infection for 24 hours from when it's taken, and George's advice is to wait until the last minute. Then he washed the caged rabbitoid and handed out the packs. The table named them **Spore Blasters**: one for each of the five (Musty-Jo's rides like a saddlebag) and **an extra one for Erleena**, which the party is carrying down to her. The party gave George the short version of the outside news: the animals watching them, Frostwatch uneasy about who is under spore control, and the decision to go for a cure.

**Eddie on the way down and on the Lighthouse.** Drop tubes go all the way to the lower deck, and George has a lift in the next room down to the garden level. The party asked Eddie to tell Erleena they're coming by the drop tube, but the route isn't settled. Asked about the Lighthouse, Eddie said **it is not part of the ship's original equipment — "that's something new"** — and he can't turn it off. He could switch off the garden's lights, but the garden feeds the ship's food dispensers. The table took this as fresh support for their first-survey idea of **breaking the light**.

**Erleena on the comm.** George put her on the line from his console: *"What's going on up there? I heard the play by play from Eddie. Is everybody all right?"* She's well and her lab is robust. She had two asks. **(1) The door they left by is still opening and closing**, and things from outside are trying to get in. Eddie says the manual override is on the console down there, *"the same console you used to open it."* **(2) The froghemoth.** The lake creatures are paying more attention to her lab, and she's afraid it will lose its mind completely and crush it. George: *that's what the backpacks are for*. The party's plan is to wash it, not kill it.

**Where it stopped.** The party asked Eddie to send the dancing androids to check the next plant growth for ropers, then took a **long rest in George's med bay**. Everyone is at full strength, unpoisoned, with slots back. Next: down to Erleena (*"we have to kill the frogmoth that we were too afraid to kill last time,"* or wash it). *(Next: [Beneath the Lighthouse](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship/Beneath-the-Lighthouse.md).
