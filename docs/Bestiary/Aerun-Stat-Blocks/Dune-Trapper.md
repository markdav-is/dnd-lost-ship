## Traits, actions, and reactions
| Name | Type | Text for DDB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Oasis Lure | Trait | The dune trapper presents as a small watered hollow. A creature must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) check to spot the wrongness. The "pool" is its gullet. |
| Engulf | Trait | When a creature enters the hollow, it must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed. A swallowed creature is restrained and blinded, and takes 10 (3d6) acid damage at the start of each of its turns. A swallowed creature can escape with a successful DC 14 check, or can be cut out: dealing 20 slashing damage to the gullet (AC 14) opens it. |
| Sand Jets | Action | *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is pulled 10 feet toward the hollow. |
