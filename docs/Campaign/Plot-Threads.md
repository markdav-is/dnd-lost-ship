# Plot Threads

Active mysteries, dilemmas, and long-game levers. Each thread page states what the players know, what is actually true, and how to escalate it.

- [The First Source](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source) — Aerun's buried secret: the worms, the Spice, and the proof this has happened before.

- [The Price of Jak's Peace](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Price-of-Jaks-Peace) — what the Aura network really costs.

- [The Merchant Alliance](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Merchant-Alliance) — the Landsraad forming in the network's blind spots.
- [The Ship's Purpose](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose) — the vessel is a machine with a purpose, still running.

## How the threads connect

The ecological thread (the mold, the Green Age, the First Source) and the political thread (Jak, the merchant houses, the Spice) are **the same story at different scales**: power consuming the living world faster than it can regenerate. [Kaelen](/NPCs/Kaelen-al-Hajra) is the bridge — she carries the ecological memory, the deep desert's secret, and exists entirely outside the network. [George Decay](/NPCs/George-Decay) is the emotional anchor: his dying is the personal stakes, the ecological collapse the world stakes, the political fracture the civilizational stakes. Keep all three present in every session.
