# The Noble Alliance

Three to five major merchant and noble houses have begun meeting in the blind spots of the Aura network. They are not yet a Landsraad — but they are moving in that direction.

## The grievance

Jak's monopoly on the [Aura network](/World/The-Aura-Network) gives him an economic and military advantage no single house can counter. The houses do not want to destroy him; they want to **negotiate from strength**. The problem: they have no leverage. The crashed ship — the most significant technological discovery in living memory — changes that. Whoever controls access to its weapons, medicine, force fields, and archives holds the most powerful bargaining chip in the world.

## The agent: Tomas Vel-Maret

A merchant factor conducting a "mining rights survey" in the Barrier Peaks — actually the alliance's eyes in the north. See [his NPC page](/NPCs/Tomas-Vel-Maret) for the offer he makes the party and the sealed orders he holds.

## DM guidance

- The alliance is **not the good guys**. "Distributed technology" means distributed among the noble houses, not the people. The party is being offered a choice between two forms of concentrated power — the Dune parallel at its most direct.
- The house names are **[ANDY LORE]** — use whatever factions the campaign has established.
- The Spásistren cannot be corrupted by the alliance; belief is their armor. The only crack is doubt in Jak himself — which is why [Valerius's severing](/NPCs/High-Sister-Valerius) matters politically as well as spiritually.

## Escalation

Tomas's visits grow urgent → Spásistren presence increases → Jak starts asking the party who they've been talking to. The party isn't asked to pick a side; they're asked to navigate a world where every side has legitimate grievances and dangerous ambitions — while the infection threatens to make all of it irrelevant.
