# The Fungal Avalanche

*Return to Frostwatch, descent encounter 4 — chase hazard.*

![The Fungal Avalanche](../../.attachments/enc_avalanche.jpg)

A deep, rhythmic thudding shakes the mountainside. Snow begins to slide from the peaks above. A massive, infected **Remorhaz** is moving beneath the ice shelf, drawn by the vibration of the hoversled's engines.

## Mechanics

- **3 rounds** before the avalanche overtakes the sled.
- Each round the pilot makes **DC 15 DEX** to stay ahead. On a failure the sled is partially engulfed: all passengers **DC 14 STR** or **restrained** by the snow (escape: DC 14 STR check as an action).
- **The Remorhaz never surfaces.** Describe its shape moving beneath the ice — a massive, heat-radiating form melting the snow above it as it surges forward. If the party escapes, it does not pursue beyond its territory.

## Running it

The Remorhaz is not the point — the avalanche and the *visual* are: a huge infected thing moving under the ice like a submarine. Scale. The infection has reached creatures that were already terrifying.
