# Lake Pit

Twenty-eight square miles of clean blue water in the northern lobe of the [Dragon's Bowl](The-Dragons-Bowl.md) — the largest body of fresh water on the continent, and the only one a traveler is likely to call beautiful.

## At a glance

- **Size** Roughly six miles across. Cerulean, cold, and clear.
- **Where** Barely thirty miles from Urik, which is the astonishing part: a city of twenty thousand thirsty people, and this lake sits untouched over the rim.
- **Why it survives** Two reasons. You must descend a thousand feet of bad ground to reach it — and it lies under the protection of the druid **Enola**, who takes her work seriously.
- **Life** The shores teem: flora, birds, game, fish. For a traveler raised on the ring it is a genuinely disorienting sight.

## Traveler's notes

- **Do nothing to foul the water.** This is the single piece of advice every guide gives, in the same flat tone, and it is not about etiquette.
- There are stories of a **sunken city** in flooded caves beneath the lake — halls of gold, the usual. Treasure-seekers have gone looking. The lake is very deep and very cold.
- Filling water-skins here is permitted and free. Attempting to haul commercial quantities is not, and has ended badly for better-armed parties than yours.
