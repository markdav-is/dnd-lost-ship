# The Green Age

Aerun — the equatorial desert continent — was not always a desert. Centuries ago it was a living world of rivers, forests, and cities. It died fast enough to be catastrophic and slow enough to be survived. What is happening in the Barrier Peaks is how it began.

## The established history (DM canon, from Andy's interview)

The world-wide cataclysm is now anchored: **the Red Dragon brought about the apocalypse that buried the ancients' technology** — the same event that entombed the networks, the teleporters, and the cities' Titan-driven infrastructure ([Timeline](/World/Timeline), [The Dragons](/World/The-Dragons)). The dragons had been the **protectors of the [Titans](/World/The-Titans)**; why the Red Dragon turned the world under is an unanswered — perhaps unanswerable — question, tangled with whether the Titans were guardians or captors.

Aerun, the central continent, took the change hardest: the Green Age ended there within the reach of oral memory, and the Sandwalkers' ancestors survived by moving.

## What Kaelen's people know

- The [Sandwalkers](/World/Factions/The-Sandwalkers) preserve oral histories back to the Green Age. They are witnesses, not scholars — knowledge carried in song, scar, and the reading of ruins.
- Their word ***vel-keth*** — "the hunger that eats the world" — names the force that consumed their homeland. Kaelen recognizes vel-keth in the ship's infection.
- Three months ago the primal spirits of the deep desert began to go **silent**. *"Dead things do not warn you. Silent things are waiting."* The Sandwalkers call this the **Defiling Silence**; Kaelen tracked it north across the sea to the ship.
- The oldest ruins beneath Aerun's sand are **not human-built**.

## In-world theories of the desertification

The Red Dragon apocalypse is the DM's truth; the peoples of Vermoon still argue over what happened, and these contested versions remain useful as faction beliefs, rumor, and half-remembered fragments of the real event:

| Theory | Cause | Use it as |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Defiling Wars** | Sorcerer-kings' life-draining magic in a catastrophic war | Aerun city-state orthodoxy; the ruins' residual energy is real |
| **The Fallen Star** | A *second* crashed vessel impacted Aerun long ago | A rumor that may **also** be true — the ark in the Barrier Peaks need not be unique |
| **The Titan's Fall** | The Titan ("the Work") caused the devastation — or died trying to stop it | The Sandwalker tradition closest to the truth: the Titans, the dragons' broken guardianship, and the Red Dragon's fire are one story |
| **The Sorcerer-King's Bargain** | One ruler traded the land's vitality for immortality | Cautionary myth that now reads as prophecy about Jak and the network |

## Mechanical hook — the Primal Rite of Remembrance

Kaelen can perform a one-hour ritual using a piece of material from the ship, granting the party a **vision of Aerun during the Green Age**. The vision contains one specific fact about the ship's original purpose or the infection's nature that cannot be obtained any other way — prepare it to deliver whatever revelation the campaign needs at that moment. It is also the cleanest way to eventually *show* the Red Dragon's apocalypse — or the Titans' arrival — rather than tell it.

## The stakes

If the ship's systems are not neutralized, the infection will drain the living energy of the land itself: the mountains die, then the forests, then the plains. The world has been through this before. It barely survived.
