# Robin Wood

**Variant Human Cleric 8 (Life Domain) / Rogue 3 (Arcane Trickster)** (Custom background), Neutral Good. Player: **Bella** (sheet exported from the markdav_is D&D Beyond account). Joined for the Return to the Ship arc; first session at the table was the Whiteout and the Breach. Sheet: `assets/Robin Wood.pdf` (2026-08-05).

The occupation's infirmary medic at Frostwatch — a healer-cleric who arrived "a few days ago with everybody" (the Spásistren deployment), ran the hold's very well-stocked infirmary, and got recruited by the party on the spot. *"You want to save the world?" "Sure, I guess. It's an adventure."* That recruitment scene was the new player's introduction to the table.

## The rogue layer

Thieves' tools, a **forgery kit**, and **Thieves' Cant**. The kind, competent medic who says she's a cleric "of sorts" can pick locks, fake documents, and speak the language of thieves. Where she learned all that is the new player's story to tell — leave the door open.

## Personality (from the sheet)

- **Appearance:** average height; brown skin, brown eyes, brown hair. **Faith:** devoted to **Chauntea** — the grain goddess, which suits a medic whose heart is in rural communities.
- **Trait:** keeps multiple holy symbols on her and invokes whatever deity might be useful in the moment — flattery is her preferred trick.
- **Ideal:** Charity — she distributes what she acquires to the people who really need it. (Good)
- **Bond:** rural communities are where her heart lives; no one should die of treatable causes just because they don't have the money.
- **Flaw:** weary of anyone who comes from money — will stop to help anyone, even at risk to herself.
- **Note on the sheet:** *"Steal from med bay B4 you leave."* Read literally, that's a standing player intention — Robin means to loot the ship's medical stores before the party's done there, in character with the Charity ideal above.

## Backstory

**Full text:** [`assets/robin-wood-backstory.md`](../../../assets/robin-wood-backstory.md) — Bella's, pasted 2026-09-22 (the sheet spells her *Robin Woods*). The shape of it:

- **A farm kid.** A small farming community where everyone knew everyone and life revolved around the land, with a small temple nearby. From a young age she wanted to learn from its clerics — *"more than anything, she wanted to help people"* — and believed the ways of the **Earth Mother, Chauntea**, were how.
- **The lesson.** Helping people turned out to be less about what you knew and more about what you had: medicine, supplies, spell components, all of it costing something, and the people who needed help most being the ones who couldn't pay.
- **The merry band.** Kids her age were robbing travelers on the road. She hung around healing them; then she saw that what they stole could buy the supplies she needed; **so she joined.** It seemed perfectly reasonable: *Chauntea provided everything. If it came from the earth, how could it belong to one person? If someone had more than they needed while someone else was starving, wasn't moving it the right thing?*
- **The temple did not agree.** When they found out, they told her she was no longer welcome. **She was genuinely confused** — she hadn't stolen for herself. She still doesn't quite understand why they were so upset.
- **On the road.** She left with the thieves, took mercenary work, and watched the group fall apart one by one until she was traveling with entirely new people. **Now she is a cleric for hire.**
- **The creed, unchanged.** Helping people matters more than rules about ownership. She has a *questionable relationship with the concept of "stealing."* Wherever she goes, if someone needs help and she has the means, she'll give it — *"whether she had to pay for those means or 'redistribute' them along the way."*

This is the missing half of the sheet: the **Rogue 3 / Arcane Trickster** levels, the thieves' tools, the forgery kit, and *"of sorts"* all have their story now.

## What's known (from the played sessions)

- Came south with the military force but **wears no implant** — no blue light. Her view of the Spásistren is dry and unillusioned: volunteers who "join up and become… robo ninjas. Not me personally." She notes they're **recruiting heavily in Grønnfjord**.
- Asked if she'd been infected: "Not that I know of" — delivered right after the party explained they'd *all* been infected and cured by the ship's antitoxin.
- Well-traveled, vague about her origins ("I'm from… around"); not strong on geography; spends a lot of time outdoors.

## DM hooks

- **"Redistribution" on Aerun.** A cleric who thinks Chauntea's bounty can't belong to one person is about to land on a continent where **every declared price is a price, bonded cargo is sacred, and theft outside the forms is an arena-grade crime** ([Aerun — A Primer](../../Aerun-Players-Guide/Aerun-A-Primer.md)). She will not see the problem. Everyone else will.
- **The Gleaners.** *Take what you need, put back what you can* is her theology said out loud by a guild — and the Gleaners work in the open and pay for their seed. That is either the home she never had or an argument waiting to happen.
- **The temple that turned her out** is still there, somewhere behind her. If the campaign ever passes a Chauntean altar, she has a question for it.
- The party recruited her *while actively suspicious of everyone at Frostwatch* — she passed their sniff test when almost nobody else did. That trust is now a stake on the table.
- The natural [Counteragent](../../Game-Mechanics/The-Counteragent.md) medic for [Return to the Ship](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md): Medicine +12 turns dose triage into her spotlight, and Steady Aim makes her deadly with serum-tipped bolts.
- Background threads deliberately open for the new player: why the forgery kit, why "of sorts," why no implant when everyone around her wears one.
- **The med bay note is a hook, not just flavor.** If the party lets her raid the ship's infirmary before departure, that's Charity in action and free Restorative Ointment/healing stock for the road; if the party or ship security stops her, that's the Flaw (resentment of the well-off/well-stocked) landing on-screen. With George staying behind to run S42 as a soap factory, the medical stores are now *his* — which makes the theft a conversation.
