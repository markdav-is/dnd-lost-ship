# The Sky Hunters

*Return to Frostwatch, set-piece encounter — aerial combat on the exposed ridge.*

![The Sky Hunters](../.attachments/enc_sky_hunters.jpg)

## Read-aloud

> *The ridge opens up ahead of you, a half-mile of exposed rock and wind-scoured snow. The hoversled hums steadily. The view is extraordinary — the Barrier Peaks spread out in every direction, white and grey and ancient.*
>
> *Then you see the shadows.*
>
> *Three of them, circling high above. At first they look like griffons — the wingspan is right, the silhouette familiar. But the shadows they cast on the snow below are wrong. The shapes are distorted, stretched, as if something inside them has shifted. As they bank toward you, you see the pale, fibrous growth threading through their feathers like frost. Their eyes are white. Their beaks are open.*
>
> *They are not hunting prey. They are hunting you specifically.*

## Forces & terrain

- **3× Fungal Peryton** — CR 2: AC 13, HP 33, fly 60 ft. **Dive Attack** (+2d8 on a 30-ft dive gore), **Flyby**, **Spore Release** (when it takes piercing/slashing damage: 10-ft cloud, DC 13 CON or poisoned 1 min). Gore +5 (1d8+3); Talons +5 (2d4+3).
- **2× Spore-Touched Snowy Owlbear** on flanking rocky outcrops, punishing anyone who lands or is knocked down ([stats](/Bestiary/Fungal-Threats)).
- **Ridge:** 60 ft wide, 500 ft long; hoversled center. Eastern drop 200 ft; western cliff sheer and unclimbable.

## Hoversled damage

On initiative count 20, if the sled took damage that round: pilot **DC 14 DEX or INT** or the sled spins 90° — all passengers **DC 13 DEX** or prone. **Natural 1:** one random passenger is thrown (DC 15 DEX or fall 1d10 × 5 ft before catching themselves, if they can fly).

## Running it

The party can fly — the perytons will try to **separate flyers from the sled** and drive them toward the cliff face; the owlbears punish landings. Keep it three-dimensional and chaotic.
