# Blink

*The gap between two heartbeats.*

## The vision

Scrub plain. Low sun. Dust hanging gold in the air, and in the dust, a herd.

Lizards. Knee-high, hundreds of them, the color of the ground, standing so still you saw the dust before you saw them. Every head is turned the same way. Not toward you. Toward nothing.

You take a step.

The herd is thirty feet to the left.

You did not see it move. There was no running, no scatter, no sound. It was there, and it is here, and the dust where it was is still settling as though it had only just stopped being there.

You take another step. The herd is behind you.

## The beast

One of them does not go with the others. A [z'tal](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#ztal), no bigger than a dog, standing in front of you with its throat going in and out. Skittish, the drovers call them. Everyone calls them that. Nobody has ever caught one.

It looks at you. You look at it. You feel your heart beat, once.

Between that beat and the next, there is a space. You have never noticed it. The z'tal lives in it. It shows you — not by moving, but by holding still so exactly that you see the beat, and the gap, and the beat, and in the gap, the whole herd, everywhere at once, and nowhere.

Then your heart beats, and the z'tal is ten feet away, still looking at you, and you are standing where it was.

You did not feel it happen. That is how you know it was yours.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. You are not where you lay down — a few feet over, on the bare dust outside the ring, with no track between.

Your guide looks at the dust, and at you, and says nothing. Blue at the edge of your eye.

## The gift

**Blink.** *As a bonus action, you teleport up to 10 feet to a space you can see.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: one small lizard, throat going, exactly where you were.
