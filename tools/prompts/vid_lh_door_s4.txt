The camera drifts slowly and smoothly back, away from the desert, until the glowing torn edges of the split lighthouse frame the desert as a tall rift again, with the roots and orange haze of the island in the foreground. Through the rift: a hostile desert under a blinding, bleached white sky with a merciless glare: a grey sea of dust, ochre cliffs with silt pouring over them, tall jagged spires of splintered black obsidian, harsh sand and grit tearing sideways on a savage wind, and a long ridge of churned sand moving across the plain as if something huge swims beneath it. Golden spores and grit blast out through the rift toward the viewer. Painted style. Slow, dreamlike, ominous. No people.
