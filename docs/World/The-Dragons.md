# The Dragons

The great dragons of Vermoon — awakened by the Prophecy of the Red Dragon, paired with the heroes of the previous campaign, and now returned to hibernation. Their concern is the world at large, not the affairs of men. **They only show up when there is a global crisis**, and the Barrier Peaks infection is not one yet.

Source: Andy's world-building interview, `assets/Vermoon World-Building Session_...transcript.txt`; names, histories, and Dildro's secret from his 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (cards *Dragon Lore*, *Titans*, *What Happened to Dildro*, *A View of Dirtroit*, *Freeing Undaloth*, *Airlantis – Save or Abandon*). The board is prep, not a transcript.

## Where dragons come from

The [Titans](The-Titans.md) made the dragons **first**, before any other people, to keep the peace and protect the world. Each of the four **Prime Dragons** was sworn protector to one Primordial Titan, and their power is *tied* to their Titan's — so when [the Antari](The-Antari.md) enslaved the Titans and bled them for power, the dragons were subdued with them.

**The Red Dragon's plan.** Seeing how it would end, the Red Dragon chose to stop the humans at the cost of destruction throughout the land. She sealed the power of the other great dragons into **colored orbs**, had them placed strategically around the world, and set them off — catastrophic explosions of magic, each according to the magic in its orb. That is the apocalypse that buried the Antari ([Timeline](Timeline.md)). The orbs survived; they are the "dragon balls" the party collected.

Afterward, with the Titans "fully asleep and dead to the world," the dragons stayed at their posts anyway — *why change now?* All but one.

## The Prophecy of the Red Dragon

The dragons activated in response to a world-threatening danger. The Prophecy of the Red Dragon brought the heroes together; each was paired with a dragon, the party gathered the **dragon orbs** ("dragon balls"), and the arc resolved aboard the orbital space station with the fall of the [First Administrator](Timeline.md). Along the way the heroes — with [Erleena Riser](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md), then an archaeologist of the ancients' technology — restored the planet-wide comm network, with the dragons' help. The prophecy is now **fulfilled** — and with no world-scale threat, the dragons have gone dormant.

**How it began (2023 board).** Before the Fire Titan was completely taken, her Red Dragon was **tasked with finding those who would be able to free the Titans**. Seven centuries later that search arrived as a **shared dream**: on the same night, each hero dreamed of standing in a red dragon's lair beside companions of many years — people they had never met — and woke in Yorrem on festival day. Eustace, who joined later, never had the dream; Essos's comment on that was *"there is always one more added."*

### Jak's young red dragon

**Canon from Mark, who played Jak (2026-09-21): Jak has a young red dragon, and it now lives at the [Temple of Nord](../Locations/Gronnfjord.md) in Grønnfjord.** Andy's board never recorded it — "Dragons for Gobbeldy and Jak, who are they, where are they?" was still on his loose-ends list at the end — and Mark's notebook from that campaign is missing, so that is nearly everything currently known. **How public it is (Mark, 2026-09-21): the dragon is not unknown to the Nords of Grønnfjord — it is a local fact of life there — but it is not widely known beyond that.** The rest of Vermoon has not heard that the Administrator keeps a red dragon. Not yet established, and not to be invented: its name; how and where Jak came by it; how it relates to the great Red Dragon of the prophecy (offspring, the same dragon made young, or no relation); and whether it sleeps like the others or is awake. Ask Mark, and Andy.

### Essos, the Black Dragon

- Found in an acid-pooled lair east of Dirtroit, tended by kobolds, with centipedes, snakes, and scorpions for pets. If the party had killed them all he would have been angry; if Shhhmeowmeow had attacked, he would simply have **left** — a fight he would win was not the point.
- **He is the dragon who destroyed Nepeta**, Shhhmeowmeow's home ([Taldea](Taldea.md)). His answer to her anger, per the notes: *we all have our part to play, and fate's weave has never been easy.*
- On the Antari: *"dumb people who deserved what they got."* On the ball Eustace carried: *more valuable, when the time comes, than any gold or weapon.*
- Afterward a working ally: reachable through the bond (a kobold turns up to guide the party), and willing to **carry the whole party** on his back — two days from Shimmerhill to Yorrem, holding onto scales and spikes, a level of exhaustion on landing. Persuasion gets a lift; **Shhhmeowmeow laying on the guilt gets it with advantage.**
- It was Essos who told the party about the Prime Dragons, the Titans they guarded, and Undaloth.

### Dildro, the Blue Dragon — "Burl"

- The party met him three times as a human before they knew: **Burl Grives**, the cloaked man reading Rico's notes in Drytide, who claimed the engine as his idea (*"How else would Rico come up with combining classic mechanics and arcane magics?"*) and left by *dimension door*; **Burl Ives**, trapped in a flooded dome in Oceana, who waved an automaton off with an Antari badge (*"They are with me"*) and walked out through a portal; and the voice that answered when someone communed with the **Silver Dragon Ball** on Airlantis, asking them to come and talk.
- **What he wants: off the planet.** He came to believe the dragons are not of this world — brought here by the Titans — and wanted to travel the stars and learn his heritage. With the Antari gone and their technology buried there was no way off, unless one survived in their cities.
- **What he did about it:** seeded [Rico's](../NPCs/Rico.md) research into magical engines; founded the **[Titan Fighters](Factions/The-Titan-Fighters.md)** to protect the Titans (and his friends the other dragons) and to hunt Antari ruins; and, once the Void was found and the prophecy woke, **dribbled breadcrumbs to the heroes**. His shopping list: engine — Rico; hull materials — Hollowstorm; electronics — the Titan Fighters; magic — the Academy; habitation — unsolved.
- Andy's design note: *"Burl is a red herring to make everyone think he is the main baddy, but really down the road they have similar goals."* The Administrator thought he was dead. He had not known about Eros Station at all until the party found it.
- When the party agreed to help him he **kept his human form** and declined the "subservient role" the other dragons took with their partners.

### Undaloth, the Green Dragon

*This answers the old open question from Eustace's sheet.*

- One of the dragons who finally stopped the humans. Sent to his assigned place, he was **ambushed**; he set off his orb where it needed to go and **died doing it**. Nobody knew what became of the body. The humans kept his orb hidden away.
- [Eustace](../Campaign/The-Party/Eustace-Thunderbeak.md) ended up with the **Green Ball** and the dreams that came with it: a nameless voice asking questions; then the dragon overwhelmed by machines and men, the orb lifted out by human hands, the body on a table in a bright room with the sea outside the windows, and eyes opening **red**.
- The Antari had rebuilt him in **Oceana's** bio-mechanical lab. The party fought the body while **[Erleena](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) uploaded his soul back into it**. He lived — with a mechanized body that no longer makes its own energy and must be **charged**, limbs he had to relearn, and **Aura talking in his head** (first in Antari, then, once he answered, in Common). He can be reached by phone.
- He and Erleena stayed in Oceana to work on his rehabilitation. The *"2,500 in gems"* on Eustace's sheet is still unexplained.

## Will they help now?

**Dormant, not gone.** The dragons' concern is the world, not local crises. The Barrier Peaks infection is — so far — a regional problem, so they sleep. DM guidance from Andy:

- They can be sought out **one by one**, and each decides whether the cause merits waking.
- If the infection escalates to a genuinely world-scale threat (a second Aerun), draconic intervention becomes plausible — a powerful escalation lever for the endgame.
- The **Blue Dragon** is the wildcard: off-world or orbiting, disposition unknown, and ideologically invested in the Titans-as-captors question ([The Titans](The-Titans.md)).

They do not factor into the Return to the Ship arc or the voyage to Aerun. Keep them in reserve.
