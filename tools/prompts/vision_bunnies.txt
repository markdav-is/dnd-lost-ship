A wide 16:9 painting in exactly the same painted style, warm golden palette and soft glowing light as the reference lighthouse painting. A psychedelic mushroom-trip vision of 'everything is connected': joyful, warm, tender, wondrous, a happy dream. Fine glowing gold threads of light, like luminous mycelium, run between every living thing, pulsing softly; golden spores drift like fireflies; colours a little too rich, edges shimmering with faint iridescence, everything gently breathing. The inner garden of the island at the foot of the mycelium-wrapped lighthouse, close to the ground among the moss. A dozen soft, fat, round rust-coloured rabbits flecked with gold spores, adorable and content, nibbling, nuzzling each other, one asleep, two kits curled together. From each rabbit fine glowing gold threads run to every other rabbit, down into the moss and the glowing roots, and away up to the tower in the background, so the whole warren is one shining web. Their hearts glow faintly gold through their fur, all at the same moment. Tiny glowing mushrooms, curling moss, gold motes. Cosy, cute, utterly peaceful.
