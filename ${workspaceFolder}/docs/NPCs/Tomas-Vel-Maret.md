# Tomas Vel-Maret

Merchant factor and the [noble alliance's](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Noble-Alliance) agent in the north — in the Barrier Peaks for weeks under cover of a mining-rights survey. Not a villain: a pragmatist who has read how stories about information monopolies end. **Not yet introduced in play.**

## The offer he makes the party

- Political protection in the Aerun city-states, independent of Jak's favor.
- Access to alliance resources: information networks, safe houses, financial support.
- A promise that ship technology will be **distributed rather than monopolized** (distributed among the houses, that is).
- Intelligence on what the Spásistren will do with the ship when the party's mission ends.

## His leverage — the sealed orders

Tomas holds a copy of the Spásistren's sealed orders: once the party's mission is complete, the ship is to be **sealed** — party rewarded, Jak keeping exclusive access to whatever data was extracted. The council's "three paths" were not the whole menu. This is the fourth path nobody presented.

## Playing him

He tells the party *true things* in service of a larger agenda — political vertigo, the Dune tone. He genuinely believes Jak's monopoly ends badly for everyone including Jak. He is not entirely trustworthy either, and he knows the party will eventually realize that; he plans for it.
