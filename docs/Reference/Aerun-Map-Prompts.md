## Revision brief for the atlas (resolved in v17)

The draft has the plateau reading correctly — keep the cliff rendering, the silt texture, the palette, the labels, and every city position. Three fixes:

1. **The wall should sweep, not jut.** Replace the squared-off peninsula on the east side with **one long graceful arc**: from Raam the rim wall curves southeast, then south, running roughly parallel to the eastern coast — a sweeping shoulder of highland that keeps the living band narrow down that whole shore — and finally bends west to reach Balic. No right angles, no rectangular promontory, no separate lobe: it is the *edge of the same plateau* flowing down the coast. The Crescent Forest fills the corridor between that curve and the sea (Nibenay near the north end, Gulg in the belly of the curve, Badlands stepping down from the wall's flank). Retire the "Prong" label; call the feature nothing, or **"The Southern Sweep."**

2. **The wall must come DOWN as it goes south.** Right now it is uniformly sheer, so Balic has no way to reach the silt. Grade it: **sheer and ~2,000 ft in the north and west** → **stepped benches and long scree ramps** along the southern sweep → **a low saddle only a few hundred feet high at Balic**, where the silt spills over in a broad ramp right down to the city. Balic must visibly sit *at a gap in the wall* — the one place the rim finally lets someone across — with its silt quays reachable by ramp and stair from the ocean harbor below. Same treatment, less extreme, at the Raam shelf.

3. **Drop the Balic Saddle to a low notch** — two or three hundred feet, not a cliff — so the ramp and the city's stairs plainly connect the ocean harbor to the silt above. Balic should read as *the one place the wall lets people across*.
4. **Put the Ringing Mountains along the whole west coast** (Tyr → Osgaker → Hollowstorm → Urik), taller than the plateau, and add a small **wind rose or arrows in the western ocean showing the prevailing wind blowing east onto them** — the visual explanation for why the west is green and the interior is dead.
5. **Draw the silt lane end to end.** The dotted **Long Reach** must start at Raam's silt quays and finish at Balic's — one continuous marked route across the gray with three waystation towers on it, not a stub that fades out mid-map. It is the continent's fastest trade route and should read as deliberate infrastructure.
6. **Close the Ring Road.** It must be a **single unbroken ochre loop** running through every settlement and returning to itself — no gaps, no dead ends, no stretches where the line fades out. Trace it once, continuously, all the way around the island: Draj → Blackguard → Raam → Nibenay → Gulg → Amber Valley → Balic → Urik → Hollowstorm → Osgaker → Tyr → back to Draj, with Eldorado on a short spur to its rim pass.

*(If a fix pass makes other things worse, re-run from the full prompt below with these three points folded in rather than patching the existing image.)*

## The continent prompt (v2 — plateau-explicit)

> A detailed fantasy atlas map of the desert island continent of **AERUN**, painted like a premium D&D campaign-setting poster: top-down painterly cartography with strong 3D terrain relief, weathered parchment-and-ocean palette, dark teal textured sea, ornate silver compass rose, elegant serif labels. Landscape 4:3. Title "AERUN" upper right; **scale bar reading 0–100–200 miles** (the continent spans ~500 miles).
>
> **THE ONE BIG IDEA — READ FIRST:** the center of this island is a **colossal flat-topped MESA** — a tableland plateau like Monument Valley scaled to half a continent. **The flat TOP of the mesa is a smooth, pale-gray dust plain (the Sea of Silt).** The gray plain is the HIGHEST large surface on the map — higher than all the green land, higher than everything except a few mountain peaks. This is a RAISED TABLE, absolutely not a crater, not a lake, not a lowland, not a shoreline.
>
> **BUILD THE TERRAIN BOTTOM-TO-TOP, three altitude bands:**
> 1. **Sea level — the ocean:** dark teal water surrounding the island on all sides. Sailing ships near harbors.
> 2. **The green coastal ring (low):** a band of living lowland around the island's entire edge — farmland, olive terraces, scrub, forest on the east — carrying **THE RING ROAD**: one **single unbroken closed loop** of ochre caravan highway that runs through every city and returns to itself — **no gaps, breaks, or fading stretches anywhere** — with tiny mekillot-wagon icons. All twelve settlements live in this band.
> 3. **The central mesa (high):** rising from the middle of the green ring, **steep terracotta cliff walls climb ~2,000 feet** on every side to the tabletop. **The mesa's outline is irregular and asymmetric — never a smooth oval or circle, and never a squared-off peninsula.** Its defining feature is **the Southern Sweep**: from Raam the cliff edge **curves southeast and then south in one long graceful arc**, running roughly parallel to the eastern coast and keeping the green band narrow down that whole shore, before bending west to reach Balic. The **Crescent Forest** fills that corridor (Nibenay near the north end, Gulg in the belly of the curve, rust-red Badlands stepping down from the wall's flank). **The wall's height falls as it sweeps south:** sheer ~2,000-ft cliffs in the north and west, stepped benches and long scree ramps along the sweep, and **the Balic Saddle — a broad shallow notch only two or three hundred feet above the coastal plain**, where the silt spills over in a wide ramp right down to the city. Balic sits *in that gap*: draw the wall visibly collapsing to a low ridge there, not a cliff.
>
> **HOW TO MAKE THE ELEVATION READ (do all of these):**
> - **Shadow logic:** light from the northwest; the mesa's southeastern cliff walls cast one continuous soft shadow band **outward onto the green lowlands**. The gray tabletop is brightly, evenly lit.
> - **Cliff texture:** the mesa edge is everywhere a rendered cliff face — vertical striations, buttresses, scree fans at the foot — with the **gray always meeting the TOP edge of the cliff** and green always at the BOTTOM.
> - **Silt-falls:** at several low notches in the rim, thin gray dust-falls spill over the edge and streak partway down the cliff face, like slow waterfalls of powder.
> - **Mountain overlaps:** the **Ringing Mountains run the entire west coast** — a continuous snow-touched spine from the northwestern lobe above Tyr, down past Osgaker and Hollowstorm, to Urik in the southwest — rising from the coastal ring and standing **taller than the mesa**, peaks overlapping in front of and above the tabletop edge, with the smoking **volcano (Mountain of the Black Crown)** at their southern end beside Urik. This range is the map's dominant landform after the plateau itself. On the east, rust-red **Badlands** mesas rise behind the Crescent Forest to shoulder height with the rim.
> - **The two spill-ramps:** at the mesa's **northeast** (toward Raam) and **south** (toward Balic), the gray descends the wall in broad **stepped, terraced ramps** — frozen lobes of silt stair-stepping partway down — the northeast one stopping at a shelf above Raam, the southern one pouring through the **low saddle at Balic** — where the wall has come down to a few hundred feet — as a broad ramp stopping dead at the **rock dam** on whose face the city is built. **The gray NEVER reaches the ocean.**
>
> **ON THE GRAY TABLETOP (sparse):** one **clearly drawn dotted shipping lane — the Long Reach — running unbroken from the silt quays above RAAM in the northeast, southwest across the eastern and southern reaches, to the silt quays above BALIC in the south.** It must visibly *connect the two portage cities end to end*: begin it at Raam's shelf and end it at Balic's crest, with a line of tiny bone channel-markers along its length, **three small waystation towers** spaced along it (label them *First Mark*, *Midreach*, *Saltbone*), and two or three skimmer-convoy icons under way. A couple of short marked spurs may branch off toward harvest shoals. **The entire northwestern third of the gray: completely empty. No lane, no icons, no labels, no texture variation. Blank.**
>
> **THE TWELVE SETTLEMENTS** (city icons as small dots-with-architecture at this 500-mile scale), clockwise from north: **DRAJ** (north coast, on a brown mudflat apron at the cliff's foot; its **low stone causeway runs inland from the Ring Road across the mud to the city gate** — a landward connection at sea level, never a road onto or toward the silt) · **BLACKGUARD** (grim NE-coast waypoint) · **RAAM** (northeast, largest icon, between its blue ocean harbor and the silt shelf-ramp above it) · **NIBENAY** (east, forest edge, rice paddies) · **GULG** (southeast, a green ring inside the Crescent Forest) · **AMBER VALLEY** (south-coast caravanserai) · **BALIC** (south coast, white harbor city built into the rock dam at the foot of the southern silt-ramp — ocean on one side, gray above on the other) · **URIK** (southwest, square yellow fortress-city under the volcano) · **HOLLOWSTORM** (west promontory) · **OSGAKER** (northwest coast) · **TYR** (northwest foothills, ziggurat and stadium) · **ELDORADO** (small town at a high rim pass behind Tyr).
>
> **LANDMARKS** (small icons, fine labels, all in the green band unless noted): the **Dragon's Bowl** — an irregular, **elongated** (not round) sunken basin in the barrens **~30 miles north of Urik** (**outside the mesa, in the lowlands**), with the tiny brilliant-blue **Lake Pit** tucked in its **northern lobe** rather than its center, and one stretch of its southern rim reading as breached; the only true blue water inland · the ruins of **Yaramuke** with the **Black Oasis at their center** (one site — the poisoned water lies *inside* the ruins, never drawn as a separate lake nearby), midway between Urik and Raam on the Road of Kings · **Altaruk**, a walled caravan fort on the southern Ring Road ~35 miles from Balic · the **Mekillot Mountains** with **Salt View** · **Walis** on its rock spire · **Ogo** under the forest canopy · **Bitter Well** on the western scrub.
>
> **FORBIDDEN (hard negatives):** do not render the gray as a lake, bay, inland sea at coast level, crater, or depression. No beaches or shorelines where gray meets green at equal height. No water touching the gray anywhere. No green or roads on the tabletop. The gray is never darker or lower-looking than the coastal ring — it is the bright, high, empty roof of the map.
>
> **MOOD:** sun-bleached and majestic; a living green ring of commerce hugging the shores of an island crowned by a vast dead gray table — and faint wakes of something enormous moving beneath the dust, subtle enough to miss.

## City prompts

One per city, in roster order, **each aligned to the approved atlas (`assets/Aerun_Atlas.png`)** — every entry opens with an *Atlas context* line stating exactly where that city sits on the continent and what must appear at the edges of its map, so the city sheets and the atlas agree. Deliverable status, reference maps, mood art, and district lore: [Aerun City Map Roster](Aerun-City-Map-Roster.md).

**Shared preamble — prepend to any city prompt:**

> A detailed top-down fantasy city map matching the approved Aerun atlas: painterly cartography, weathered parchment-and-ocean palette, soft 3D relief on walls and terrain, ochre **Ring Road**, ornate compass rose, serif labels, a **scale bar in feet**, and a boxed legend. Landscape 4:3. The city fills most of the frame with its immediate hinterland at the edges. **Continental context to honor:** the interior of Aerun is a raised gray silt plateau ringed by cliffs; the coastal lowlands are mostly **arid tan scrub**, green only in the western mountain valleys and the eastern Crescent Forest. Where the silt appears on a city map it is **matte bone-gray, flat, and ABOVE the city** — never water, never below.

### Tyr — the Iron City *(approved)*

> *Atlas context: far northwest, cradled in the green foothills of the snow-capped **Ringing Mountains**, which fill the map's western edge. The Ring Road arrives from the southwest coast and departs northeast toward Draj. The rim cliffs and the gray silt lie east/southeast beyond the town of **Eldorado** at its pass — visible as a distant wall, not adjacent. Green, watered country: this is the wettest city on Aerun.*
>
> Double-ring plan: an inner walled precinct holding the **Golden Tower**, **High Bureaus**, **King's Gardens**, and **Templar District**; an outer ring holding the **Artisan District**, **Sculptor's Square**, **the Warrens** with the **Elven Market** inside them, **Noble**, **Merchant**, and **Caravan Districts**, **Iron Square**, the **Gladiatorial Stadium**, the **Brickyards**, and the **Slave Pits**. At the heart, the great stepped **Ziggurat** — sealed since the champion's assassination. Gates: the **Grand Gate** (west), **Stadium Gate**, **Caravan Gate**. Behind the city, mountain slopes with the **iron mine approaches** climbing away and three guard posts on the mine road. Terraced gardens and irrigated fields on the lower slopes. ~15,000 inside the walls.

### Draj — the Mudflat City *(approved)*

> *Atlas context: the **north coast**, at the foot of the plateau's northern cliffs. Ocean to the north; a broad brown **mudflat apron** all around the city; the gray silt sits high atop the cliffs to the **south**, far above and out of reach. The Ring Road runs east–west along the firm coastal ground inland of the mud.*
>
> Warrior-theocracy on a mudflat island at sea level. **The causeway:** one low stone road running **from firm ground where the Ring Road passes, across a few miles of soft impassable mudflat, to the Golden Moon Gate** — the city's only land connection and its whole defense (any army leaving it bogs down). It is a road over **mud, at coastal level**: it must **not** extend into the gray silt and must **not** climb the cliffs. Dense walled grid of clan compounds; at the center the walled **Two Moon City** precinct with the **Palace of Glorious Conquest** (a 200-ft stepped pyramid), the **Arena**, **Temples of Kal and Guthay**, the **Father and Master Temple**, the **Royal Menagerie**, and the **House Tsalaxa Compound**. Beyond the walls: the **Fields of Plenty** — irrigation ditches, grain and hemp — with reed marsh and mud pools where the flats meet the cliff foot. ~18,000 people.

### Raam — the Silt-Shelf Metropolis *(approved)*

> *Atlas context: the **northeast**, exactly where the plateau's cliff edge meets the ocean — the city is wedged between them. **Ocean harbor on the northeast face** at sea level; the **silt shelf ~1,000 ft above at its southwest back**, where the **Long Reach** lane sets out across the gray (show its first bone channel-markers leaving the quays). The Ring Road passes along the coast below. This is the busiest transfer point on the continent.*
>
> The largest and least-governed city on the ring, straddling two levels and **built into the cliff between them**. Below: deepwater piers, ships, and the Ring Road's **Caravan Exchange Yard** outside the walls. Above: the **Silt Transfer** quays on the shelf, skimmers moored on the gray. Between: **the Ascent Quarter** — switchback freight terraces cut into the rock face, warehouses, porter barracks, toll-houses, and tenement galleries carved into the cliff between the bends, with **counterweight hoists** (silt-ballast skips, giant-hair rope) working the final ~300-ft band beside zigzag porter-stairs. City plan: the walled **Queen's Hill** with the **Ivory Palace**, Royal Barracks, Hall of Conquest, and Consecrated Sepulcher of Badna; then **Temple Quarter**, **Official Quarter**, **Cons Quarter**, **Ascetic Row**, and the sprawling **Low Quarter** dissolving into unwalled chaos at the edges. Mark the **Public Armory**, **Natural Arena**, **School of the Way**, **Crematory**, and the **Office of House M'Ke**. ~40,000 inside, as many again in warlord estates outside.

### Nibenay — the City of Bubbling Springs *(approved)*

> *Atlas context: the **east coast**, between the **rust-red Badlands** rising to its north and west and the northern tip of the **Crescent Forest** to its south. The cliff wall and gray silt stand beyond the Badlands to the west; ocean to the east. The Ring Road runs north–south through the city, from Raam down toward Gulg.*
>
> Ancient, decadent, relief-carved city built atop hot springs. At its heart the walled forbidden sub-city (**Royal Palace / Naggaramakam**), enterable only by the templar-wives. Districts: **Western District**, **Sages' District** with **Sages' Square** and the **Academy of the Free Purpose**, the **Hill District**, and the **Reservoir District** around the great **City Reservoir**. Feature the **Temple of the Sun**, the **Serpent Towers**, **Palm Court**, the **Temple of Trade**, the **Elven Market**, the **Arena**, and a **Necropolis** on the hill outside. Spring-fed channels thread the streets; **rice paddies** terrace the forest-facing (southern) side; agafari timber yards by the **Mekillot Gate** where the logging roads come in from the forest. Every building carved with stone reliefs. ~24,000 people.

### Gulg — the Thorn City *(approved)*

> *Atlas context: **inside the Crescent Forest** on the southeast, deep under canopy — the only city with no open horizon. The Ring Road runs along the forest's eastern edge and Gulg hangs off it by a guarded **spur track**; **Ogo** lies south under the same canopy. No silt, no cliffs, no ocean in frame — just trees to every edge.*
>
> Break the pattern of the other cities: **no stone walls, no grid**. The city is a great organic oval defined by a single living **hedge-wall of interwoven thorn-trees**, with the forest canopy pressing close on every side (let trees overhang and interpenetrate the city; dappled light). At the exact center, **Sunlight Home**: one agafari tree grown to *titanic* scale — the tallest thing on the map by far — the small wooden palace in its highest limbs and the nganga's huts on the branches below. An inner earthen ring, the **Mopti Wall**, encloses the royal precinct around the tree. Everything else is **dagadas**: dozens of round walled hut-compounds along twisting dirt roads with dusty squares, animal pens, and groves between them — no two roads parallel. Gates: the **People's Gate** (where the spur track leaves for the Ring Road), the **Queen's Gate**, plus hunters' and exiles' posterns. Outside the hedge: travelers' dagadas, **House Inika's lone Trading House** by the spur, and hunters' trails radiating into the trees. Label candidates: Sunlight Home, Mopti Wall, People's Gate, Queen's Gate, Drum Circle, Elven Market dagada, Trading House. Smallest city (~8,000) — keep it tight, green, and strange.

### Balic — the City of Two Seas *(next)*

> *Atlas context: the **south coast, directly at the foot of the **Balic Saddle** — the broad low ramp where the silt plateau comes down toward the sea (see the atlas: the gray descends a wide chute between cliff shoulders and stops just above the city). Ocean and its harbor to the **south**; the ramp and gray silt rising to the **north**; **Altaruk** and the Mekillot Mountains east along the Ring Road; arid tan scrub with irrigated groves inland. The Ring Road arrives from Urik in the west and departs east toward Altaruk.*
>
> **One-line touchstone: Minas Tirith built into the Rim Wall, with a sea of gray dust on top instead of a white tower.** The city climbs the saddle's rock face in **concentric walled tiers**, each ~100 ft above the last, divided by a great stone buttress, linked by switchback ramp-roads and tunnels — but the summit is not a citadel: it is the **silt quay deck**, schooners moored on the gray **above the rooftops**, where the **Long Reach** lane comes in off the silt. **Two waterfronts on opposite faces:** the **south face** opens on the blue **ocean harbor** — stone quays, breakwater lighthouses, sailing ships, the busiest port on the map set; the **north side** is the **dam**, the rock barrier holding the silt ramp back, its face terraced with the city. Two portage systems to draw: **the Cochlea** — expose a cutaway or great open shaft-mouths of a **colossal spiral porter-stair turning down through the dam's interior** (Silo-style: one endless helix, market landings at each revolution, lamplight in the bores, a double file of porters ascending and descending) — and the exterior **Stair of Sails** crane galleries with silt-ballast hoist skips for heavy freight. Fill every stair with **life at human scale**: porter files under chitin back-frames, pack-crodlu strings, stalls on the landings. Precincts to label: **Guilders'**, **Arena**, **Harbor** (ocean side), **Silt Quarter** (dam-crest side), **Market**, **Villa**, around the central **megalitheum**; the **agora** ringed by the **Elven Market** at the base of the old dictator's rocky fortress (empty now — the **Chamber of Patricians** meets there). **Theaters** everywhere, ramshackle to amphitheater — name the **Thespian House** (Market Precinct) and the **Olive Tree** inn on the **Road of Legions**. Land approach only from the west, through olive groves, vineyards, and grain farms with legion field-camps among the crops. Wealthiest city (~24,000): make it gleam. *The visual: a city carved into a dam holding back a sea of dust, its heart a turning screw of stairs.*

### Urik — the City of Lions *(next)*

> *Atlas context: the **southwest**, on open arid tan barrens, with the smoking **Mountain of the Black Crown** looming immediately north of the city and the southern end of the **Ringing Mountains** beyond it. The Ring Road passes the walls east–west (Hollowstorm northwest, Balic southeast). The rim cliffs and gray silt stand off to the northeast, distant. **Yaramuke's ruins and the Dragon's Bowl lie north up the Road of Kings** — off-map, but the road to them should leave the north gate.*
>
> The anti-Balic: **rigid rectilinear military grid**, the only square city in the series. Massive **yellow-glazed walls** with lion reliefs and banner-poles; walled internal quarters with checkpoint gates between them — the city is compartmentalized like a fortress. At center, the **citadel**: a full square mile of palace, templar administration, drill fields, barracks, and armory — a fortress inside the fortress, home of the thousand half-giant **Imperial Guard**. Quarters to label: **Potters' Square** (kilns; the city's artistic glory), the **Obsidian Quarter** (workshops, deliberately identical buildings — draw the uniformity), the **Old District** (the one irregular, characterful quarter), and the **Destrier's Kilns**. Gates open onto **sealed roads** with guard posts — nothing enters Urik unobserved; the Ring Road passes through a controlled caravan plaza outside the walls. Behind everything, the **Black Crown** smoking, with **obsidian quarry terraces** scarred into its flanks and a guarded slave road climbing to them. Outside the walls: irrigated plantations and vast kank herds on the tan plain. Observatory towers welcome — Urik is famed for its astronomers. ~20,000 people, all exactly where Hamanu's Code says they should be. Make it feel disciplined, prosperous, and airless.

### Continuity checklist (apply to every city sheet)

- **Silt is always gray, matte, flat, and HIGH** — above the city where it appears at all. Never blue, never water, never below the streets.
- **The Ring Road is ochre** and enters/leaves each city in the directions the atlas shows.
- **Lowlands are arid tan scrub** except the western mountain valleys (Tyr) and the eastern forest (Nibenay, Gulg).
- **Scale bars in feet** on city sheets (the atlas is in miles) — city footprints run one to a few square miles.
- **Arenas everywhere**: every city has one, because the arena is the continent's court ([the War of Champions](../World/Dark-Sun/Glossary.md)).
- **Cliff faces read as columnar ochre rock** with scree at the foot, matching the atlas's rendering of the Rim Wall.

## Drill-in template (regional maps)

> A detailed fantasy regional map, painted in the same style as [describe/attach the continent map]: top-down painterly cartography, soft 3D relief, parchment palette, serif labels, compass rose. This map covers **[REGION]** of the desert continent of Aerun — roughly [WHICH ARC of the coastal ring], with the ocean on one side and the gray Sea of Silt behind the Rim Wall cliffs on the other. The Ring Road crosses the whole map through [CITIES]. Show: [CITY-SPECIFIC TERRAIN + LANDMARK LIST from the Glossary geography table]. City icons are large and architecturally distinct: [CITY ICON NOTES]. Mood: [MOOD].

**Planned drill-ins** (one per session of need):

1. **The Northwest** — Tyr, Eldorado, Osgaker: Ringing Mountains, iron terraces, Walis spire, the Rim Wall pass.
2. **The Southwest** — Urik and the Black Crown: the volcano, obsidian quarries, Yaramuke ruins on the Raam road — and the **Dragon's Bowl**: an irregular, *elongated* sunken basin with a ragged thousand-foot rim (emphatically not a neat circle), 35–50 miles long, Lake Pit's blue water in the northern lobe, and one torn-looking breach in the southern rim.
3. **The South** — Balic and the dam: the two-seas harbor, the Cochlea, the southern silt-ramp, Altaruk road-fort, Mekillot Mountains + Salt View, Amber Valley.
4. **The East** — Nibenay and Gulg: the Crescent Forest between them, rice springs, the hedge-ring with **Sunlight Home**'s titanic tree rising from its center, Ogo, the **Witchgrove** deep in the forest (a darker, denser, *wrong-looking* stand of trees), Badlands behind.
5. **The North** — Draj, Blackguard, Raam: the mudflats and causeway, the silt arm, the sprawl.
