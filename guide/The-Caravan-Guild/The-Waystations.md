# First Mark, Midreach, and Saltbone

*Three towers, and why you do not miss one.*

The three waystations on [the Long Reach](The-Long-Reach.md): squat stone towers built on the firmest shoals in the open silt, and the only fixed points between Balic and Raam. Your guide has no signal out here; this page is what the Council could tell you before you left.

## The three

Listed in the order you meet them sailing north from Balic.

- **Saltbone:** the southern station, after the run up from Balic's crest. Named for what the marker crews kept digging up when they sank the foundations, and nobody has ever wanted to know more than that.
- **Midreach:** the middle of nowhere, in the middle of the crossing. A rope-yard, a cistern, a barracks, and a beacon. The crews here are the most senior and the strangest.
- **First Mark:** the northern station, a day out from Raam's shelf. The busiest of the three: the last chance to turn back, and the first chance to fix what the crossing has already broken.

## What they are

Each is the same kit: a tower with a **beacon fire** on top, a **cistern**, a **rope-yard** for repairs, and barracks for the marker crews who maintain the lane. All Guild property, all permanently staffed, all resupplied by the convoys that stop at them.

## Visitor's notes

- **A convoy that misses a waystation is a convoy in trouble.** The stations are spaced for a day's run; overshooting one means a night on the open silt.
- **Beacons are answered, always,** by anyone who can see them. This is the one rule of the silt that even smugglers keep.
- **The stations are never surprised.** Your convoy is expected before its sail clears the horizon, and the crews will not say how. Ask, and you will be told about the weather.
- **Talk to the crews.** They see everything that crosses, remember all of it, and are bored enough to tell you. They will not, however, discuss the northwest.
