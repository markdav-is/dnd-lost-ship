# The Vision Quest

*The desert gives the Will to those who go and ask. This is the going, set down for players, one page at a time.*

**How to use this:** these pages are **player-facing and spoiler-free**. They are a story you walk through. Read the page, choose, follow the link. Where a page says *the DM rolls*, the DM rolls; the dice and the numbers are on the DM's page, not here. Read [Aerun — A Primer](Aerun-A-Primer.md) first if you have not.

## What it is

Everyone born on Aerun carries some small trick of the mind. Visitors do not — not at first. Stay long enough and one may come to you on its own. Or you can go and ask.

The asking has a shape, and every village under the Rim Wall knows it:

- **A guide.** Someone who has been up and come back. They are not hard to find. They are hard to afford.
- **A crossing.** Over the wall, into the gray, to a place the guide knows.
- **The Spice.** The desert's one luxury. The desert people gather it; the families sell it. It stains the eyes blue, lengthens the years, and sharpens the Will. Up there, your guide gives you some.
- **A vision.** What you see is yours. Something comes to you in it — a beast, the desert people say, and they do not say more.
- **A gift.** You wake with something you did not have.

## Who goes

- **Visitors** with no wild talent. This is the road most walk.
- **Natives** do not go. The desert gives once, they say, and it gave them at birth. A native who goes anyway is asking for something else, and the guides charge accordingly.

## A warning

People come back changed. A thin blue ring at the edge of the eye, where there was none. A habit of looking at things a moment before they happen.

Some do not come back. The guides will tell you the number if you ask. The honest ones give small numbers.
