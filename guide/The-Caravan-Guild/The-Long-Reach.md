# The Long Reach

*Three hundred and fifty miles of gray, and the fastest crossing on Aerun.*

The Caravan Guild's marked shipping lane across the open silt, running between Balic's crest and Raam's shelf.

> **No signal on the Reach.** The Aura network does not reach beyond the Rim Wall. From the moment your skimmer leaves the quays until it reaches the far terminal, your guide will receive nothing. Read this page before you board.

## At a glance

- **The arithmetic:** five or six days by skimmer convoy, against **two to three tendays** hauling the same freight the long way round the Ring Road. Everything urgent goes this way, and pays for the privilege.
- **The traffic:** silt skimmers and the Guild's **long haulers**, the passenger-and-parcel workhorses of the lane. People, post, Spice, and light cargo ride the Reach; bulk freight goes round the ring, because nothing heavy floats on dust.
- **The markers:** the lane is staked end to end with **bone-and-obsidian channel markers**, mekillot ribs sunk upright in the dust and capped with black glass that catches the sun. In clear air the next one is always in sight.
- **The waystations:** three towers on the firmest shoals: [First Mark, Midreach, and Saltbone](The-Waystations.md).
- **Who runs it:** the Guild, entirely. Both terminals, the markers, the schedule, and the rates.

## Lane law

Simple, universal, and enforced by everyone aboard:

1. Stay between the markers.
2. Pay the rate.
3. Right of way to the loaded vessel.
4. Answer a beacon.
5. **Stop for nothing that hails you from the gray.**

### The traveler's beacon

Rule four has a price, and the Guild will happily sell it to you. Every waystation and Guild terminal stocks the **traveler's beacon**: a single-use bone-and-glass burner, about **fifty ceramic**, that when struck burns **Guild-blue**, the same color as the channel markers and the waystation lights. It is visible for miles and burns about six hours.

The fifty ceramic buys the flare and a line in the Guild's book, and the line is the real product. A beacon is **registered** to its buyer, and lighting a registered beacon binds any Guild vessel that sees it to answer. Strike one falsely, or strike one that is not in the book, and you have committed a crime of the kind that ends with an arena verdict.

Every Guild hand is taught the doctrine that keeps rules four and five from contradicting each other. Learn it before you cross:

> *A beacon is light; it never speaks. If it speaks, it is not a beacon.*

A true beacon burns blue and says nothing. Anything out on the gray that calls, waves, or hails is not a beacon, and the lane's last rule has already told you what to do about it.

## Visitor's notes

- Passage can be bought at either terminal: the silt quays on top of Balic, or Raam's shelf. It is not cheap and it is not negotiable; the Guild's rate is the rate.
- The lane holds to the eastern and southern reaches and **never bends northwest**. Ask a pilot why, and you will get a shrug and a change of subject.
- It is the quickest way across Aerun and the easiest place on the continent to vanish. Travel with a reputable skimmer convoy, and count your companions at each waystation.
