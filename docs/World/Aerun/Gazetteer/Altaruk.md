# Altaruk

A walled caravan fort on the southern Ring Road between Balic and Amber Valley. Fifteen-foot walls, five hundred paid spears, and no interest whatsoever in your business.

## At a glance

- **Size** A fort with a town inside it — a few hundred permanent, plus whoever is sheltering that night.
- **Held by** Three merchant houses jointly, Wavir chief among them. Shared ownership is deliberate: no single family can close the gates against the others.
- **Living** Tolls, stabling, water, and the mercenary contract. The garrison is professional, well paid, and bored, which is the ideal combination.

## What a visitor sees

A blunt square of wall with a single gate, a yard big enough to hold two caravans, cisterns, a smithy, and a drinking hall that never quite closes. Nothing decorative anywhere. The commander is a preserver of some reputation, which is unusual enough that people mention it.

## Traveler's notes

- Altaruk keeps an unofficial rule that wizards of the gentler tradition are welcome and the other kind is turned away at the gate. Nobody puts this in writing. Everyone knows.
- It is the safest bed on the southern road and priced accordingly.
- Giants come down out of the highland now and then to raid the fields west of here. The garrison handles it. That is the entire reason the garrison exists.
