# Alien Echoes

*Return to Frostwatch, descent encounter 3 — ambush combat.*

![Alien Echoes — the cloaker](../../.attachments/enc_cloaker.jpg)

A creature from the ship has escaped. It has been living in the mountain wilderness for days, adapting to the cold, and it is hungry.

## The creature

**Cloaker** (MM p. 41) — describe it partially coated in frost and fungal growth; its moaning carries a strange harmonic that sounds almost *mechanical*. It has been hunting the ravines below the crash site and has grown bold.

## Tactics

Attacks from above — drops from a rocky overhang onto the **rearmost passenger** of the hoversled, attaching and smothering while its moan disorients the rest of the party.

## Running it

The horrors of the ship are no longer contained; the cloaker is a harbinger. After the kill, **DC 14 Intelligence (Nature)**: the fungal growth on its body is the same russet mold from the ship — **but adapted. It is thriving in the cold.**
