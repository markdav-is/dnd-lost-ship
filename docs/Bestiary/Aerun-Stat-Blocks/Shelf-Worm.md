## Traits, actions, and reactions
| Name | Type | Text for DDB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Silt Breach | Trait | If the worm ends its burrowing movement directly beneath a creature, it erupts from the silt or sand: that creature must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone, and the worm's bite attacks against it have advantage until the end of the worm's turn. |
| Bite | Action | *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature and the attack was a critical hit or the target was prone, the target is swallowed. A swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the worm, and it takes 7 (2d6) acid damage at the start of each of the worm's turns. A swallowed creature can escape by using its action to succeed on a DC 15 Strength check, emerging prone in a space within 5 feet of the worm. |
| Tail | Action | *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage. |
