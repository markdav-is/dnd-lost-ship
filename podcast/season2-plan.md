# Wandering Aerun — Season Two plan

*Host: Westerly (the intern). Weekly. Two topics an episode — one from the wider world, one local city — and two rotating sponsors. Episodes are written a week ahead and tuned to where the table actually is; the party can be interviewed as "the Professor's outlanders."*

**Fixed format (from ep009):** season-two theme in → welcome + silt report (the public-service bit, played straight) → sting → **sponsor A** → sting → **segment 1: the world** (a chapter of the wider lore, often with a guest) → sting → **sponsor B** → sting → **segment 2: the city** (a place on the ring, told like a traveler's guide: how to get in, what to see, what to avoid) → sting → mail → outro → theme out. Reuse the ep009 theme, sting, and sponsor-read strings verbatim for cache hits.

**Standing rules.** Player-safe only — and *public*-safe: the show is an in-world broadcast, so it knows only what Aerun's docks know. Nothing about the ship, the infection, the Sandwalkers' secret, the Order of the Sere, Jak's dragon, or what is really under Eldorado. The Wanderer stays gone and unexplained unless Mark says otherwise. Westerly is out from under his shadow now: she stops quoting him, keeps only the last rule at sign-off, and by mid-season is doing her own things (a running "ask a Reckoner" bit, listener corrections she reads with relish).

## Guests and voices

- **Erleena** (three visits: 010 short, 018, 021). Her voice is cast.
- **The party**, when the table lends them, as "the outlanders who came with the Professor." Westerly never uses their names on air unless the player wants it — "the bird," "the horse," "the one with the bow." Each interview is a short segment, not the whole episode, so a no-show costs nothing: the world segment stands on its own. Voices: cast per player when it happens (Voice Design prompts on request).
- **New one-off voices** to cast as they come: a Spáhus clerk, a Gleaner field-teacher, a Scarlands sailor. The DROVER (Harl) and RECKONER (Yaxa) already exist for callbacks.
- **The Wanderer:** absent all season unless Mark decides otherwise. If he returns, it is a finale beat, not a segment.

## Working method

- One script a week, drafted from this table plus whatever the table did; Mark reviews, then a single render.
- Segment 2 lifts its facts from the Players Guide gazetteer page for that city; segment 1 from the book page and the DM lore, filtered player-safe.
- After each episode locks: fold any new canon it establishes into the wiki (the standing sync rule).
- Sponsor reads: write each once, in its category's voice, and reuse the exact string if it ever runs again.
