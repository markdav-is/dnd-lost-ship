# Caria

The green, temperate southwestern continent — Gleaners Guild country, where the 2023 campaign began: Yorrem and its harvest festival, the mill town of Oldspire, the dig called the Void where the Aura network came back on, and the drowned city of Oceana off the far coast.

Source: Andy's 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (lists *First Adventure*, *Adventuring outside of Yorrem*, *Travel Back to Yorrem*, *Preparation and Travel to Oceana*). Prep notes, not a transcript. Placement on the continent follows the [world map](../World.md).

## Getting around

- Crossing Caria on foot takes about **five days**.
- **Yorrem → Summervalley** by sea: a week. **Yorrem → Drytide** ([Taldea](Taldea.md)): a week, 24 gp a head, food included.
- **By dragon:** two days from Shimmerhill to Yorrem on Essos's back — and a level of exhaustion on landing. Nobody rides a great dragon comfortably.

## Yorrem

**Capital of the [Gleaners Guild](Factions/The-Second-Age-Guilds.md)** and the largest GG city: a walled coastal port whose offices oversee production across the world. You need a pass to leave by the gates in troubled times; a G4CE badge and a known face get you back in.

- **The end-of-harvest festival** draws people from all over Vermoon — stalls of rare finds, a **fighters' ring** (pay to go a round with a local; d6 for their level), and games of chance (**Three-Dragon Ante** and **Life and Death**). The campaign opened here: a shared dream of fighting a red dragon beside strangers, then the festival, then the [Titan Fighters'](Factions/The-Titan-Fighters.md) night raid.
- **Beds:** the **Leaky Boot** (7 cp, by the docks, you may get robbed) · the **Farmers Table** (5 sp, nice but not flashy — where the party met Erleena after hours) · the **Horn of Plenty** (8 sp, lavish, mid-city).
- **Shops:** **Rhed**, an elderly gravel-voiced aarakocra enchanter (Bottle of Breath, Eyes of Charming, Alchemy Jug, adamantine ring armor, potions of resistance) · **Ronnie**, a half-orc woman who runs the big general store · a smithy.
- **People:** **Elmor Kimem**, captain of the Gleaners' guard (G4CE), who hired the party · **[Professor Erleena Riser](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md)** at the Academy of the Fettered Mind (library: 5 gp a head) · **Granny Weatherwax**, smuggling for the Titan Fighters from the docks.
- **Outside the walls:** farmland dappled with old machines and ruins turned into places to live and work.

## The road to Oldspire

Three days from Yorrem. Somewhere along it stands a door only one man can open: above it the words **SEVENDE SONN SEVENDE** and a **seven-pointed star**. It opens for no one but [Jak](../NPCs/Jak-Bjornsson.md), the seventh son of a seventh son. Inside: four braziers for the four elements and a puzzle that wants the fifth; a gargoyle; and a chest holding an immovable rod and a fine **tattoo needle** for an eldritch tattoo.

## Oldspire

A **mill town built around a fallen Antari spire** — half of it still standing over the roofs, half lying on the ground, all of it now shops, offices, and houses. The spire was one of Aura's **communication towers**; Erleena worked with Aura and the guilds to raise a replacement ([The Aura Network](The-Aura-Network.md)).

- **The Simple Owl Inn** — 25 sp a room, no food.
- A G4CE office, and a very small Academy communications office.
- The bar where the half-orc **Zarrth** sized the party up for the Titan Fighters — and where [Eustace](../Campaign/The-Party/Eustace-Thunderbeak.md) first joined the party, meeting Shhhmeowmeow on her way back from the G4CE office.

## The Forest of Silent Boughs

Beyond Oldspire. The trees are tall enough to hide **Titans Rest**, the Titan Fighters' tree fort, in the canopy — invisible from the forest floor and found only after dusk.

## The Void

Through the forest, at the foot of the **Eroded Tips** mountains, lie two Academy dig sites — an old, picked-over one, and a **new entrance** that was the talk of everyone in the know. They call it **the Void** because nothing lives there and nothing seems ever to have: bare metal rooms, a few desks, shelves long since emptied. The "evil eye" pendant opened a locked desk; the plastic card inside opened the sealed doors; behind them stood the **server banks**, a dais, and a headband on a cord. **This is where the Aura network came back on**, after 721 years, and registered its first new administrator ([The Aura Network](The-Aura-Network.md)).

## Summervalley

A small **resort town on the southern coast** — warm beaches, fresh fish, massages, sailors who love the place. A Gleaners town too: sap, fruit, herbs and warm-climate spices are tracked and shipped from here. When the party came through it was cold, the fish were not coming in, and the leisure ships were afraid to sail.

- **Caria Sands** (2 gp, high-end rooms for every kind of humanoid) · **Ocean View** (10 sp, decent).
- North of town: farms troubled by animals, and a **gnoll den** where the stolen supplies ended up.

## The west coast and Oceana

The northwestern country has **no towns** — the locals blame the **mechanical beasts** that roam it: weathered, rusted Antari machines, some missing limbs, tearing regular furrows through the grass and knocking over trees. The damage gets worse toward the coast.

Offshore, near the **Triple Isles**, the currents run sideways to the beach and spiral to a churning center. Under it, 5,000 feet down, is **[Oceana](The-Elemental-Cities.md)** — reached by a submerged deck and an elevator that descends for a very long time. (An emergency passage also runs from the city to one of the isles.)
