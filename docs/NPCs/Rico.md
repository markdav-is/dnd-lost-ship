# Rico

Engineer and recurring fixer from the previous campaign — the hands-on counterpart to all of Vermoon's inscrutable ancient machinery. **Status: off-screen; whereabouts currently unstated.**

## History with the party

- First worked with the party at **Dry Tide**, on the car — the same job where they unknowingly met the disguised [Blue Dragon](../World/The-Dragons.md).
- Solved the **Airlantis fuel problem**: worked out how to synthesize fuel for the flying city, giving the party effectively unlimited operation of their base ([The Elemental Cities](../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md)).

### Detail from the 2023 board

From Andy's previous-campaign prep (`assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json`; see [The Red Dragon Campaign](../Campaign/The-Red-Dragon-Campaign.md)). The board uses *he* and *they* for Rico interchangeably.

- **"The young upstart"** of **Drytide**, the [Tinkerers'](../World/Factions/The-Second-Age-Guilds.md) port on [Taldea](../World/Taldea.md) — with a reputation bad enough that nobody in town would help his crew, and the other racers treated them as a target "for trying to do change and do things differently."
- **The Great Race.** The party put money into his entry: a rough, lifted truck of scrapped metal and wood on big wheels. One of them rode along to fight off the other cars. They **tied for second**; Drytide's opinion of Rico turned around overnight. He left the party early to be at the bench in the morning — "anything above failure is better than what they thought would happen."
- **The engine is a hybrid** of classic mechanics and arcane magic: **elementals** do the work, and the fuel is a magical way of *feeding the elemental*. Rico's own guess is that this is how the Antari used the Titans — one per city, wind, water, fire, earth (earth by moving tectonic plates for geothermal heat).
- **It was not entirely his idea.** "Burl" — the Blue Dragon — seeded the research, because he wanted an engine for a spacecraft. The night of the race the party caught Burl reading Rico's notes. Rico knows he had "help from Burl"; whether he knows what Burl is, the board does not say.
- **Airlantis.** Brought up to the city, he recognized its engines as close kin to his own and adapted his fuel recipe — which needs **fossilarium**, bought from Shimmerhill. The fuel lasts a long time and needs only small top-ups.
- Last noted **still building a ship at Drytide**, and now knowing the right fuel.

## Using Rico now

- The natural NPC to call when the party wants **Airlantis airborne again**, a teleporter coaxed back to life, or salvaged ship technology reverse-engineered — a friendlier, healthier engineering resource than the ship's dying scientists. If **Road 1** (the purge) is ever walked, Rico is who fuels, arms, and captains the city south ([The Three Roads](../Adventures/The-Three-Roads.md)).
- Where has Rico been during Jak's two-year ascendancy? An engineer who understands the ancients' infrastructure is exactly who the Administration — or the [merchant alliance](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Merchant-Alliance.md) — would want on payroll. Which side got there first is an open hook.
