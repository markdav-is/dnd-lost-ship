# The Dragon's Bowl

![The Road of Kings country](../../../.attachments/darksun_road_of_kings_4e.jpg)

A vast sunken basin in the barrens some thirty miles north of Urik — not a valley so much as a country that has dropped. Three busy caravan roads pass within sight of its rim, and almost nobody goes down.

## At a glance

- **Size** Thirty-five to fifty miles on its long axis; the floor is a thousand-plus square miles. It is genuinely large enough to get lost inside.
- **The descent** However you enter, you go down a thousand feet of steep, treacherous slope that tends to end in sheer rock. There is no easy way in.
- **What's in it** [Lake Pit](Lake-Pit.md) in the northern lobe — the largest true water on Aerun — and a great deal of untouched flora and fauna, which tells you how rarely anyone comes.
- **The feeling** This is the part travelers mention. Entering the Bowl produces an unearned, overwhelming sense of awe and smallness. Hardened caravan guards describe finding themselves on their knees without deciding to kneel. It passes. It is not remembered fondly.

## The story

Every account agrees: the Bowl was made when **the first great dragon was born, tearing itself out of the living rock.** Nobody claims to have witnessed this. Everybody tells it the same way.

## Traveler's notes

- Despite lying between three trade roads, the Bowl is silent and empty. Caravans camp on the rim and do not discuss why they prefer it.
- The whole region is under the protection of a druid — a mul named **Enola** — and she is not a rumor. Travelers who befoul the water meet her. Travelers who behave sometimes get help they did not expect.
- One stretch of the southern rim looks broken rather than eroded, as though something came out. Guides do not point it out and change the subject if you do.
