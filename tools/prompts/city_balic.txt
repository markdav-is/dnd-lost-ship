City map of BALIC — the City of Two Seas.

Atlas context: the south coast, directly at the foot of the Balic Saddle — the broad low ramp where the silt plateau comes down toward the sea (see the atlas: the gray descends a wide chute between cliff shoulders and stops just above the city). Ocean and its harbor to the south; the ramp and gray silt rising to the north; Altaruk and the Mekillot Mountains east along the Ring Road; arid tan scrub with irrigated groves inland. The Ring Road arrives from Urik in the west and departs east toward Altaruk.

One-line touchstone: Minas Tirith built into the Rim Wall, with a sea of gray dust on top instead of a white tower. The city climbs the saddle's rock face in concentric walled tiers, each ~100 ft above the last, divided by a great stone buttress, linked by switchback ramp-roads and tunnels — but the summit is not a citadel: it is the silt quay deck, schooners moored on the gray above the rooftops, where the Long Reach lane comes in off the silt. Two waterfronts on opposite faces: the south face opens on the blue ocean harbor — stone quays, breakwater lighthouses, sailing ships, the busiest port on the map set; the north side is the dam, the rock barrier holding the silt ramp back, its face terraced with the city. Two portage systems to draw: the Cochlea — expose a cutaway or great open shaft-mouths of a colossal spiral porter-stair turning down through the dam's interior (Silo-style: one endless helix, market landings at each revolution, lamplight in the bores, a double file of porters ascending and descending) — and the exterior Stair of Sails crane galleries with silt-ballast hoist skips for heavy freight. Fill every stair with life at human scale: porter files under chitin back-frames, pack-crodlu strings, stalls on the landings. Precincts to label: Guilders' (mark within it the Gleaners' Chapter Hall, the G4CE Office, and the colonnaded Academy of the Fettered Mind), Arena, Harbor (ocean side), Silt Quarter (dam-crest side), Market, Villa, around the central megalitheum; the agora ringed by the Elven Market at the base of the old dictator's rocky fortress (empty now — the Chamber of Patricians meets there). Theaters everywhere, ramshackle to amphitheater — name the Thespian House (Market Precinct) and the Olive Tree inn on the Road of Legions. Land approach only from the west, through olive groves, vineyards, and grain farms with legion field-camps among the crops. Wealthiest city (~24,000): make it gleam. The visual: a city carved into a dam holding back a sea of dust, its heart a turning screw of stairs.
