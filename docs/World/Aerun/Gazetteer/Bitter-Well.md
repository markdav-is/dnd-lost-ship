# Bitter Well

An oasis on the western scrub plains, inland of Hollowstorm. The water is cool and genuinely sweet. The name comes from the people who dug it.

## At a glance

- **The find** For generations, caravan drivers crossing this plain could *hear* running water and never locate it. A band of dwarves finally sank a shaft through a thin shelf of rock and found an underground stream beneath — the rock had been amplifying the sound like a drum-skin.
- **The disappointment** The stream turned out to be small. The dwarves had planned to sell water to caravans and grow rich; instead they got enough for their own families and a name that records their feelings about it.
- **The catch** A typical caravan can drain the well dry, and it takes up to **six days** to refill enough to serve another.

## Traveler's notes

- **Do not plan your crossing around Bitter Well.** There is roughly an even chance the caravan ahead of you has emptied it.
- The dwarven families still hold the shaft and charge modestly. They are civil, weary, and have heard every joke about the name.
- Camping here is safe and pleasant, which on this coast is worth more than the water.
