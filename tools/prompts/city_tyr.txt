City map of TYR — the Iron City.

Atlas context: far northwest, cradled in the green foothills of the snow-capped Ringing Mountains, which fill the map's western edge. The Ring Road arrives from the southwest coast and departs northeast toward Draj. The rim cliffs and the gray silt lie east/southeast beyond the town of Eldorado at its pass — visible as a distant wall, not adjacent. Green, watered country: this is the wettest city on Aerun.

Double-ring plan: an inner walled precinct holding the Golden Tower, High Bureaus, King's Gardens, and Bureau District; an outer ring holding the Artisan District, Sculptor's Square, the Warrens with the Elven Market inside them, Noble, Merchant, and Caravan Districts, Iron Square, the Gladiatorial Stadium, the Brickyards, and the Slave Pits. At the heart, the great stepped Ziggurat — sealed since the champion's assassination. Gates: the Grand Gate (west), Stadium Gate, Caravan Gate. Behind the city, mountain slopes with the iron mine approaches climbing away and three guard posts on the mine road. Terraced gardens and irrigated fields on the lower slopes. ~15,000 inside the walls.
