## Party-level DM notes

- **The party can fly** (Eustace natively; others via magic) — encounter design in the source modules explicitly accounts for this (e.g., the Sky Hunters ridge fight).
- Level range across the recent modules: written for five ~10th-level characters; the sheets show 8–9.
- **Session-note aliases:** "Musty Joe" = Musty-Jo Mustang; "Beyonce" = Beyond-Ce.
- Andy (Beyond-Ce's player) is the keeper of Aerun lore — the **[ANDY LORE]** gaps in the desert-continent material are his to fill. See [The Green Age](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age).
