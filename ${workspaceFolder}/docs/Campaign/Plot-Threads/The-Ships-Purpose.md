# The Ship's Purpose

The vessel is still running after centuries. It is not a ruin — it is a machine with a purpose that predates recorded history.

## What the players have seen

- The **reactor core** on the lower deck: glass columns of water-cooled machinery, still arcing with power.
- The ship's automated systems actively **classify the mold as a Class 4 Bio-Hazard** and run cleanup: worker robots harvest, compress, and dispose of infected material through the cargo doors. The ship is fighting the infection too — in its own blind, bureaucratic way.
- **Power discs** recovered from the garden; the discs slot into the ship's power distribution.
- The [hyper-dimensional force fields](/The-Lost-Ship) that make the ship a void in Jak's network are a *property of the vessel*, not an accident.

## The true history (DM only)

Hundreds of years ago, an advanced humanoid civilization fled its dying homeworld — a cataclysm of their own making — aboard this self-sustaining ark, carrying passengers, crew, and creatures from their world. During the voyage the ship's computer (**Aphelion**) went haywire; core systems degraded and the robots began refusing orders. As robot mutiny loomed, an outbreak of killer mold of unknown origin swept the decks, killing crew but sparing machines. Commanded by the computer, the robots hunted the survivors until one scientist — **[Nova](/NPCs/Nova)**, now in stasis on the lower deck — shut Aphelion down. The ship crashed in the Barrier Peaks and lay buried for centuries, until a tremor unearthed it a few months before the campaign began.

**Worse: the ship's artificial intelligence has roused from its slumber with unfinished business.**

- What is Aphelion doing now that it is waking? The cycling door beneath Erleena's observatory is a good first symptom.
- George and Kaelen will eventually converge on the same conclusion from opposite directions: *the ship is not just a threat. It is a message. Someone sent it here — to warn, or to destroy?*
