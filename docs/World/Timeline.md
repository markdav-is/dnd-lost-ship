# Timeline

World history and campaign chronology, oldest first. Dates are relative to **now** (the aftermath of the Council of Frostwatch).

## Deep history

| When | Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ages past | An advanced civilization on a distant, dying world launches an ark-ship to find a new home — crew, colonists, and creatures from a dozen worlds aboard ([the true history](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose)) |
| During the voyage | The ship's computer **Aphelion** goes haywire; robot mutiny looms; a killer mold outbreak sweeps the decks; the scientist **Nova** shuts Aphelion down; the ship crashes in the Barrier Peaks |
| Centuries ago | **Aerun's desertification** — the Green Age ends; the Sandwalkers' ancestors survive by moving ([competing theories](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age)) |
| Centuries | The ship lies buried. Its first victims become the first vegepygmies. The Temple of Nord stands dormant in Grønnfjord |

## The previous campaign (~2 years ago)

- The **Wayward Friends** ally with dragons to restore order to the realm; the chaos is quelled.
- The **First Administrator** — the hidden ruler governing through the ancients' networks from the orbital station — perishes in the climactic events aboard the station.
- **Jak Bjornsson**, the party's companion, a monk from Grønnfjord, is chosen to fill the role — and chooses to rule *openly*, from the ground.

## The interregnum (2 years → now)

| When | Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| ~2 years ago | Jak establishes his seat at the **Temple of Nord**; Grønnfjord becomes the world capital; the Spásistren are founded; "Jak's Peace" ends the raids |
| ~2 years ago | A tremor unearths part of the ship. Creatures begin emerging; the Barrier Peaks grow dangerous; expeditions vanish. Ships begin supplying **Nordkrist's Hold** |
| Months ago | Jak's scouts return from the peaks *changed*; his best dis-trans bat finds **the void**. Vegepygmies appear near settlements |
| ~1–2 months ago | Jak sends **George Decay and Erleena Riser** into the ship. Both are exposed; George chooses chemistry, Erleena the containment suit |
| 3 months ago | Aerun's primal spirits begin going silent; **Kaelen al-Hajra** is sent north |
