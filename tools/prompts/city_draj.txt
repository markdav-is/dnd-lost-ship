City map of DRAJ — the Mudflat City.

Atlas context: the north coast, at the foot of the plateau's northern cliffs. Ocean to the north; a broad brown mudflat apron all around the city; the gray silt sits high atop the cliffs to the south, far above and out of reach. The Ring Road runs east–west along the firm coastal ground inland of the mud.

Warrior-city on a mudflat island at sea level. The causeway: one low stone road running from firm ground where the Ring Road passes, across a few miles of soft impassable mudflat, to the Golden Moon Gate — the city's only land connection and its whole defense (any army leaving it bogs down). It is a road over mud, at coastal level: it must not extend into the gray silt and must not climb the cliffs. Dense walled grid of clan compounds; at the center the walled Two Moon City precinct with the Palace of Glorious Conquest (a 200-ft stepped pyramid), the Arena, the Moon Houses of Kal and Guthay (the moon priests' bureau), the Gleaners' Seed Hall, the Royal Menagerie, and the House Tsalaxa Compound. Beyond the walls: the Fields of Plenty — irrigation ditches, grain and hemp — with reed marsh and mud pools where the flats meet the cliff foot. ~18,000 people.
