# Chapter 2b: The Hooded Runaway

*Salt View is full of people who left their previous employment without notice. He left the Guild.*

He sleeps in the prop-loft behind the theater, among painted flats of cities he has actually seen. The hood is up. It stays up. When he turns his head toward you there is, under the cloth, a blue so deep it has no white around it.

He was a navigator of the Silt Academy. Graduates are not released. He released himself, and the Guild has a price on him that Salt View finds more entertaining than profitable.

He does not explain why he helps people go up. You suspect it is the only thing he is still allowed to be good at.

> "I will take you. I will not talk on the way. If you talk, I will turn around."

## The test

He walks you out onto the salt flat below the town at noon. The white hours, when everyone on Aerun lies still. The glare comes up off the flat like a second sun.

He walks. You walk behind him. You do not speak. He does not look back to see if you are there; he knows. An hour out, two hours, and the flat has no edges anymore and the only thing in the world is the hood ahead of you, moving.

Then he stops, and turns, and you are still there, or you are not.

*The DM rolls for the flat. Losing your footing is not losing the test. Speaking is.*

## The price

He puts a thing in your hand: a tube of bone, a hand long, sealed at both ends with black wax. It weighs almost nothing.

> "When you have what you went for, carry this to a waystation on the Long Reach. Any waystation. Give it to the water-master. You never open it and you never ask me what is in it, now or after. That is the price."

He waits to see if you will ask anyway. People do.

## Choose

- **[Accept the price](3-The-Wall.md)** — put the tube away, and go up.
- **[Refuse and go back](1-The-Village.md)** — hand it back, and walk down into the town.
