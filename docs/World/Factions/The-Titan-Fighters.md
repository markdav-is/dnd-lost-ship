# The Titan Fighters

The "bandits" of the 2023 campaign's first act — a secret society sworn to protect the Titans, hunting Antari ruins on every continent, and quietly founded by the Blue Dragon for reasons of his own.

Source: Andy's 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (cards *The Mysterious Key*, *Aftermath of the Attack*, *Oldspire Town*, *Journey to the Bandit Hideout*, *SHIMMERHILL*, *Adventure Under the Mountain*, *What Happened to Dildro*). Prep notes, not a transcript.

## What they looked like from outside

- **The Yorrem attack.** On the night of the harvest festival they hit the city in coordinated raids — guards and bystanders killed — to seize an artifact from a festival vendor (the same thing [Gobbledegook](../../Campaign/The-Party/Gobbledegook.md) had been sent to get). They got away with the vendor; the vendor threw the party an ornate **"evil eye" pendant** on the way out — three concentric rings of segmented squares, blue outside, yellow within, black at the center.
- **Wanted.** The Gleaners' guard ([G4CE](The-Second-Age-Guilds.md)) put a bounty on them; wanted posters followed the party as far as Drytide.
- **The mark.** An ornate **bandana — yellow and gold triangles on red**. Wearing one is how the party got past them more than once.
- **Smuggling.** A dockside storage front in Yorrem with a hidden door to the cellar; supplies moved in and out of the city with **Granny Weatherwax**. Shipments noted going to Yorrem, Wildfield, Hollowstorm and Fairview.

## What they actually are

- **Protectors of the Titans** — and thereby of the great dragons. Their password under Shimmerhill is **"Kaia,"** the Earth Titan's name.
- They interrogated Dr. Methuselah Nye about the evil eye, the dig, and the **mythical cities**; they map dig sites and try to get into them; they collect Antari electronics and work on them themselves. They are doing, with less access, what the party ended up doing.
- **Founded by Dildro.** Per the board's last entries, the Blue Dragon — in his human guise as **Burl** — created the Titan Fighters: a group that would work for the protection of the Titans and his fellow dragons, and that "could also get out and better search for the ruins of the Antari" he needed for his way off the planet ([The Dragons](../The-Dragons.md)). In his parts list for a spacecraft, the Titan Fighters are the line marked **"Electronics."**

## Known bases

| Base | Where | Notes |

| **The mountain base** | Under the Bolt Mountains, on the edge of buried [Dirtroit](../The-Elemental-Cities.md) | A large Antari facility, guarded by casters projecting illusory aboleths. Houses the **battery room** the party needed to reconnect |

## Named members

- **Zarrth** (also *Zarath*) — half-orc, met in the Oldspire bar; questioned the party's motives and could either lead them into Titans Rest or wait for them at the bottom of the fort.
- **Kald Yangeon** — leader of the mountain base. Noticed the party sneaking through and let them be, to see what they would do.
- **"The Bard"** — a Titan Fighter leader on Taldea who paid the party for fixing the power problem and wanted Jak to stay on. Jak's refusal earned him a **Titan Fighters sigil bearing the Bard's insignia**, to show other Fighters he works with them.

## A lever the party left behind

**Esmerelda Underchin** of the Gleaners in Shimmerhill offered 200 gp for everything the party found under the mountain *including the base's location* — or 100 gp if they held the location back and protected the Fighters. Which one they took is not on the board; ask the table.

## Now

Unwritten. Their founder went to the stars; the Titans they protect still sleep under the cities; and the man who holds their sigil is the Administrator. Andy's unanswered planning question stands: *how did the Titan Fighters come to be, and do the factions even know about the Titans' predicament?*
