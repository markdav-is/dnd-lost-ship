City map of URIK — the City of Lions.

Atlas context: the southwest, on open arid tan barrens, with the smoking Mountain of the Black Crown looming immediately north of the city and the southern end of the Ringing Mountains beyond it. The Ring Road passes the walls east–west (Hollowstorm northwest, Balic southeast). The rim cliffs and gray silt stand off to the northeast, distant. Yaramuke's ruins and the Dragon's Bowl lie north up the Road of Kings — off-map, but the road to them should leave the north gate.

The anti-Balic: rigid rectilinear military grid, the only square city in the series. Massive yellow-glazed walls with lion reliefs and banner-poles; walled internal quarters with checkpoint gates between them — the city is compartmentalized like a fortress. At center, the citadel: a full square mile of palace, the Code's administration halls, drill fields, barracks, and armory — a fortress inside the fortress, home of the thousand half-giant Imperial Guard. Quarters to label: Potters' Square (kilns; the city's artistic glory), the Obsidian Quarter (workshops, deliberately identical buildings — draw the uniformity), the Old District (the one irregular, characterful quarter), and the Destrier's Kilns. Gates open onto sealed roads with guard posts — nothing enters Urik unobserved; the Ring Road passes through a controlled caravan plaza outside the walls. Behind everything, the Black Crown smoking, with obsidian quarry terraces scarred into its flanks and a guarded slave road climbing to them. Outside the walls: irrigated plantations and vast kank herds on the tan plain. Observatory towers welcome — Urik is famed for its astronomers. ~20,000 people, all exactly where Hamanu's Code says they should be. Make it feel disciplined, prosperous, and airless.
