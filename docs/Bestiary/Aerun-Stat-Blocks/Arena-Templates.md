# Arena-Bred & Sunwarped (templates) — D&D Beyond Homebrew
*The two modifier templates from the Aerun stat blocks — what the pits breed, and what defiling leaves behind.*

**How to use:** these are templates, not monsters. In D&D Beyond, apply one by duplicating an existing homebrew monster (Collections → your monster → Duplicate) and editing the fields listed below. Adjust the Challenge Rating field last, and let DDB recompute XP.

## Arena-Bred
Apply to any beast or monstrosity. Its scarring is cosmetic — the changes are these:

| DDB field to edit | Modification |
| :--- | :--- |
| Hit Points (average and dice) | +20% HP (add hit dice to match) |
| Saving Throws / Traits | Add trait: **Trained Nerve.** The creature has advantage on saving throws against being frightened. |
| Traits | Add trait: **Crowd Sense.** While at least 20 creatures can see the creature, it gains +2 to hit. |
| Traits | Add trait: **Trained to a Bell.** The creature was raised to a signal (Wisdom (Animal Handling) DC 15 to learn it); sounding the signal can force one round of restraint. |
| Challenge Rating | +1 |

## Sunwarped
Apply to any beast. What defiling leaves behind:

| DDB field to edit | Modification |
| :--- | :--- |
| INT | Drops to `1 (-5)` if higher |
| Actions | Every natural attack deals an extra 3 (1d6) necrotic damage |
| Traits | Add trait: **Ash Aura.** Natural plants within 10 feet of the creature wither over the course of an hour. |
| Damage Vulnerabilities | Add: radiant |
| Challenge Rating | +0 to +1 |
