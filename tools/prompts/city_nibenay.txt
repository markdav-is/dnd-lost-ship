City map of NIBENAY — the City of Bubbling Springs.

Atlas context: the east coast, between the rust-red Badlands rising to its north and west and the northern tip of the Crescent Forest to its south. The cliff wall and gray silt stand beyond the Badlands to the west; ocean to the east. The Ring Road runs north–south through the city, from Raam down toward Gulg.

Ancient, decadent, relief-carved city built atop hot springs. At its heart the walled forbidden sub-city (Royal Palace / Naggaramakam), enterable only by the merchant family wives. Districts: Western District, Sages' District with Sages' Square and the Academy of the Free Purpose, the Hill District, and the Reservoir District around the great City Reservoir. Feature the Sun Garden (the Gleaners' walled, terraced teaching garden), the Serpent Towers, Palm Court, the Hall of Trade, the Elven Market, the Arena, and a Necropolis on the hill outside. Spring-fed channels thread the streets; rice paddies terrace the forest-facing (southern) side; agafari timber yards by the Mekillot Gate where the logging roads come in from the forest. Every building carved with stone reliefs. ~24,000 people.
