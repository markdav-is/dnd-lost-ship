# The Mekillot Mountains

An isolated range near the south coast, standing alone between Balic's country and the eastern forest. Small by the standards of the Ringing Mountains, and considerably more trouble.

## At a glance

- **The name** From a distance the range resembles the humped shell of a mekillot — the enormous beasts that haul the Guild's argosies. Nobody who has seen both disputes it.
- **Size** A short range, crossable in a day or two by the passes, which is precisely why anyone bothers.
- **Who's there** [Salt View](Salt-View.md) on the eastern face — an ex-slave village of raiders and actors — plus herders in the foothills and whatever is using the caves this season.

## Traveler's notes

- Caravans on the Gulg and Nibenay roads pass close to these hills and are robbed here more often than anywhere else in the south. The robbing is usually professional and rarely lethal; Salt View prefers cargo to corpses.
- The passes hold water later into the dry season than the surrounding plain, which is why the routes go this way at all.
- Hiring an escort out of Salt View costs about what losing your cargo would, and comes with better theater.
