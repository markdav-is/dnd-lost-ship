# The Antari

"The ancients." "The Fallen." The human civilization that arrived on Vermoon in a broken-down starship, enslaved the Titans to power cities of metal, and was buried by the Red Dragon roughly **720 years ago**. Everything the wiki calls *ancient technology* — Aura, the teleporters, the elemental cities, the laser pistols — is theirs.

Source: Andy's 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (cards *Ancients/Antari*, *Dragon Lore*, *Titans*, *Space Station*). These are **prep notes** — what Andy planned, which is not always what reached the table. Where they disagree with his later interview, the disagreement is flagged rather than settled.

## Who they were

- A group of **noble humans** from a world consumed by large-scale war. They gambled on a starship with no known destination to make a better life somewhere else. **Theirs was not the first Antari ship to reach Vermoon** — a terraforming ark had [crashed in the Barrier Peaks](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose.md) long before (see *Why it matters now*). This page follows the refugees.
- The refugee ship **barely made it to Vermoon**. The [Titans](The-Titans.md) and [dragons](The-Dragons.md) allowed them to land and stay; without that kindness they would have died in space.
- What they brought was mixed. The good: science, medicine, a talent for working with others. The bad: their own gods, ambitions, and needs beyond what anyone else needed — **scarcity and demand**, and then fights over land.

## How it went wrong

1. **Settling in.** Given a second chance, they made themselves comfortable — and, seeing themselves as smarter than every other people on the planet, fell back into taking what they wanted and expanding past what they had been given.
2. **The contagion of ambition.** Their drive spread "like a plague" to the other peoples of Vermoon. Some drove it out and kept their cultures; others followed the humans.
3. **Asked to leave.** The Titans and dragons intervened again and again to keep the peace. Finally the Antari were told to go — but leaving meant harvesting *more* resources to build another ship.
4. **The argument that won the war.** The Antari's case to the peoples of Vermoon: all life on this planet *could not* have been made by the Titans — **the Titans lied to you.** It swayed enough people to their side. (This is the root of the captor theory on [The Titans](The-Titans.md), and it is what later turned the Blue Dragon.)
5. **The enslavement.** They realized the Titans could *power* things. Starting with the Fire Titan **Savita** — held hostage so the others would not retaliate — they enslaved the Titans one by one, using their science to pull power out without killing them. The dragons, whose power was tied to the Titans, were weakened with them.
6. **The cities of metal.** On Titan power they built across the land, in the sky, and in deep water: [Dirtroit, Eldorado, Airlantis, Oceana](The-Elemental-Cities.md) — and the orbital **Eros Station**.
7. **The Red Dragon.** Seeing where it led, the Red Dragon sealed the other great dragons' power into colored **orbs**, placed them around the world, and set them off. The catastrophe depended on the magic in each orb. The Antari way of life ended. ([The Dragons](The-Dragons.md); [Timeline](Timeline.md).)

## What they left

- **Ruins everywhere** — dotted across the landscape of every continent. Word of a newly found cache or vault sends adventurers running; Andy's notes call the Second Age's relationship with the ruins "glory or ruin again."
- **Aura** and her server banks, the communication towers, the portals, the repair drones, the automatons — see [The Aura Network](The-Aura-Network.md).
- **A written language** almost nobody can read. [Erleena Riser](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) partly deciphered it; [Jak](../NPCs/Jak-Bjornsson.md) reads it through his implant.
- **A sigil** — found by Shhhmeowmeow, identified by Erleena as the mark of the Antari; it appears on their maps and equipment. **Badges** in that style still make the automatons stand down (Burl used one in Oceana: *"They are with me."*).
- **Deep Scions** and other servants out of Oceana, noted as still keeping tabs on the party on the Antari's behalf.

## The sleepers

The Antari are not all dead. On **Eros Station** — the orbital station, reached by portal — a population lies in **stasis pods**, frozen as they were over 700 years ago. Their journals (in the station's east-side labs) record the landing, how they integrated with the locals, the routes the Titans walked, their plans to take the world, the war with the Vermoonians, their destruction at the Red Dragon's hands — and the last-ditch plan: **sleep until they can come back, retake the world, and combine the Titans into a super-weapon to use against their enemies.**

One sleeper woke when the party restored power: **the Administrator**, who took Aura's permissions away from Jak and Erleena and began using the cities to take the planet back. That is the First Administrator of the [Timeline](Timeline.md).

> **Flagged — confirm with Andy.** The board names the Administrator twice, differently: **Eriska Hargitay** (2025-07-29) and **Bernadette Cranston** (2025-08-19), and makes her an Antari **human**. Andy's later interview remembers the First Administrator as **elvish, with strange summoning abilities**. The wiki's other pages still carry the interview version; nothing here overrides it until Andy says which is right.

## Why it matters now

- **Two ships (canon, 2026-09-21 — Mark's, not from Andy's board).** Two Antari ships reached Vermoon, a long time apart. **The first was a terraforming ark**, which **crashed in the Barrier Peaks long before the refugees arrived** and is [the Lost Ship](../The-Lost-Ship.md) of the current campaign ([The Ship's Purpose](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose.md)). The second is the refugee ship of this page. Neither knows about the other: the ark's mind (Eddie) and its one surviving crew member, **[Nova](../NPCs/Nova.md)**, went dark before the refugees came, and nothing in Andy's notes suggests the refugees ever found the wreck. Nova is Antari — the only one awake on Vermoon, older than the sleepers, and no part of their plan. The ship's "alien" key cards, laser weapons, stasis tanks, and ship-mind are an **earlier generation** of the technology behind Aura and the cities. [Erleena](../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) recognized it long ago; it seemed too obvious to mention, and she will explain it if anyone asks.
- **The terraformer and "the Titans lied" (open hook).** The refugees' case against the Titans was that they could not have made all life on Vermoon. A terraforming ship that got here first sits right beside that claim. Nothing is decided — whether the ark ever did its work, or who made what — and it should stay that way until the table forces it ([The Titans](The-Titans.md)).
- **Whatever is still asleep on Eros Station** after the Administrator's fall is unrecorded. The board ends mid-fight.
