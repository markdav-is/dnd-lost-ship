# The Balic Saddle

*The one gap in the wall, and the reason Balic exists.*

The Balic Saddle is the broad low notch in the southern Rim Wall where the highland finally comes down. The silt, finding a way downhill, spills through it in a wide gray ramp that stops dead against the rock [Balic](Balic.md) is built into.

## At a glance

- **Height:** a few hundred feet, where the rest of the wall stands thousands. This is the gap.
- **What it means:** it is the reason Balic exists, the reason Balic is rich, and the reason everything the south trades goes through one city's tolls.
- **The ramp:** gray silt descends the saddle in a broad chute between cliff shoulders and comes to rest above the city, held there by the rock, with the silt quays and moored skimmers at the top of it.
- **The crossing:** cargo goes up and over on human backs, by the crane galleries of the **Stair of Sails** on the outside and the **Cochlea**, the spiral stair through the rock, on the inside.

## Visitor's notes

- Stand in Balic's ocean harbor and look up: you will see the masts of silt schooners along the skyline. It is the strangest sight on the continent, and the locals have stopped noticing it.
- Silt giants occasionally come *down* the ramp to raid the fields. The legions handle it, the fields get replanted, and nobody moves away.
- The saddle is why the southern road exists at all. Cut it, and the entire south would have to go the long way round.
- Above the quays you are beyond the Rim Wall, and your guide will lose its signal. See [The Sea of Silt](../The-Sea-of-Silt.md).
