# Russet Mold & Infection

The campaign's signature hazard. Golden-brown/rust-red mold broadcast from the [Lighthouse](../The-Lost-Ship/The-Lighthouse.md); infected creatures transform into vegepygmies after death. Two rule sets are in play — patch contact (dungeon hazard) and systemic infection (campaign clock).

## Mold patches (contact hazard)

- **3-foot kill radius.** Within it: save vs. the mold (DC 15 CON as converted) or become a mold culture — death in 2–5 turns, and a **vegepygmy rises from the body in 21–24 hours**. On a successful save: 5d4 damage.
- Destroyed by: **alkaline solutions, defoliant spray, fire**. Lasers are too focused; explosive/incendiary grenades work.
- Corpse protocol (per George): burn or dissolve the dead. *There is no other way.*

## Systemic infection (bites, spores, exposure)

**Exposure:** breathing spore clouds or wounds from infected creatures → **DC 16 Constitution saving throw** (encounter-specific DCs 12–16 appear throughout the modules; use the listed DC for each source).

| Stage | Onset | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Latent** | 1d4 hours | No visible symptoms; the creature feels *unusually calm* |
| **Symptomatic** | +24 hours | Mild hallucinations, poor sleep, **compulsive urge to protect infected creatures** |
| **Active** | +72 hours | Visible fungal growth; disadvantage on CHA checks; begins acting in the infection's interest |
| **Full Corruption** | +1 week | Death; rises as spore servant or vegepygmy |

**Treatment ladder:**

- *Lesser restoration* — halts progression, does not reverse.
- *Greater restoration* — reverses one stage.
- **Full cure** — needs a live sample from the second source on Aerun; see *Why the cure needs Aerun*, below. Erleena's agent already works in a dish; the Aerun sample is the missing piece.
- **Stopgaps:** George's anti-fungal treatment — refined with Eddie's computational help and proven on himself; halts progression the same way *lesser restoration* does, doesn't reverse it, but he now has real doses to share, not just a stimulant that keeps him personally functional. MED-1's injections, Harah's Sandwalker stabilizer (slows progression, eases pain).
- **Spore-blaster (George):** strips the active spore coat off an infected creature and breaks the Lighthouse's control for 24 hours — no Hive Awareness, no Spore Link, no direction. It does **not** halt or reverse infection; pair it with a dose. Stats on [The Counteragent](The-Counteragent.md).

## Why the cure needs Aerun

Erleena's agent stops the mold in a dish. She still can't turn it into a cure without a sample from the other source, and the reason is simple biology:

- **Every case on Vermoon comes from one source.** The Lighthouse seeded all of it, so every sample she has is a cousin of every other. With one family line she can't tell which features are the disease itself and which are just this strain adapting to this ship, this valley and this cold.
- **Aim at one strain's quirks and it mutates around you.** A drug built against the surface features of a single strain is exactly how you breed a resistant one. The strain has already drifted in a month; a narrow cure would stop working in a season.
- **Rajaat is an independent outbreak of the same thing,** a thousand years older and a continent away. Two lines that evolved separately for a thousand years will have changed everything they can afford to change. Whatever they still **share** is the part the mold can't change and survive: its anchor, the foothold that ties it to the black salient. A cure aimed there works on both strains, and on anything either one turns into.
- **It has to be taken live, at the source.** Spores that drift from their source change within days; her own dish showed it. A sample in a jar arrives as something else. So she goes herself, with her instruments, and reads it where it grows. That is why she won't send the party with a jar and a shopping list.

The Aerun source has also been **held in check for a thousand years**, which makes it a working control. It shows her what a strain looks like on her instruments when it is contained, so she will know what "cured" reads like before she tries it on George.

## Horror-mode extras (Frostwatch rules)

- **Fear checks:** WIS DC 15 when witnessing mutation or a fungal horror; frightened 1 minute on failure.
- **Secret objectives:** infected PCs receive hidden goals (e.g., "protect the fungus") — hand these out privately.
- **Symptom table:** Copper's d6 diagnostic list is on [his page](../NPCs/Copper-the-Surgeon.md).

## The bigger picture

The mold **networks**: spore-linked creatures share senses; herds act as scouts; and the behavioral changes begin *before* any visible growth — the mechanical seed of [The Invisible Infection](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Invisible-Infection.md). Destroying the Lighthouse ends the *broadcast*; what it does to the already-infected is the DM's call and the heart of the [dilemma](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Lighthouse-Dilemma.md).

*DM only:* the mold networks because it is not really a mold — it is the physical manifestation of the **Black Salient**'s negative-energy bleed — a protrusion of the Black — the Dark Sun realm Rajaat was sent to — anchored to the Lighthouse (see [The Ship's Purpose](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose.md)). Hive Awareness and Spore Link scale with the Salient's influence: the further the bleed spreads, the more the infected act as one thing with one goal. George's anti-fungals and spore-blaster don't touch the Salient itself; they deny it footholds, one host at a time — which is exactly why it is fighting back.
