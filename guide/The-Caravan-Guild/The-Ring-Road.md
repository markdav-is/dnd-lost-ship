# The Ring Road

*The oldest continuously maintained thing on Aerun, and the reason it is one place rather than seven.*

The great highway that circles the whole continent, touching every city and most of the towns: fourteen hundred miles of graded roadbed, wells, and waystops.

## At a glance

- **Length:** about 1,400 miles all the way round. A caravan making twenty to twenty-five miles a day circles the continent in **roughly two months**.
- **Between cities:** neighbors sit 100 to 150 miles apart on the eastern sweep, four to seven days of travel. Close enough to quarrel; far enough that marching an army is ruinous. The western legs are much longer.
- **The traffic:** Guild **mekillot argosies**, rolling fortress land-trains the size of villages, plus house caravans, pilgrims, couriers, herds, and everyone walking.
- **Upkeep:** shared, grudgingly, by the families whose trade depends on it. Wells and waystops are kept by whoever's territory they sit in, so quality varies enormously from one stretch to the next.

## Who works an argosy

Three sorts, and everyone aboard is Guild:

- **The crew**, who handle cargo and haul lines.
- **The drovers**, at the helm and the brakes, who drive the mekillot teams. It is a trade learned on the road.
- **The Navigators**, hooded, from the Academy, who ride openly, command the train, and keep the beasts calm.

## The circuit

Draj, Blackguard, Raam, Nibenay, Gulg, Amber Valley, Balic, Urik, Hollowstorm, Osgaker, Tyr, and back to Draj.

Eldorado sits on a short spur toward the high country. Gulg hangs off the road by a forest track, because the road does not enter the wood.

## Visitor's notes

- **Do not walk alone.** It is possible on the settled stretches and unwise everywhere else. Attach yourself to a caravan at any city gate, or at [Amber Valley](../House-Wavir/Amber-Valley.md), where hiring is done openly each dawn.
- **The road is neutral ground** by ancient custom. Feuds are not conducted on it. This holds well in the west, indifferently in the north, and not at all near the Mekillot Mountains.
- **Do not ask about the sealed hold.** They say raiders who board a great argosy always find less aboard than the manifest promised, and that every great argosy carries a sealed hold the crews will not enter and will not discuss. *An argosy is never late*, people say on the road, the way they say things they have stopped questioning.
- **There are two shortcuts, and both cross the silt:** [the Long Reach](The-Long-Reach.md) from Balic to Raam, and the shoal routes the desert tribes use and will not sell.
