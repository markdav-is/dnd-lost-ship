# Chapter 3: The Wall

*Two thousand feet of ochre rock, and above it the gray.*

The wall is not a cliff, not quite. It is columns, stacked and broken, with long fans of scree at the foot and a sheer lip at the top. Where the rim dips, silt spills over and smokes down the face in slow cascades. They look like waterfalls. They are not water.

Your guide knows a way. Every guide knows a way. There are three, and the guide tells you the cost of each and lets you choose, because the desert does not give to those who were sent.

*Whichever you take, the DM rolls. The wall takes something from most who climb it. It does not usually take the climb.*

## Choose your way up

**The night stair.** A line of ledges up a dry silt-fall channel, climbed in the cold hours by feel. Above you the fall is still smoking over the lip, a gray plume against the stars. The dust settles on you as you climb. The cold gets into the rope. You reach the rim as the sky goes pale.
*Survival. The wall in the dark.*

**The day pass.** A prospector's route, a real path, cairned, broad enough to walk. It is in full sun the whole way and there is no shade on it anywhere. You climb in the white hours because that is when the path is empty. The rock is hot enough to feel through your boots. You reach the rim with the sun still up and your water half gone.
*Constitution. The heat.*

**The silt-edge.** Not up the wall at all, but along it — a shelf where the rim steps down into benches, and the dust comes almost to the lip. You walk the firm margin between rock and gray. The guide goes first, reading the ground, and you put your feet where the guide's feet went. One step wide and the ground is not ground.
*Wisdom. Reading what the guide reads.*

## The top

All three ways come to the same place. Your guide has been here before; the guide's feet find it without looking.

A hollow of firm ground a little way out into the gray. A ring of stones somebody set, a long time ago. The dust lies flat and pale to every horizon and the wind moves over it without a sound.

Your guide sits down, and takes out a small packet, and waits for you.
