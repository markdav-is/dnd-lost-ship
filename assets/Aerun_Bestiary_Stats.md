### Arena-Bred (template)
Apply to any beast or monstrosity: **+2 AC-worth of scarring is cosmetic** — instead give +20% HP, advantage on saves vs. being frightened, and **Crowd Sense** — +2 to hit while at least 20 creatures can see it.
