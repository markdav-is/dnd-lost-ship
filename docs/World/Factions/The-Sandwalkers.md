# The Sandwalkers

The silt-shoal tribes of [Aerun's](../Aerun.md) interior — the people who watched the world die once, adapted to the cure that scarred it, and have served for a thousand years as the unchosen **wardens of Rajaat**.

## Culture

- **Witnesses, not scholars.** Oral histories stretch back to the Green Age, carried in song, scar, and the careful reading of ruins. *"The sand remembers everything. The problem is that most people don't speak sand."*
- A culture of **harsh religious discipline and survival** in the realm of capricious storms beyond the Rim Wall — entirely off the grid, invisible to the network Jak thinks is the world.
- Primal shamanism: they listen to the spirits of the land. Their most feared omen is the **Defiling Silence** — the absence of sound that precedes the death of a place. The name is not metaphor: their ancestors *heard* it when the defilers unmade the continent's green.
- They have **never accepted Aura implants** and carry no trackable technology.
- They **revere the great worms** as forces of nature that maintain the balance of the world — because, uniquely, they know that this is literally true.
- Language: Common, a Primordial desert dialect, and Sandwalker sign. They trade information across vast distances.

## The wardenship

The Sandwalkers guard **[Rajaat — the First Source](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md)**: the buried origin of the spore infection, contained for a thousand years by the quarantine-silt their ancestors made and the worm ecology that closes the loop. *"Contained only by sand and worms and the prayers of people the coastal cities have never met."* They did not choose the duty; they inherited it. A true cure — the kind the ship might yield — would give them a choice for the first time. This is Harah's quiet, personal stake in the party's success.

### The prayers are the Veil

The line about prayers is **literal**. The wardens' deep orders maintain **[the Veil](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md)** — a massed, generations-deep psionic working that hides the deeper places of the Sea of Silt from every approaching mind. Not illusion: *perception-editing*. A creature can fly directly over the First Source and see only empty gray; expeditions cross the veiled deeps and remember crossing nothing. The daily rites of the warden-orders — the chants, the fasts, the Spice sacraments — are shifts on the largest psionic working in the world. Every Sandwalker raised to the duty gives some hours of every day to *praying the Source out of the world's mind*.

This is why the wardens' Spice-fed psionic strength is not a luxury: **sight contests sight**. Any psionic mind powerful enough and suspicious enough could begin to muddy through the Veil — which is why the wardens watch the dragons, and why a certain [blue-eyed student](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/Spicy-Jak.md) will someday matter to them enormously.

**Their secrets, in rings:** the Rajaat story (now told at the Council of Frostwatch) · the defiler method and the [Order of the Sere](The-Order-of-the-Sere.md) (yielded under pressure) · the full worm ecology, **the Spice's true nature, and the Veil** (never — the world's economy runs on not knowing, and the world's safety runs on not seeing).

## In the campaign

Their emissary is **[Harah Tabr](../../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md)**, sent to track the Defiling Silence to its new, second source. If the campaign travels to Aerun, the Sandwalkers are guides, oracles, and gatekeepers of the Sea of Silt — and the only people who can broker an audience with what lives beneath it. And note the cost of Harah's mission: **every warden the Silence pulls south is a mind withdrawn from the Veil.**
