# Tomas Vel-Maret

Merchant factor and the [merchant alliance's](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Merchant-Alliance.md) agent in the south — in the Barrier Peaks for weeks under cover of a mining-rights survey. Not a villain: a pragmatist who has read how stories about information monopolies end. **Not yet introduced in play.**

## The offer he makes the party

- Political protection in the Aerun city-states, independent of Jak's favor.
- Access to the houses' resources: information networks, safe houses, financial support.
- A promise that ship technology will be **distributed rather than monopolized** (distributed among the houses, that is).
- Intelligence on what the Spásistren will do with the ship when the party's mission ends.

## His leverage — the sealed orders

Tomas holds a copy of the Spásistren's sealed orders: once the party's mission is complete, the ship is to be **sealed** — party rewarded, Jak keeping exclusive access to whatever data was extracted. The council's "three paths" were not the whole menu. This is the path nobody presented.

## Stats — Rogue 12, Mastermind (house factor)

*He plans never to draw a blade — which is why the blade he carries is excellent. Party-parity CR 7 alone; he is never alone.*

**AC** 16 (fine studded leather +1) · **HP** 84 (13d8+26) · **Speed** 30 ft · STR 10, **DEX 18**, CON 14, **INT 16**, WIS 14, **CHA 18** · **Saves** DEX +8, INT +7 · **Skills** Persuasion +12, Deception +12, Insight +10 (Insightful Manipulator: reads INT/WIS/CHA relative to his own after 1 minute), Investigation +7, Sleight of Hand +8

- **Sneak Attack 6d6** · **Rapier** (+8, 1d8+4) · **concealed dagger** (+8, 1d4+4, poisoned 1/day: DC 14 CON or 3d6 + poisoned) · **pocket laser pistol** (+8, 3d6 radiant, 10 shots — house-acquired ship salvage; producing it is a statement).
- **Master of Tactics:** Help as a bonus action, at 30 ft — in a fight he *directs*, granting advantage like a conductor.
- **Misdirection** (reaction): redirect an attack targeting him to a creature within 5 ft providing cover. His bodyguards know the terms of their employment.
- **Cunning Action**, **Evasion**, **Reliable Talent** (no social or investigative roll below 10 + modifier — the terrifying one).
- **Retinue:** two house **veterans** (MM stats) as porters-who-aren't; in the city-states, add a house templar or agent per the [Merchant Houses](../World/Factions/The-Merchant-Houses.md).
- **Gear:** impeccable papers under three names, the sealed-orders copy (never carried on his person), letters of credit good in seven cities.

## Playing him

He tells the party *true things* in service of a larger agenda — political vertigo, the Dune tone. He genuinely believes Jak's monopoly ends badly for everyone including Jak. He is not entirely trustworthy either, and he knows the party will eventually realize that; he plans for it.

**Which house holds his contract?** Open choice with flavor consequences: [Tsalaxa](../World/Factions/The-Merchant-Houses.md) makes him a spy first, Wavir a principled anti-monopolist, Vordon an ambitious empire-builder's tool. His name follows no house — deliberately.
