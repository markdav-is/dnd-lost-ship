Erleena alone in her sickbay lab fifty feet under a lake. She stands at a wall display, one hand raised to a row of instrument traces on the screen — each trace a jagged line with the same tall black spike. She is in the cream medical uniform with the orange sleeve stripe, sleeves pushed up, tired and intent. Behind her, three biobeds along the curved wall, a diagnostic table in the middle of the room, an open sliding door showing an empty corridor. In a round concave porthole on the far wall, three large glowing eyes on stalks hang in black water, watching her; smaller pale fish shapes press against the other portholes. Nobody else in the room. Bright even white light inside, black water outside.
