# Chapter 2c: The Kreen Hunter

*Something tall in the scrub that has been standing there since before you noticed it.*

The thri-kreen is seven feet of sand-colored chitin and stillness. Four arms; the upper pair holds nothing, the lower pair rests on a chatkcha, the thrown blade of the kreen. The eyes are faceted, and in each facet a small blue light.

The hunter has taken the Spice before. Not for a gift — kreen are born with theirs, and the desert gave once. *For the pack*, the hunter says, and does not explain what the pack needed or whether it is still alive. The hunter says very little. What is said is in a clicking Common with no wasted sound.

> "You go up. I go up. You keep up. That is all."

## The test

Kreen do not sleep. You knew that. You did not know what it means until the first night, when the hunter is still walking and you are still walking and the moons are down and the hunter is still walking.

A day and a night, across the scrub toward the wall. The hunter does not slow for you. The hunter does not leave you either; when you fall behind, the dark shape ahead simply stops and waits, with no impatience at all, which is worse.

*The DM rolls for the pace. Falling down is allowed. Not getting up is not.*

## The price

At the foot of the wall the hunter turns and looks at you with all of that face.

> "When you have the gift, the first time you use it, it is not for you. It is for someone else. Anyone. The pack is whoever is nearest. This is the price. I will know."

You believe it.

## Choose

- **[Accept the price](3-The-Wall.md)** — nod, and start to climb.
- **[Refuse and go back](1-The-Village.md)** — the hunter does not argue. The hunter is already walking away.
