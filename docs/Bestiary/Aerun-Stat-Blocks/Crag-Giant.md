## Traits, actions, and reactions
| Name | Type | Text for DDB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pass Toll | Trait | The giant parleys before it attacks, always. Its opening rock lands *near* you. |
| Multiattack | Action | The giant makes two greatclub attacks. |
| Greatclub | Action | *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage. |
| Rock | Action | *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. |
| Avalanche Start (1/Day) | Action | While on a slope, the giant starts an avalanche in a 60-foot line. Each creature in that line must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 33 (6d10) bludgeoning damage and is buried; on a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't buried. |
| Rock Catching | Reaction | If a rock or similar object is hurled at the giant, the giant can, with a successful DC 10 Dexterity saving throw, catch the missile and take no bludgeoning damage from it. |
