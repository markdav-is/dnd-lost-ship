# Timeline

World history and campaign chronology, oldest first. Dates are relative to **now** (the aftermath of the Council of Frostwatch). Deep-history entries incorporate Andy's world-building interview (canon).

## Deep history

| When | Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| The first age | The four Primordial **[Titans](The-Titans.md)** — Kaia, Savita, Ermir, Dwyn — shape Vermoon and its peoples and are revered as benevolent gods. They make the **[dragons](The-Dragons.md)** first, as the world's peacekeepers and their own protectors. Whether the Titans were makers or **captors who brought the people to Vermoon** is the world's buried question |
| The ark — long before the landing | The **first** Antari ship to reach Vermoon: a self-sustaining **terraforming ark** — crew, colonists, a garden biosphere, creatures from a dozen worlds ([the true history](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Ships-Purpose.md)). On the voyage its computer **Aphelion** goes haywire; robot mutiny looms; a killer mold outbreak sweeps the decks; the Antari scientist **[Nova](../NPCs/Nova.md)** shuts Aphelion down and falls into a stasis tank; **the ark crashes in the Barrier Peaks**. How long before the refugees is not set — only that neither the ship's mind nor Nova has ever heard of them |
| The landing | **[The Antari](The-Antari.md)** — noble human refugees of a war-torn world — limp in on a failing starship, the second Antari ship to reach Vermoon and unaware of the first. The Titans and dragons let them stay. They bring science, medicine, their own gods, and scarcity |
| The enslavement | Asked to leave, the Antari instead persuade much of Vermoon that **the Titans lied** about making it, and enslave the Titans for power — **Savita first**, then the rest. The dragons, tied to the Titans, are subdued. Cities of metal rise on land, in the sky, under the sea: **Dirtroit, Eldorado, Airlantis, Oceana**, and the orbital **Eros Station**, all run through **Aura** |
| *(interview)* | Andy's interview remembers a **First Administrator — an elvish leader with strange summoning abilities** — arriving at the head of a group that lands and quietly takes over, ruling through the ancients' networks. His 2023 prep makes the Administrator an Antari human sleeper instead. **Unreconciled — ask Andy** |
| The apocalypse, ~720 years ago | The **Red Dragon** seals the other great dragons' power into colored **orbs**, has them placed around the world, and detonates them. The cities fall or are buried, the infrastructure goes dark, and the age of laser pistols becomes the age of legends. The Green Dragon **Undaloth** dies placing his orb. The board ties the Antari's downfall to "the sacrifice of Savita," the Fire Titan, without saying more. A remnant of Antari go into **stasis on Eros Station**, meaning to come back. *(How the apocalypse squares with the dragons' duty to protect the Titans: per the board, it was the Red Dragon's plan to stop the people who had enslaved them)* |
| The Second Age | Out of the ashes, a society of cooperating factions governed by "the Board" — the **[Second-Age Guilds](Factions/The-Second-Age-Guilds.md)**. Antari ruins dot every landscape; their cities become myths |
| Centuries ago | **Aerun's desertification** — the Green Age ends; the Sandwalkers' ancestors survive by moving ([the theories](../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age.md), now shadowed by the Red Dragon apocalypse) |
| Centuries | The ark lies buried; its first victims become the first vegepygmies. The Temple of Nord stands dormant in Grønnfjord |

## The previous campaign (~2 years ago)

Arc by arc on **[The Red Dragon Campaign](../Campaign/The-Red-Dragon-Campaign.md)**. In brief:

- The **Prophecy of the Red Dragon** gathers the heroes with a shared dream on the eve of Yorrem's harvest festival; each is in time paired with a dragon ([The Dragons](The-Dragons.md)) and the party collects the dragon orbs.
- In the dig called **the Void** on Caria, the **[Aura network](The-Aura-Network.md)** comes back on after 721 years and registers Jak as its administrator. On Taldea the party passes buried **Dirtroit** and the sleeping Earth Titan, meets the Black Dragon, and re-seats the power orbs at **Lost Rock** — the grid, the phones, and the **teleport network** come back online.
- Restoring power also wakes **the Administrator** on the orbital station, who locks Jak and Erleena out and turns the cities' controls on the world: wrong tides, racing storms, heat from the Amber Valley.
- The party ranges worldwide — Drytide and its race, the underwater city **Oceana** (where the Green Dragon Undaloth is restored and Erleena shuts down the currents), and the falling flying city **[Airlantis](The-Elemental-Cities.md)**, which they save and keep.
- The arc resolves aboard the **orbital space station** (Eros Station): the **First Administrator** perishes; the **Blue Dragon** — Dildro, the party's string-pulling "Burl" — declares it was never meant to be kept on this world and stays aboard, bound outward. The chaos is quelled; the prophecy fulfilled; the dragons return to hibernation.
- **Jak Bjornsson**, the party's companion, a monk from Grønnfjord, is chosen as the new Administrator — and chooses to rule *openly*, from the ground.
