# Level 2 — The Theater

The ship's entertainment deck, claimed by mind flayers. **Status: explored — the party cleared it in a brutal psychic brawl.**

## What happened

Two **mind flayers** and four **intellect devourers** had taken the theater and were using the ship's technology to study the conquest of other worlds. In the fight, [Musty-Jo](/Campaign/The-Party/Musty-Jo-Mustang) was grappled, stunned, and dominated multiple times; [Beyond-Ce](/Campaign/The-Party/Beyond-Ce) took heavy psychic damage and was briefly turned against the party before breaking free. The last mind flayer dropped on stage.

## DM notes

- The drop tubes from this level require a **violet key card** to descend — the gating that pushed the party through the walkway level to find one.
- The mind flayers' research is a loose end: what did they learn about the ship, the mold, or other worlds? Their notes/apparatus could hold a revelation if the party returns. Were they also drawn by the void — the one place *their* enemies couldn't scry either?
