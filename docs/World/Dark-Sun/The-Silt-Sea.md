# Ecology of the Silt Sea

The continent's heart is a dead ocean that is *not dead at all* — it is a **managed organism**, a thousand-year-old machine of dust, spores, worms, and prayer whose product is the Spice and whose purpose is the [First Source's](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md) imprisonment. This page is the DM's systems reference: how the basin works, what lives in it, and where civilization dares to touch it.

## The basin — the dead sea on the roof of the continent

The silt fills a **highland basin** — the bed of the Green Age's rain-fed **heart-sea**, a great inland sea on the central plateau, unmade in the defiling. The real-world analog is the **Salar de Uyuni**: a vast dead sea-bed high in the mountains, flat, pale, and dry *because* it is high. Nothing flows up to it; everything it ever held has been draining out through the rock for a thousand years.

| **Rim Wall outer face** | A **~2,000-ft climb** from the ring to the crest (sheer upper cliffs over long scree slopes; higher in the south and west) |

| The arms (spill-lobes) | Stepping down through rim gaps: **Raam arm** pooled ~+1,000 ft · **Forked Tongue Estuary** ~+700 ft behind Balic's sill · **Draj's flats** at the wall's foot (seep-fed, near coastal level) |
| The Dragon's Bowl | Sunk ~1,000 ft *into* the plateau — floor around +800 ft, Lake Pit in its northern lobe |
| The deeps | Unknown. [Veiled](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md). Do not chart them; you can't |

**Silt-falls:** wind pushes the silt over the rim's low points in slow, permanent gray cascades — the arms are frozen spill-lobes stepping down through the gaps, and the smallest falls smoke off the crest wherever the wind runs. From the coastal ring you look **up** at the wall, and on a hard wind-day you can see the gray breathing over the top.

**Orientation (per the Aerun Atlas):** unlike Athas — where the silt lies east of the cities — Aerun's silt mass sits **center-north** of the continent, and the widest living band with the great ocean front runs along the **south**. The continental rule of thumb: **silt to the north, ocean to the south.** Per city: **Balic** (south coast) faces ocean south, silt north up the estuary — the rule exactly. **Raam** (northeast coast) inverts locally: ocean on its northeast face, the silt arm at its southwest back. **Draj** (north coast) is pinched between the two: ocean a few miles north behind it, its mud-shelf arm spreading south toward the deeps. **Tyr and Urik** keep the silt at their eastern backs beyond the mountains; **Nibenay and Gulg** at their western backs beyond the Badlands.

The silt itself is powder finer than flour — it **flows like water and drowns like water**. A person sinks; a mekillot sinks slower; nothing swims. Crossing means Guild **silt-crawlers**, Balic-built **silt skimmers and schooners** (psionically buoyed), giant-wading channels, or the [Sandwalkers'](../Factions/The-Sandwalkers.md) secret **shoals** — ridges of firmer sediment that shift with the seasons and are read like tides.

## Why it stays dry (the weather engine)

1. **Rain shadow.** Like the Atacama beside the Pacific: ocean winds rain out on the coastal ranges and the Rim Wall, and descend inside hot and empty. The coastal ring is green *because* the interior is dry — the mountains harvest the whole continent's rain for the coast.
2. **The haze dome.** The silt makes its own weather: the vast hot plain lofts a permanent particulate haze that suppresses rainfall outright — droplets cannot form in that much dust. Storms that crest the wall rain a brief crust of mud at the basin's edge and die. The **silt-storms** are this engine venting: dust hurricanes that rearrange the shoals and bury the unwary.
3. **The Drought (the true layer).** Water is the spore vector — the Green Age walls fell to spores in the *groundwater*. Elevation makes the quarantine self-keeping: **nothing flows up**, rain that falls drains *out* through the rock, and the worms' larval stage **sequesters** what lingers in the deeps. The dryness is the quarantine's outermost wall — and the drainage is the continent's darkest open secret: **the coastal ring's springs are the dead sea bleeding out**. Nibenay's bubbling hot springs, Tyr's deep aquifer, the seeps that make Draj's mudflats — the coast drinks the murdered sea, filtered through a thousand years of stone. (Filtered *enough* — the wardens test the springs in ways the cities never see.) **Wetting the interior at scale is a containment breach** — the wardens' driest law.

## The food chain (the containment cycle)

There is exactly one economy in the deep silt, and it runs on the enemy:

1. **Spawn** — spores rise from the First Source, far below.
2. **Mass** — with nothing living to infect, they clump into drifting masses.
3. **Bloom** — the masses detonate into violent fungal blooms across the silt surface.
4. **Consumption** — **the great worms** rise and feed on the dead blooms; nothing else on Vermoon can digest them.
5. **The Spice** — the worms' castings, harvested from the shoals: psionics, long life, blue eyes, and the fortunes of the [seven families](../Factions/The-Merchant-Houses.md).

Everything else in the silt lives on this chain's margins: **silt horrors** ambush along the arms and shallows (tentacled things that erupt under skimmers); **silt runners and floaters** scavenge bloom-edges; **cloud rays and silt drakes** ride the haze dome hunting whatever surfaces; the estuary's **giants** wade the shallows spearing what the horrors miss. Rule of thumb for encounters: **life clusters where the silt is shallow or the blooms are fresh** — the open deeps are empty, silent, and veiled.

**Rain-years are bloom-years:** the rare storm that breaches the dome supercharges the cycle. The coast experiences this only as a bumper Spice harvest and never asks why. The wardens experience it as a siege.

## The wardens' layer

The [Sandwalkers](../Factions/The-Sandwalkers.md) walk the shoals, read the currents, cull blooms that drift toward the arms, and maintain [the Veil](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md) that hides the deeps from every approaching mind. Their law, in order: nothing wet, nothing charted, nothing woken. They tolerate the Guild's crossing lanes and the harvest shoals; they forbid one place only — and no one who crosses it remembers there was anything to forbid.

## Where civilization touches the silt

**The two portage cities** — the only places the living economy and the dead sea meet at scale, both perched on the rim with a harbor on each face:

- **[Raam](../../Reference/Aerun-City-Map-Roster.md) — the northeastern portage.** Ocean harbor at sea level; the **Silt Transfer** on the spill-lobe arm **a thousand feet above the city's back**. Freight climbs the wall by **the Ascent** — three switchbacks of ledge-road — and tiers of **counterweight hoists** (obsidian counterweights, mekillot-turned windlasses) raising argosy containers in stages to the skimmer quays on the gray. The hoist-heads are Guild property; the queues at the platforms are a district unto themselves — and in a city this lawless, the hoists are the one thing *nobody* riots near.
- **[Balic](../../Reference/Aerun-City-Map-Roster.md) — the southern portage.** The city stands on **the sill**: the rock dam at the mouth of the estuary spill-channel — the one place the silt found a path downhill toward the sea, and stopped. Blue quays at sea level; the town on the ~250-ft sill crest; and behind and **above** it, the estuary's silt hanging at **~+700 ft**, held by the dam the city is built on. The **Stair of Sails** — terraced crane galleries — climbs cargo the ~700 ft from the ocean quays up to the silt quays. Nothing sails through; everything portages, and Balic taxes the seam — the richest toll on Vermoon. **The sill is the most dangerous rock on the continent:** breach it and the hanging silt comes down — a gray avalanche that flows like water, spore-laden, racing through the city toward the sea. Not a flood; a containment failure with an address. The wardens know. The Sere know. Balic's engineers pray nobody else works it out.

**The lesser touches:** **Draj's mud-shelf** (~100 ft down — shallow enough for wall-seeps to make mud; the ring's breadbasket and a standing moisture anomaly the wardens quietly watch) · the Guild's marked **crossing lanes** between the portages · **North & South Ledopolus**, the dwarves' doomed causeway across the estuary · and **Eldorado's rim pass**, where prospectors descend chasing rumors and mostly feed the horrors.

## DM systems summary

- **Travel:** Guild lane crossing = expensive and safe-ish; off-lane = shoal-reading (Survival vs. shifting routes) + horror ambushes + storm risk. The deeps: you cross them and remember nothing.
- **Encounter gradient:** arms and shallows = alive and hungry · open silt = empty and eerie · deeps = the Veil.
- **The clock:** every disturbance of the cycle — a big harvest, a rain-year, a warden shortage ([the Silence is pulling them south](../../Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-First-Source.md)) — ratchets bloom pressure. The silt sea is the campaign's second infection clock, running quietly behind the Scarlands one.
