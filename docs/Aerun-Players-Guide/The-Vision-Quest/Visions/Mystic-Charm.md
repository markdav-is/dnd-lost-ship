# Mystic Charm

*To be looked at by it is to want to stay.*

## The vision

A slot canyon. Rust-red walls close enough to touch both at once, and a strip of sky far up. The floor is soft with something pale. Silk. Sheets of it, laid over the stones like bedding, and more of it hanging in the shadows above.

You know what lives here. Everyone who walks the Badlands knows. Its silk sells well enough that people go looking for it, and that is how it eats.

You should go back. The canyon is very quiet. You do not go back.

## The beast

It is above you, and then it is around you. A [silk wyrm](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silk-wyrm): a serpent as long as the canyon is deep, its coils the color of the walls, coming down slow as a rope paid out by a careful hand. It does not crush. It *settles*. One coil across the floor in front of you, one behind, one along the wall at your shoulder, and then the head, level with yours.

It looks at you.

You have been looked at before. Not like this. The regard of the wyrm is warm and whole and entirely without hurry, and while it is on you there is nowhere else in the world you would rather be. You are aware — somewhere far back — that this is how it eats. The awareness does not help. Being seen like this is worth whatever it costs.

It does not eat you. That is the lesson. It holds the look a moment longer than it needs to, so that you can feel the edges of it — where the warmth begins, how it rests on you, what it asks and what it does not — and then it closes its eyes, and you are only in a canyon, and cold.

Your own eyes have learned something. You will not be able to unlearn it.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your guide is looking at you, and keeps looking, a moment past the point where the guide meant to stop, and then turns away with a small sharp shake of the head.

Blue at the edge of your eye.

## The gift

**Mystic Charm.** *As an action, one humanoid you can see within 90 feet must make a Charisma save or be charmed by you until the end of your next turn.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a coil settling, a head level with yours, warm.
