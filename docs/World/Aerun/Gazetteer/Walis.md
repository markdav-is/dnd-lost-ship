# Walis

A village on top of a five-hundred-foot rock spire in the northwestern foothills, up a side canyon that most travelers pass without a glance. From below it looks like the ruin of an old castle. This is on purpose.

## At a glance

- **Access** By rope. There is no path. A cargo bucket on a windlass lifts visitors — but only visitors the villagers already know. Everyone else stands at the bottom and shouts, then leaves.
- **Population** Perhaps two hundred, all related, all extremely careful.
- **The reason** Walis sits on **the only gold mine on Aerun**. This is not a secret so much as a fact nobody says loudly, since the alternative to secrecy is a siege.

## Traveler's notes

- Arriving unannounced achieves nothing. Arrive with someone Walis trusts, or do not arrive.
- The village trades gold sparingly, through intermediaries, in amounts small enough not to move a market or attract a house.
- The maps mark it. The maps also mark it wrongly, by about four miles, and the villagers have never troubled to correct anyone.

*(Cartographer's note: the atlas renders this name as "Walis." Some copies read "Walls." Both refer to the same spire.)*
