# Mind Thrust

*It teaches by reaching in.*

## The vision

An iron cage. On Aerun, where iron is cargo and nothing else, a cage of it — black bars as thick as your wrist, the sand around it dark with old stains. You are outside it. You are very glad to be outside it.

Inside is something you do not look at directly. Handlers, in the arena, wear blindfolds and use poles. You have no pole.

It turns toward you. The bars do not matter to it. The bars were never for *it*.

## The beast

A [gaj](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#gaj): the mind-eater, a thing like a beetle the size of a cart, with three long feelers that lift toward you from between the bars and do not need to touch you.

It reaches in.

There is no other way to say it. Something goes into your head that is not you, and it is *looking*, turning things over, opening what is closed, and you cannot stop it and you cannot scream because it is holding the part of you that screams. It is not cruel. A cruel thing would want something. It is only *reaching*, because that is what it does.

And while it is inside you, you feel the shape of the reach.

That is the gift, and it is an ugly one. You feel where the reach begins, in the gaj, and where it ends, in you, and the line between — a line that goes one way, and could go the other. You do not push back. You are not strong enough. But you *see* the line, and you will always see it now, and you will know how to throw something down it.

The feelers withdraw. The gaj settles. It is, you think, satisfied: it has taught.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. A headache like a nail. Your guide is sitting further away than before, and does not come closer until you have spoken.

Blue at the edge of your eye. Something in your head that was closed is not quite closed anymore.

## The gift

**Mind Thrust.** *As an action, one creature you can see within 60 feet must make an Intelligence save or take 1d6 psychic damage. The damage grows as you do.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: three feelers, lifting, between black bars.
