# Dimensional Movement

*The road folds a little under you.*

## The vision

The Ring Road. You know it at once — the packed stone, the cairns, the wall on your right hand and the sea on your left, the way it has been every day for tendays. You are riding.

You do not remember mounting. The thing under you runs on two legs, long-necked, feathered in dust, the stride of it eating the road in gulps. A courier's mount. The waystation ahead has been ahead for an hour and is no closer.

The mount does not care. It runs the way a thing runs that has never once arrived and has stopped expecting to.

The cairns go by. The same cairns. You count them. They do not add up.

## The beast

It is a [crodlu](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#crodlu), and somewhere in the second hour you realize it is running without you, and you are running beside it, and neither of you has slowed. It turns its head on that long neck and looks at you with one flat bird's eye.

Then it does something with the road.

You cannot say what. The cairn ahead is the cairn behind. The waystation that was an hour off is a minute off, then an hour, then a minute — the distance is not shrinking; the road is *pleating*, like cloth pushed across a table, and the crodlu's feet come down on the folds.

It shows you twice. The third time, your own foot finds a fold.

It is a small thing. Two strides that cost one. The crodlu does not congratulate you. It runs on, toward a waystation it will never reach, and leaves you a little further along than you were.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your legs ache as if you had walked all night, and your guide — who did not sleep — says you did not move at all.

Blue at the edge of your eye. The road is still under you somewhere.

## The gift

**Dimensional Movement.** *As an action, your speed increases by 5 feet for one minute.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a long neck turning, one eye, the road going on.
