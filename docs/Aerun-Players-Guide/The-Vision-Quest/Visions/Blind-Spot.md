# Blind Spot

*What it is to be the place an eye slides off.*

## The vision

A dune crest. Someone is standing on it.

You are thirsty and it is a long way to anything, and the figure on the crest has water — you can see the skin at its hip, dark with it — and it is waving. Not urgently. Just: *here*. *This way*.

You walk toward it. The dune is further than it looks. The figure does not get closer, and it does not get further, and it keeps waving, and at some point you understand that you have been looking at it for a long time and cannot say what its face is like.

You try. You look straight at it. Your eye goes to the sky beside it, to the sand below it, to your own feet. Back to the sky.

## The beast

It lets you see it once. A [sand bride](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#sand-bride): the beautiful stranger at the dune crest, which is not beautiful, which is not a stranger, which is a thing that hunts by being what you want to see. It does not want you, here. It is showing you the trick.

It walks down the dune to you and stands very close, and you cannot look at it. Your eye slides. You look at its shadow. You look at the place its shadow should be, and there is no shadow, and you look at the sky.

Then it puts a hand on your face, cool, and turns your head, and for one breath *you* are the thing on the crest. You feel the eye of the desert go past you like wind past a stone. Nothing stops on you. Nothing can.

It lets go. It does not wave goodbye. It was never there.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your guide looks at you and then looks slightly to the left of you, and frowns, and looks again, and finds you.

Blue at the edge of your eye. The guide does not mention it.

## The gift

**Blind Spot.** *As an action, one creature you can see within 120 feet must make a Wisdom save; on a failure you are invisible to it until the end of your next turn.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a figure on a crest, waving, with no face you can hold.
