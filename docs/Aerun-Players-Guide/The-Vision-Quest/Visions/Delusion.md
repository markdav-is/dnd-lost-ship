# Delusion

*It shows you what you dread until you learn to hold the picture yourself.*

## The vision

Water. A pool in rock, dark, still, a long way from anything. You kneel to drink.

The thing you are most afraid of is standing on the other side of the pool.

Not a monster. Yours. The face, the place, the moment — whatever it is, it is there, with the exact weight it has in the worst hour of the night, and it is looking at you with perfect patience.

You do not drink. You cannot look away. The pool does not ripple.

## The beast

After a long time you notice the lizard.

It is lying along the rock beside the pool, the length of a man, dun-colored, with a broad flat head and eyes that do not blink. An [id fiend](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#id-fiend). It haunts lonely water. It eats fear, and it is eating now, slowly, the way a thing eats that has all day.

You understand that the figure across the pool is not across the pool. It is in the lizard's mouth. The lizard is holding it up for you, like a lamp.

So you take it.

You do not know how. You reach, without hands, and take the picture out of the fiend's jaws, and hold it yourself. It is heavy. It is the heaviest thing you have ever held. And once it is in your hands and not the fiend's, it is only a picture — something that can be turned, and shown, and put down.

The id fiend watches you do this. It is not angry. It blinks, once, which it has never done, and goes back into the rock.

You put the picture face-down on the stone, and drink.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your guide is looking at something over your shoulder that is not there, and as you sit up the guide's eyes clear, and come to you, and stay.

Blue at the edge of your eye. Your mouth tastes of still water.

## The gift

**Delusion.** *As an action, one creature within 60 feet perceives a sound or a 5-foot image of your choosing that only it can perceive, for one minute.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a flat head by dark water, holding something up for you.
