# Heat & Survival

Rules for desert travel on Aerun's Athasian side — the heat-side mirror of
[Cold and Survival](Cold-and-Survival.md).

## Water rings

Each player holds a box of small blue beads — **water rings**. One ring
represents about a liter of water (a real gallon is ~3.8 L; round it to 4
rings and don't sweat the math). Rings are spent daily per traveler based on
terrain, and refilled at wells, oases, or water merchants.

| Zone | Rings/person/day | Examples |
| :--- | :-: | :--- |
| Shaded / oasis / night travel | 2 | Verdant belt, walled towns, traveling after dark |
| Normal desert | 4 | Ring Road, scrub plain, badlands |
| Hot exposure | 8 | Open dunes, salt flats, midday travel |
| Extreme | 12 | Sea of Silt, obsidian plains, a "sun's fury" event, forced march |

Running out of rings for the day means going dry — see **Heatstroke** below.

## Heatstroke

At designated points (each hour in extreme heat, each day short a ring):
**DC 13 Constitution saving throw**; characters not native to hot climates
save at **disadvantage**. Failure: the same escalating-exhaustion track as
Frostbite — accumulated failures bring exhaustion, reduced Constitution, or
collapse until the character is shaded, watered, and rested.

**Mitigation:** shade, wide-brimmed gear, veiled clothing → advantage or
reduced risk; magic (*create water*, *goodberry*) or a *Rejuvenate*d oasis →
automatic success or lower DC. Standard Grønnfjord cold-weather kit is
useless here — desert gear is its own shopping trip.

## Race-by-race: how the party's bodies handle it

The current party's races weren't built for Athas, so translate each one on
its own physiology rather than reaching for the Athasian race list wholesale.

| Character | Race | Water ring modifier | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Beyond-Ce | Human | none | Baseline — the rate the table above already assumes. |
| Eustace Thunderbeak | Aarakocra | −2 rings/day while flying; normal on the ground | Flight keeps him off the radiant heat coming up off sand/rock. No benefit if he's grounded — hollow bones don't help him walk the Sea of Silt. |
| Gobbledegook | Goblin (Small) | half rings, round up | Standard size-based ration halving applies same as food — a smaller body loses less water. |
| Musty-Jo Mustang | Centaur (Large, quadruped) | +4 rings/day | Horse physiology: a real horse drinks 5–10 gallons a day. Treat him as drinking for two travelers. His hooves also sink in loose dune sand — same mobility tax as the ship's corridors, inverted. |
| Shhhmeowmeow | Tabaxi | −1 ring/day; disadvantage on Heatstroke saves unless groomed/shaded | Feline kidneys are built for low water intake (desert-cat ancestry) — but that fur retains heat hard. Cheap to hydrate, easy to overheat. |
| Robin Wood | Variant Human | none | Baseline, same as Beyond-Ce — no species quirk to account for. As the party's cleric she can prepare *create water*, which per the Mitigation note above can auto-succeed or lower the group's Heatstroke DC when she has a slot to spare. |

Use this table as the standing ruling once the party crosses into the Aerun
desert arc; adjust per NPC/monster only if it comes up (kanks, crodlu, and
other mounts drink separately and aren't tracked against the party's rings).
