# The Lighthouse Dilemma

The central question of the campaign: the [Lighthouse](/The-Lost-Ship/The-Lighthouse) is the source of the russet mold outbreak — and possibly the key to reversing it. Destroy it, contain it, or study it?

## The three paths (as presented at the Council of Frostwatch)

| Path | Advocate | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Containment** — alchemical charges seal seven mountain passes; permanent Spásistren quarantine | Valerius (neutral broker) | Buys time only; seals in survivors; the infection eventually finds a way through. *Kaelen: "Containment was the first thing my people's ancestors tried."* |
| **The Purge** — orbital incendiary strike sterilizes the valley | Copper ("Do it."); the pragmatists | Irreversible; the ship's technology lost; the valley uninhabitable for a generation; must be certain nothing has spread beyond the blast radius. Brennan: it burns three hundred years of home. |
| **The Cure** — return to the ship, go deeper, find the medical/research levels, synthesize a cure or trigger decontamination | Erleena, Brennan; *the path Aerun never had* (Kaelen) | Slowest and most dangerous; gives the infection time; risks the only people who have come back out. |

## Who wants what

- **[Jak](/NPCs/Jak-Bjornsson):** stop the threat to the southern continent — the cure can wait, the world cannot.
- **The scientists ([Erleena](/NPCs/Erleena-Riser), [George](/NPCs/George-Decay)):** preserve and study; destroying the Lighthouse destroys the cure knowledge forever.
- **The pragmatists (Copper, many Spásistren):** burn it down and be done.
- **[Kaelen](/NPCs/Kaelen-al-Hajra):** will not vote; if pressed — the cure, "because it is the only path that ends it rather than delays it."

## DM secrets

- **The fourth path nobody presented:** per [Tomas Vel-Maret's](/NPCs/Tomas-Vel-Maret) copy of the sealed orders, the Spásistren's standing instruction is to **seal the ship once the party's mission ends**, giving Jak exclusive access to extracted data. Valerius's three-path council was not entirely honest.
- Every hour the Lighthouse operates, the region's infection clock advances — but the Lighthouse is also the mold's *organizing node*: destroying it without a cure in hand may leave thousands of infected to transform with no path back.
- Erleena's lab beneath the Lighthouse means any purge or demolition decision has a named, sympathetic NPC standing in the blast radius.
