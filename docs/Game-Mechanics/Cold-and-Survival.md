# Cold & Survival

Rules for the Frostwind Crossing and Barrier Peaks travel, from the Frostwatch Horror module.

## Frostbite

At designated points (hours in a blizzard, each camp made): **DC 13 Constitution saving throw**; characters not native to the harsh lands save at **disadvantage**. Failure: numb pale fingers, slowed movement; accumulated failures bring exhaustion, reduced Dexterity, or permanent injury until proper rest and care.

**Mitigation:** huddling, heat sources, extra furs → advantage or reduced risk; magic and high-quality gear → automatic success or lower DC. Standard human-sized cold-weather kit from Grønnfjord's provisioners is *inadequate* for the Crossing — quality gear is a plot point, not a shopping line.

## Travel challenges

| Challenge | Check |
| :--- | :--- |
| Navigate whiteout | Survival DC 15 (failure: lost time, new encounter roll) |
| Calm panicking sled dogs | Animal Handling DC 14 |
| Spot crevasses | Perception DC 14 |
| Swamp/deep snow slog | Athletics DC 12 (halved movement on failure) |

## Sled logistics

The dogs are life support: no team, no crossing ([Korrin's](../NPCs/Korrin.md) whole worldview). Sleds carry the gear that keeps everyone alive; abandoning a sled is abandoning margin. Axe beaks (recovered at [Think of the Children](../Adventures/Think-of-the-Children.md)) are a viable cold-weather mount alternative.

## Atmosphere dial

Describe creeping numbness and urgency; the cold is a lethal, ever-present antagonist — and now it's the *safe* part of the mountains. Pair with the "Signs of Spreading Infection" table in [Return to Frostwatch](../Adventures/Return-to-Frostwatch.md) once the party is below the crash site.
