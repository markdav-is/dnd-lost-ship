# Current Status

Snapshot of the campaign state for session prep. Last updated after the *Return to Frostwatch* council.

## Where things stand

- The party has completed a **rapid survey of the alien starship** — Level 1, Level 2 (theater), the Garden Level, and the lower reactor/cargo deck. The bulk of the ship remains unexplored, but its general workings are understood.
- The **source of the russet mold has been found: the [Lighthouse](/The-Lost-Ship/The-Lighthouse)**, at the heart of the Garden Level's island.
- **[Erleena Riser](/NPCs/Erleena-Riser)** has been recovered alive and is establishing a cure-research lab in the marine observatory beneath the Lighthouse. **[George Decay](/NPCs/George-Decay)** survives on his drug regimen; two scientists now work aboard toward reversal treatments.
- The party escaped the ship through the **cargo disposal purge** and rode a recovered hoversled down to [Frostwatch Hold](/Locations/Frostwatch-Hold) — finding the mountains visibly dying and the infection networking through wildlife.
- Frostwatch is now **occupied by ~30 [Spásistren](/World/Factions/The-Spasistren)** with military wagons of alchemical incendiaries and containment gear.
- At the **Council of Frostwatch**, [High Sister Valerius](/NPCs/High-Sister-Valerius) severed her Aura implant — an act of heresy — and laid out [three paths](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Lighthouse-Dilemma): containment, orbital purge, or the cure. The desert stranger [Kaelen al-Hajra](/NPCs/Kaelen-al-Hajra) revealed that her continent, Aerun, died this same way centuries ago.

## The question on the table

**What do you do with the Lighthouse?** Destroying it ends the outbreak; preserving it may be the only way to cure the infected — including George. Every faction wants a different answer. See [The Lighthouse Dilemma](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Lighthouse-Dilemma).

## Clocks the DM is running

- **Infection spread** — every day the Lighthouse broadcasts, more of the Barrier Peaks falls; three foothill villages quarantined, two gone silent.
- **George's decline** — his anti-fungal stimulant supply is finite; Kaelen's Sandwalker remedy can buy time.
- **Political pressure** — the noble alliance ([Tomas Vel-Maret](/NPCs/Tomas-Vel-Maret)) is circling; Jak's sealed orders for the ship exist whether or not the party knows it.
