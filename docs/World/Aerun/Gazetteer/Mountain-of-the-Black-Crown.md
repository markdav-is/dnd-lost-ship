# Mountain of the Black Crown

![The Black Crown](../../../.attachments/darksun_black_crown_volcano_art.jpg)

The volcano at the southern end of the Ringing Mountains, standing immediately north of Urik. It smokes constantly and has done within living memory of anyone's grandfather.

## At a glance

- **What it gives** **Obsidian** — the glassy black stone that arms and tools half the continent. Urik's entire economy rests on the quarry terraces cut into its flanks.
- **What it costs** The quarries kill more people than Urik's arena does. The stone's edges slice hands and arms to uselessness within days; survivors are marked for life, and the guarded slave road up the mountain runs one direction far more often than the other.
- **The crown** The dark ring of old lava and ash around the summit that gives the mountain its name — visible from the whole southwestern plain, and from the sea on a clear day.

## Traveler's notes

- Ash falls on Urik whenever the wind shifts, which is often. Locals sweep it without comment.
- Nobody climbs above the quarry line. There is no rule about it; there is simply no reason and a great deal of loose ground.
- Buy obsidian tools in Urik, not from the road-traders. The traders sell the seconds, and a blade that shatters is worse than none.
