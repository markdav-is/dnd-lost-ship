# The Second-Age Guilds

The four working factions that governed Vermoon through "the Board" before Jak's Administration — the Academy of the Fettered Mind, the Devout Coalition, the Gleaners Guild, and the Tinkerers of Thingamajigs. They are the world the party grew up in, and they are still there underneath Jak's Peace.

Source: Andy's 2023 campaign board, `assets/2023-dnd-campaign.json` (list *Vermoon*, plus the Yorrem, Shimmerhill, Drytide and Summervalley session cards). Prep notes, not a transcript.

## The Second Age

This is the **second age** — "an age of utopian ideals and working together, well, at least mostly." After the fall of the great civilization ([the Antari](../The-Antari.md)), life floundered for a long time; out of the ashes came a society of factions that keep each other in balance.

- **No borders.** The factions are not countries. Each has a **main city** where its heads sit and "assign" work to smaller arms, plus cities placed wherever its work needs them. A single town often hosts several factions' offices side by side (Shimmerhill has all four).
- **Specialists who trade.** Nature-based factions produce food and materials; the arcane aid research and adventuring; the mechanically inclined build and maintain transport. Each helps the others as needed.
- **The Board.** Each faction's leader sits on "the board" that ultimately governs the land and sets the standards every "socially inclined person" lives under. All of it rests on shared memory of the catastrophe.
- **Outsiders.** Those who live outside — cast out, shunned, or unwilling — are **tolerated but not accepted**, and do not enjoy society's benefits. Some work with a faction here and there; some answer only to themselves.
- **Planned five or six, wrote four.** Andy's list of focuses was Arcane, Science, Mechanics/Engineering, Nature, Spirituality; only the four below were written up.

## The Academy of the Fettered Mind

*Also written "Academy of the Chained Mind," "College of the Fettered Mind," and once "Academy of the UM."*

- **What it is:** the arcane faction — one main city and many smaller campuses teaching every school of magic, *and the strict control of it*.
- **The chain.** On mastering a practice, a mage **forges a link** of a particular material to mark the mastery and the vow not to use that power for the ill of man. (Which metal goes with which school was never decided.)
- **Why the fetters:** magic is too easy a solution, and that was part of what undid the old world — so there are rules for when you are "allowed" to use it. *Of course, if no one is around to see you use magic improperly, who is to know?*
- **In play:** druids of the Academy work the Yorrem harvest with the Gleaners. The Yorrem library charges 5 gp a head. Offices in Oldspire (a tiny communications post) and Shimmerhill (on the Shelf, researching found artifacts and inventions such as *Arrows of Electric Sting*). **[Professor Erleena Riser](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md)** keeps her office at the Yorrem academy; her friend **Dr. Methuselah Nye** is the scholar the party rescued from the Titan Fighters.

## The Devout Coalition

*"The DC."*

- **What it is:** the religious faction, overseeing every temple and practice it can. **All gods are welcome** and represented, though not every city has a temple to every god. Its stated job is parity: that no god or gods overtake too much of the population.
- **Capital: the Galare,** in the mountains, difficult to reach by those who do not know the way. The lead priest of each temple travels there for the **council of the Standard Bearers**. (The world map's **Holy Citadel** on Malandara is the airship destination named in the Drytide notes; whether it *is* the Galare is not stated.)
- **Temperament:** the ones who "know best" — the faction most inclined to tell the others what they can and cannot do.
- **In play:** in Shimmerhill, a temple covered in reliefs of the gods, assisting with delving and "ridding the world of any undead or bad beings." Their racing team at Drytide was **the Holy Ones** — a lifted golden truck etched with holy symbols.

## The Gleaners Guild

*"The GG."*

- **What it is:** steward of everything natural — the proper use, care, creation, and harvesting of what comes from the earth. Mission: balance between what is needed and what is taken.
- **Capital: Yorrem** (on Caria), the largest GG city, with offices overseeing production worldwide. The year turns on Yorrem's **end-of-harvest festival**, which draws people from across Vermoon — the 2023 campaign opened there.
- **Politics:** works closely with every faction because every faction needs materials — which makes it the most overworked and most requested of the four. It **withholds goods from factions that don't play nicely.**
- **Other GG towns:** **Shimmerhill** (mining, on Taldea), **Summervalley** (fish, sap, fruit, herbs and spices, on Caria's south coast), **Oldspire** (a mill town).
- **The guard — G4CE.** The Gleaners Guild Guard, written *G3*, *G4CE*, *G-Fource*, and said aloud as **"G-Force."** Captain **Elmor Kimem** in Yorrem hired the party to hunt the festival attackers, then put them on **contract**: a **G4CE medallion** shown at any office gets work. (Downtime rule Andy used: pick Easy / Medium / Hard, roll 12+ / 18+ / 22+, earn 50 / 100 / 200 gp.) **This is the "gee force" on [Eustace's](../../Campaign/The-Party/Eustace-Thunderbeak.md) sheet, and the G-Force Medallion in his pack.**
- **Musty-Jo** was pegged by Andy as "probably part of the nature faction."

## The Tinkerers of Thingamajigs

*"T2," "the TT Guild," "Tinkers and Thingamajigs."*

- **What it is:** electrical engineers and mechanics, using science and mathematics to create, restore, and repair contraptions for all the factions — including **airships** (about 60,000 gp, and only through the guild).
- **Home port: Drytide** (on Taldea) — mixed-use streets, shop below and home above, inventors building from salvage or from nothing. Home of the **Great Race** and of **[Rico](../../NPCs/Rico.md)**.
- **In play:** below the Shelf in Shimmerhill, repairing mining machines and working found artifacts. After the party restored power, Drytide's tinkerers found their scavenged Antari **phones and tablets** suddenly responsive — they were trading working ones within days.

## After Jak

Nothing on the board says what became of the Board when [Jak](../../NPCs/Jak-Bjornsson.md) took the Administrator's seat and ruled in the open. Four guild-factions that governed by consensus now share a world with one man who sees through every implant — how each has taken that is unwritten, and is the current DM's to decide.
