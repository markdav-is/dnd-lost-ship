# Aerun Beasts

*Companion volume to [A Wanderer's Journal of Aerun](../../assets/Aerun_Wanderers_Journal.md) — the Chapter Five
that book declined to carry. DM-facing: entries give role, region, and where to find stats in
`/assets`. Player-safe on the deep secrets (the worm–Spice–spore cycle appears only as the desert
people's unexplained lore).*

deep-linkable per creature, e.g. [tembo](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#tembo),
[mekillot](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#mekillot), [silt horror](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-horror-white).*

## 3. The Sea of Silt

Everything below the gray line. Lane law rule five exists because of this section.

> **The Worms of Aerun** *(Aerun-original — build from 5e purple worm, scaled)*
> The rule the whole continent knows: **the deeper the silt, the bigger the worm.**
> - **[Shelf worms](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#shelf-worm)** (margins, shoal country): cart-sized; treat as a **young purple worm** (halve HP,
>   CR ~7). Dangerous the way any big animal is.
> - **[Lane worms](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#lane-worm)** (the Long Reach basins): the wakes the crews stop pointing at. Full **purple worm**
>   (MM, CR 15) and up; convoys carry Guild-mandated silence protocols — engines of noise draw them.
> - **The deep ones** (the far basins, the blank): hull-length-beyond-guessing. No stats; they are
>   weather. If one surfaces, the scene is about the wave.
> The Sandwalkers follow the great worms and harvest Spice where they have passed, and will not
> discuss it. **DM note:** the full worm–spore–Spice cycle is in the First Source docs; nothing in
> play should confirm it from the coastal side.

| Creature | Threat | Aerun notes | Stats |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **[Silt Horror](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-horror-white)** (family) | The tentacled reason for rule five | [White](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-horror-white), [black](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-horror-black), and brown varieties by depth; a horror's take is a masted vessel, crew and all. Waystation crews log wakes like harbor masters log ships | ToD; CC4 |
| **[Silt Runner](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-runner)** | Small reptilian silt-skimming raiders | Shoal-country packs; board becalmed vessels. STC runs them as a full people — parley is possible, cargo is the price | STC; CC4 |
| **[Silt Drake](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-drake)** | Serpent of the shallows | Suns on the shoals; navigators read its basking spots as depth marks, which tells you how navigators think | ToA; CC4 |
| **[Floating Mantle](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#floating-mantle)** | Levitating ray-parasite | Drifts the silt-fall updrafts along the Rim Wall; drops onto deck crews | CC4 |
| **[Cloud Ray](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#cloud-ray)** | Vast soaring shadow | The shape that darkens a convoy and keeps going — mostly. Guild flags mean: *it circled* | ToD; CC4; ToA |
| **[Silt Giants](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#silt-giant)** | The raiders of the ramp | Wade the shallows the way we wade surf; come **down the Balic Saddle** to raid the fields (the legions handle it; the fields get replanted). Island clans on the deep shoals per WJ | ToD; CC4; WJ |

## 5. The Pits and the Arena

What the Houses breed, buy, and bet on. Arena stock is a market: a live capture outprices a
bounty ten to one, and the [**Arena-Bred** template](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#arena-bred-template)
(after CC4's monster theme) is the mechanical hook — bigger, meaner, crowd-trained.

| Stock | Why the crowd comes | Stats |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **[Gaj](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#gaj)** | The mind-eater in the iron cage — Houses keep them quietly as *interrogators*; in the arena, blindfolded handlers and long poles | ToD; CC4; WJ |
| **[Braxat](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#braxat)** | The main event. See §2; taken alive at ruinous cost | ToD; CC4 |
| **[Kirre](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#kirre) / [Tigone](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#tagster)** | Cat versus cat, the connoisseur's card | ToD; ToA |
| **[Dune Reaper warriors](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#dune-reaper-warrior)** | Caged singly; [matrons](Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#dune-reaper-matron) are never brought inside walls twice | ToD; CC4 |
| **Half-giant champions' stables** | Not beasts — but bout cards bill them beside the beasts, which tells you what the pits think of the distinction | ToD; CC4 |
