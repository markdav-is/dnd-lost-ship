# Spicy Jak (future arc — Jak goes missing)

At some point ahead, **the Administrator vanishes**. No abdication, no succession, no explanation — the throne of black stone sits empty, the orbs of the Temple of Nord drift without direction, and the most-watched man on Vermoon becomes the thing he feared most: **a void in the network**.

He has gone to [Aerun](../../World/Aerun.md). Voluntarily. Alone. To learn what the desert monks know — and to eat the Spice.

## Why he goes

Everything in Jak's arc points here:

- **His blind spot, named at the council:** he thinks in networks, populations, and control ([Valerius's phrase](../../NPCs/High-Sister-Valerius.md)). The void showed him a place his sight simply *ends* — and [the invisible infection](The-Invisible-Infection.md) raised the unbearable possibility that his sight is itself **compromised**: if something rides the network, then every implant, every dis-trans bat, every orb is an open door into his own mind.
- **Two women showed him the alternative.** Valerius proved a person can *sever* and remain herself. [Harah](../../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md) proved a person can walk the world with no network at all and see *more* — *"The network sees what it's pointed at. The desert sees everything."* Jak heard that line. It has been working on him ever since.
- **He was a monk before he was a king.** The implant made him powerful; it never made him *whole*. The Aerun tradition offers what the network cannot: sight that is **his** — that cannot be blinded, tapped, or ridden.

## The Dark Sun frame: the avatar road (rules-canonical)

The destination is straight from the conversion doc: the **Way of the Unseen Mind** monk tradition ([Dark Sun Players Guide, Ch. 2](../../Game-Mechanics/Dark-Sun-5e-Rules.md)) climaxes at 17th level in **Psionic Ascension**, whose crowning feature is named **Psionic Avatar** — spend 5 ki to enter a heightened psionic state: resistance to *all* damage, empowered Mind Strike, and a psychic-backlash reaction that punishes anyone who raises a hand against you. The desert monks' whole curriculum (Telekinetic Mastery, Psionic Stomp, Spinning Strike, the ascension itself) is the ladder Jak has gone to climb.

Athasian lore gives the road its gravity — the other ascensions it stands beside:

| Road | Walker | End state |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Dragon** | Defiler-psions (the sorcerer-kings' road) | An immortal engine of appetite |
| **The Avangion** | Preserver-psions | The luminous guardian — Athas's only benevolent apotheosis |
| **The Psionic Avatar** | Way of the Unseen Mind monks | The self as its own instrument — power that takes nothing from the land and nothing from other minds |

The resonance that makes it Jak's story: **an Administrator is an avatar built from machines** — implant, network, orbital sight. The Unseen Mind teaches the same office *without the machine*: sight, reach, and protection generated from discipline alone. Jak — who has held absolute power and felt it distort him — is precisely the student that path was written for.

**Where he trains:** with desert masters of the Unseen Mind beyond the [Rim Wall's](../../World/Aerun.md) reach — orders who serve no sorcerer-king, tolerated by the [Sandwalkers](../../World/Factions/The-Sandwalkers.md) because their discipline takes nothing from the land. (Whether Harah's people quietly sponsored his crossing is a lovely ambiguity — she is, after all, the only person he knows who could move him across the sea *invisibly*.)

## The Spice, taken in a big way

Ambient Spice gives nearly every Aerun-born a minor wild talent ([canon](The-First-Source.md)). Jak does not take it ambiently. The monks' deep practice uses the Spice as sacrament — massed exposure, fasting, trance — and Jak, characteristically, **commits absolutely**:

- **The eyes:** blue-within-blue, the heavy user's stain. The most recognizable face on Vermoon now carries the desert's mark — he can never again pretend to be what he was.
- **Latent talents surfacing:** roll on the wild talent table (Players Guide p. 112) — more than once. Suggested spine: **telepathic touch** (he no longer needs the network to hear), **psychometry** (objects tell him their history), and prescience flashes — not Dune-grade foresight, but *thin moments*: he answers questions half a breath before they're asked.
- **The cost:** dependency is real. Withdrawal strips the talents and opens the old wound — a man who has now been all-seeing **twice**, by machine and by sacrament, learning both can be taken away. The Spice's true nature ([the worms, the First Source](The-First-Source.md)) means his new sight is, in a way he doesn't yet know, *borrowed from Rajaat's jailers*.

**Spicy Jak, statted:** take his [current block](../../NPCs/Jak-Bjornsson.md), strip every Aura-network feature, and rebuild as **Way of the Unseen Mind** progression: Mind Strike and telepathy · Telekinetic Mastery (Psionic Stomp, Spinning Strike) at the 11th-level tier · 2–3 rolled wild talents · cannot be surprised (prescient flinch — no network required, no off-switch at the void's edge) · vulnerability: Spice supply. The arc's mechanical finish line is the 17th-level **Psionic Avatar** itself. Same fists. New sight.

## Meanwhile, the world he left

The vacuum is the playable part:

- **The Spásistren without their node.** Ten thousand zealots whose god has gone silent. Valerius — the one High Sister who already knows how to stand alone — either holds the order together or watches it schism into she-who-waits and she-who-seeks factions. Her severing stops being heresy and starts looking like *foresight*.
- **The political scramble:** the [merchant alliance](The-Merchant-Alliance.md) moves within days; [Tomas](../../NPCs/Tomas-Vel-Maret.md) suddenly holds sealed orders signed by a ghost; Nordkrist's Hold construction continues on momentum, unsupervised.
- **The party's dilemma:** they are the only people Jak would have told — *did* he tell them? Options: a sealed letter that arrives after he's gone ("Don't look for me. Cover for me."), a cold trail they're begged to follow, or total silence and the slow realization that their friend chose the desert over them. Finding Spicy Jak in an Aerun arc — blue-eyed, barefoot, *calmer than they have ever seen him* — is the reunion scene the whole thread aims at.

## How it ties back

- **The hopepunk read:** Jak's arc lands as growth, not abdication — the man who ruled through total surveillance learns to see without owning what he sees. If [Return to the Ship](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md) ends with the network cleansed and his grip loosening, this is the *next* step of that same softening: he doesn't loosen his grip on the world; he opens his hand entirely.
- **The dark read (hold in reserve):** an avatar is still absolute power, just portable. The campaign's long question — does the crown corrupt him — doesn't end when he trades the crown for the Spice. It sharpens. The sorcerer-kings were students once too.
- **The Rajaat braid:** his new sight runs on the Spice; the Spice runs on the worms; the worms guard the First Source. If the cure ever frees the wardens and the desert blooms ([the epilogue flight](../../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md)), what happens to the sacrament — and to every talent it woke? Spicy Jak has a personal stake in Rajaat's fate that no one, including him, has priced in.

## Timing

Fire this **after** the Return to the Ship arc resolves (his soft landing there is the setup), ideally as the bridge into an Aerun campaign phase: the party crosses the sea for the First Source sample, the Sere reconciliation, or the cure delivery — and somewhere out past the Rim Wall, a rumor of a blue-eyed southern monk is waiting for them.
