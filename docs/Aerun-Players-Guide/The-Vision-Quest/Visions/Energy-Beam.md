# Energy Beam

*Four titans in a ring. You must choose one.*

## The vision

You are standing in a valley that is four valleys.

North of you, a high pass in cloud, the wind screaming through it. East, a slope of black slag with heat coming off it in sheets. South, a cliff of gray stone so old it has no edges. West, terraces running green down toward a sea you cannot see, and rain on them, real rain, falling.

In each direction, something very large is lying down. Now each of them lifts its head.

## The beast

[Drakes](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#elemental-drake). Four of them, each the length of a Guild hull, each the thing its valley is made of. The air drake is the shape the wind takes when it is paying attention. The earth drake has not moved in a thousand years and has never needed to. The fire drake's scales are the colors a forge goes through. The rain drake is the only one you could call beautiful, and it is the one you trust least.

They do not speak. They look at you. They are not asking which of them you *like*.

You have to walk to one. That is the whole of the vision. The valley is still and the four heads are up and nothing will happen until you pick a direction and walk, and the first step is the choice, and there is no second step back.

*Choose now, at the table, and say it aloud: air, earth, fire, or rain.*

Whichever you walk to lowers its head as you come. It breathes on you. Cold, or thunder, or fire, or lightning, or the sting of acid in rain — whichever it is, it does not harm you. It goes *through* you, and leaves a line, like a channel cut in a dry bed, that water will find again.

The other three put their heads down. They are not offended. They were never yours.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. The air around you is not right — too warm, or too cold, or carrying a smell of ozone or wet stone — and it clears as you stand.

Blue at the edge of your eye. One hand is tingling and will not stop for an hour.

## The gift

**Energy Beam.** *As an action, one creature you can see within 90 feet must make a Dexterity save or take 1d6 damage of the type you chose: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. The damage grows as you do.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward. Neither does the drake you walked to.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: one head lowering out of its own weather.
