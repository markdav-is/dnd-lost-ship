# Vigor

*Patience turns out to be a thing you can hand to someone.*

## The vision

The Ring Road at dusk. An argosy is coming — you hear it before anything, the long groan of the hull, the creak of a village-sized thing on wheels — and ahead of it, in harness, the mountain.

You have seen mekillots before. From the road, from the side, from a safe distance. You have not stood in front of one with the sun going down behind it and watched it come.

It fills the road. It fills the sky. Each foot comes down like a decision that has been made for a very long time. The argosy behind it is a toy.

It stops, because you are in the way, and it does not seem to mind.

## The beast

A [mekillot](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#mekillot). Guild-bred, Guild-owned, the engine of everything that moves on Aerun. No Navigator tonight; the hood at the bow is empty, and no drover walks the harness line. It is alone with you, and it lowers its head — a boulder with eyes — until the great blunt snout is level with you and the breath of it is warm and grass-sweet and moves your hair.

It does not do anything. That is what it does.

It *stands there*. You feel the weight of it, and under the weight, what holds the weight up: not strength, exactly. Something more like *endurance* — the plain fact of a thing that has hauled argosies for forty years and will haul them tomorrow, that does not hurry and does not stop and has never once asked how far.

It leans into you. Very gently. A mountain leaning on a person, and not crushing them. And some of that — a little, a handful — comes *across*. Not to keep. To hold for a while.

You look at your hands. There is nothing in them. But you know, the way you know your own name, that you could turn and put your hand on someone's shoulder and *they* would have it instead.

The mekillot lifts its head, and the argosy groans, and the mountain walks on through you toward a waystation it will reach exactly when it reaches it.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. Your guide is tired — really tired, the kind that shows — and without thinking you put out a hand.

Blue at the edge of your eye. Your guide straightens, and looks at you, and does not ask.

## The gift

**Vigor.** *As an action, one willing creature you touch gains 5 temporary hit points for one minute. A creature can receive this once per day.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a boulder with eyes, lowering, breath that smells of grass.
