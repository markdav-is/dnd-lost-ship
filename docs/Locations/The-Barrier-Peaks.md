# The Barrier Peaks

The southern mountain range — forbidden zone, crash site, and the void in the world's all-seeing eye. Increasingly dangerous for exactly as long as Jak has ruled.

## Reputation

- Strange lights and sounds at night; monsters "like nothing in the old tales" — metallic, multi-eyed, part metal and part flesh; a stench "like rotting meat mixed with hot metal."
- Expeditions vanish. Sea captains refuse the coastal waters: things in the water that aren't fish, metal gleaming beneath the waves, machinery grinding in the deep.
- The clerics keep creature corpses preserved in brine; no sage can identify them.

## The void

The heart of the range does not exist in Jak's reality: no scrying, no network perception, no teleportation, ethereal, or astral travel. The cause is [the ship](/The-Lost-Ship) itself — its hyper-dimensional properties. The ship was always there. It was always active. It has been broadcasting spores for a very long time.

## The infection landscape (current)

Since the party's survey, the mountains are visibly dying:

- Pale fungal veins trace the ridgelines like exposed nerves; snow glows faintly at night with bioluminescent spores.
- Wildlife doesn't flee — it **watches**, eyes filmed white, coordinated like scouts ([The Invisible Infection](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Invisible-Infection)).
- Three foothill villages quarantined; two silent; scouts unreturned.
