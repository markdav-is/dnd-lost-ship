## Traits, actions, and reactions
| Name | Type | Text for DDB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The Bell | Bonus Action | The belgoi rings its bell at one creature within 300 feet of it that can hear it. The target must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or it must use its movement on each of its turns to walk toward the sound. The target repeats the saving throw at the end of each round, ending the effect on a success. A creature that is asleep when the bell is first rung automatically fails the save — it walks dreaming. A creature holding fire in its hand has advantage on the saving throw. |
| Draining Touch | Action | *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage. The belgoi regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt. |
