# Wandering Aerun

*An unlicensed voice on the Guild's own Relay, and why everyone on the Ring Road has an opinion about it.*

## What everyone knows (no check)

- Somewhere on **Relay channel forty-three** there is a broadcast that should not exist: an unlicensed program hosted by a man who calls himself only **the Wanderer**, transmitting from an undisclosed relay somewhere in the [Sea of Silt](Gazetteer/The-Sea-of-Silt.md). He talks about the roads, the gates, the water, and the people who work them, in no hurry at all.
- The Relay belongs to the Guild, only Navigators are taught its working, and no one else is supposed to be able to speak on it. **The Guild does not discuss the broadcast** — which, on Aerun, is how everyone knows it is real.
- **Transcription wafers** of past episodes circulate in the markets, and can be found tucked into copies of *A Wanderer's Journal of Aerun*. Owning one is not illegal. Nothing about the broadcast is illegal, exactly, which is part of what makes the Guild's silence so loud.

## Recurring voices

People who have spoken on the air, and are now mildly famous for it:

| Name | Who they are |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bellin** | Keeper of the **Midreach** [waystation](Gazetteer/The-Waystations.md), halfway across the Long Reach |
| **Master Yaxa** | Of the School of Reckoning, seconded to House Wavir |
| **Clerk Praxa** | Of the [Balic](Gazetteer/Balic.md) west gate — twenty-two years of service, and the reason travelers now know what the wax and the triplicate log are actually for |

Caravan crews within reach of a far-speaker mark the broadcast hours the way they mark the beacon schedule. The Houses would very much like to know where the Wanderer's relay stands. So far the silt is keeping it.
