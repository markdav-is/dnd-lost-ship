# Salt View

A village on the eastern face of the Mekillot Mountains, founded by people who left their previous employment without notice. Boisterous, unruly, and — improbably — the finest theater on Aerun.

## At a glance

- **Population** A few hundred ex-slaves of every race, governed loosely by an ex-gladiator mul named **Xaynon**.
- **Living** Two trades. The old one is raiding caravans on the Gulg and Nibenay roads. The new one is **sending out acting troupes** to perform for wealthy merchants — who pay well and, unlike nobles, are not sneered at in return.
- **Standing** Officially it should not exist. In practice the houses find it useful and the cities find it distant.

## Traveler's notes

- The troupes are genuinely superb — a generation of people who survived the arena and discovered they preferred an audience.
- Salt View will not perform for nobles. Not for any fee. Ask why and you will get a long answer and a longer drink.
- Traveling this stretch of road, a caravan either hires Salt View as escort or is robbed by it, and the town is perfectly open about the choice.
