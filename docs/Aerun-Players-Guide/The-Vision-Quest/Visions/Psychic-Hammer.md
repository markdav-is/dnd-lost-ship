# Psychic Hammer

*It shows you its fist. Then it shows you the fist was never the point.*

## The vision

Night. Stony waste. You are crouched behind a boulder because something is walking in the dark, and the ground tells you how big it is before your eyes do.

It is not hurrying. Things that size do not. Each step comes down with a pause before it, as if the leg were deciding, and then the weight arrives and the stones jump.

It stops on the other side of your boulder. You can hear it breathing. The breath smells of acid.

## The beast

A [braxat](../../../Bestiary/Aerun-Stat-Blocks.md#braxat). Huge, plated, walking upright like a man and built like a siege tower; the apex bounty, the one the arena promoters pay fortunes for and the one bounty crews die taking. It looks down at you over the boulder. It has known you were there since you chose the boulder.

It lifts one hand. The fist is bigger than your torso. It holds it there so you can look at it properly — the plates, the knuckles like stones, the sheer *mass* — and then it sets the fist down, gently, on the top of the boulder.

The boulder moves.

Not from the fist. The fist is resting. The boulder — a ton of rock, easily — slides backward a yard across the ground with a sound like a door opening, and stops, and you are crouched behind nothing.

The braxat looks at you. *The fist*, it means, *was to get your attention. This is the thing.* Something went out from it — from behind the eyes, not the arm — and it was not force the way a fist is force. It was *insistence*. The rock was simply told that it was somewhere else.

It lets you feel the shape of that telling. Then it turns and walks away into the dark, in no hurry, and the stones jump after it for a long time.

## The waking

Dawn. The ring of stones. One of the stones in the ring is out of its place, a yard back, and there is a groove in the dust behind it.

Your guide looks at the stone and at you. Blue at the edge of your eye.

## The gift

**Psychic Hammer.** *As an action, one creature you can see within 120 feet must make a Strength save or take 1d4 force damage and be pushed 10 feet. The damage grows as you do.*

You choose now whether this gift runs on your Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. It does not change afterward.

The beast stays. The first time each day you use the gift, you see it for an instant: a fist the size of your chest, resting, not striking.
