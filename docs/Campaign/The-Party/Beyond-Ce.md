## Backstory (from the sheet)

In the player's own words, closely summarized. She is *she* throughout.

- **Not a hero, and not interested.** *"Heroes seek glory. They tell stories about themselves. They chase monsters because they want to be remembered for killing them. Beyond-Ce has always been more practical."*
- **What she does: she gets people where they're going.** If someone needs to get from one place to another, she can get them there; if the road is dangerous, she knows how to navigate it; if something comes looking for her passengers, *"she knows how to make it regret finding them."* And if someone is willing to pay enough, she takes the job.
- **Her reputation:** a reliable **escort and guide**, built over years. Merchants, travelers, adventurers, couriers — *"and occasionally people who would rather not explain why they need to leave town."* She doesn't ask many questions. She doesn't make promises she can't keep. **Most importantly, she doesn't abandon her clients when things go wrong.**
- **The line people say about her:** ***"If Beyond-Ce agrees to get you somewhere, you will probably get there."*** — *"The important word is* probably."
- **"The Road."** The backstory's next section opens: *she learned early that traveling with other people requires more than knowing which direction to walk — "You have to know when to stop."*

> **The export cuts off there.** D&D Beyond's PDF keeps only what fits in the box — about 1,200 characters — and the text stops at the start of "The Road," mid-thought. There is clearly more. Ask Andy for the full text (it is on his D&D Beyond character page), and paste it in.

**Still blank, and still Andy's:** *which* faction the Faction Agent background means; where she is from; the Collar of the Beast.

## DM hooks

- **Took heavy psychic damage and was briefly dominated** in the mind flayer theater fight — psychic scars available for callbacks.
- Faction Agent background: the natural hook for [Tomas Vel-Maret and the merchant alliance](../Plot-Threads/The-Merchant-Alliance.md) to make contact through. Which faction is the blank on the sheet.
- Player Andy is the world's creator-DM and keeper of Aerun deep lore — the remaining **[ANDY LORE]** insertion points in [The Green Age](../Plot-Threads/The-Green-Age.md) are his to define, in or out of character.
- A sorcerer watching wizards defile: if any arc tempts a player character toward the [Arcane Defilement](../../Game-Mechanics/Dark-Sun-5e-Rules.md) rules, the party's arcanist has a front-row seat — and Andy knows exactly what it costs.

- **She is an escort by trade, and the arc just handed her a client.** The Return to the Ship arc ends with the party taking [Erleena](../../NPCs/Erleena-Riser.md) — and fifty pounds of books — off the ship, across the ice, and over a sea to Aerun. That is, word for word, the job on Beyond-Ce's sheet: get someone from one place to another, through danger, and don't abandon them when it goes wrong. Let Erleena *hire* her, or try to. *"Probably"* is the word to play with.
- **Aerun runs on her profession.** A continent of caravans, bonded cargo, and guides who state a price ([Aerun — A Primer](../../Aerun-Players-Guide/Aerun-A-Primer.md)) is a place where a reliable escort with a reputation is understood immediately — and where the Transit Guild has opinions about freelancers.
