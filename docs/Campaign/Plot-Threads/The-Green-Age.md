# The Green Age

Aerun — the equatorial desert continent — was not always a desert. Centuries ago it was a living world of rivers, forests, and cities. It died fast enough to be catastrophic and slow enough to be survived. What is happening in the Barrier Peaks is how it began.

## What Kaelen's people know

- The [Sandwalkers](/World/Factions/The-Sandwalkers) preserve oral histories back to the Green Age. They are witnesses, not scholars — knowledge carried in song, scar, and the reading of ruins.
- Their word ***vel-keth*** — "the hunger that eats the world" — names the force that consumed their homeland. Kaelen recognizes vel-keth in the ship's infection.
- Three months ago the primal spirits of the deep desert began to go **silent**. *"Dead things do not warn you. Silent things are waiting."* The Sandwalkers call this the **Defiling Silence**; Kaelen tracked it north across the sea to the ship.
- The oldest ruins beneath Aerun's sand are **not human-built**.

## Competing theories of the desertification

| Theory | Cause | Implication |

| **The Titan's Fall** | The colossal entity called the Titan ("the Work") caused the devastation — or was the last attempt to stop it | The Titan still slumbers in the deep desert; the ship's energy may awaken or corrupt it |
| **The Sorcerer-King's Bargain** | One ruler traded the land's vitality for immortality | Echoes Jak: the Aura network may be draining something too |

## Mechanical hook — the Primal Rite of Remembrance

Kaelen can perform a one-hour ritual using a piece of material from the ship, granting the party a **vision of Aerun during the Green Age**. The vision contains one specific fact about the ship's original purpose or the infection's nature that cannot be obtained any other way — prepare it to deliver whatever revelation the campaign needs at that moment.

## The stakes

If the ship's systems are not neutralized, the infection will drain the living energy of the land itself: the mountains die, then the forests, then the plains. The world has been through this before. It barely survived.
