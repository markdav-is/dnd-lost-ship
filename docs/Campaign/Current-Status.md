# Current Status

Snapshot of the campaign state for session prep. **Last updated 2026-10-21, after Return to the Lighthouse (session 4, played 2026-10-20): the party is out of the ship with Erlina, on a hover cart, bound for the coast and a ship to Balic.**

## Where things stand

- The **Council of Frostwatch has happened as played**: Valerius severed her implant, revealed the true spread, and [Harah](../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md) **revealed a second source** — on Aerun, under a thousand-year quarantine — and the stakes became planetary triage.
- **The party chose the Cure path** and set out for the ship **by dogsled convoy on the glacier route behind [Korrin's](../NPCs/Korrin.md) lead sled** (the spore-choked ravines are still impassable — the sledmaster's route avoids them). **Five went**: Shhhmeowmeow, Eustace, Musty-Jo, Beyond-Ce, Robin ([The Party](The-Party.md)). **Harah stayed at Frostwatch**; Gobbledegook is no longer with the party.
- **Played so far:** [Return to the Ship](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md) (the Whiteout, the Breach, the Lab at S42), session 3 of [The Awakened Ship](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship.md) (the frog), and [Return to the Lighthouse](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Lighthouse.md) ([Adventure Log](Adventure-Log.md)). At the Lighthouse door Shhhmeowmeow, Robin and Beyond-Ce saw the desert, Musty-Jo saw the connected world, and Eustace fainted. **Erlina came up, joined the party and left the ship with them.** The cargo door is under Eddie's control and sealed behind them. **Not done:** the antenna, Nova (still asleep in her pod, unknown to the party), and the Send-Off round-robin.
- **Conditions carried into next session:** none recorded. **The visions linger:** Shhhmeowmeow, Robin and Beyond-Ce feel *compelled* toward it; Musty-Jo believes the spores could make a better world. **Doses not yet taken.**
- **Erlina travels with the party**, in the medical uniform, with her old suit, a sprayer, her notes and the box of *A Brief History of Vermoon*. Her read: the Lighthouse is a projection from a darker reality, the mold is what happens where it touches the garden, and the answer lies in the Silt Sea.
- **Where the arc landed:** Erlina is going to Aerun with the party. **George stays** on the ship with Eddie, holding the Lighthouse's attention, and the sealed door helps them contain the spread.
- **The second reason to go:** the **fire city under Eldorado** — a buried elemental city Erlina has seen only from its gate room and never dug, and a backstory thread of Shhhmeowmeow's. Unrelated to the infection ([The Elemental Cities](../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md)).
- **Harah is in contact.** Robin reached her by *sending*. She can meet the party at **Frostwatch or Balic**, and she wants to come. The party chose Balic and is skipping Frostwatch, because they don't trust the Spásistren. The council's wish for the [Order of the Sere](../World/Factions/The-Order-of-the-Sere.md) to keep the spread down still runs through her.
- **Robin recruited** — the occupation's infirmary medic ([her page](The-Party/Robin-Wood.md)) — now a full **PC** (new player).
- **The party is withholding the network-corruption theory.** After Copper's warning and the infected-wildlife descent, they deliberately kept the invisible-infection details out of official channels — *"the people we're talking to will have a glowing ear"* — and debated reaching Jak directly with information for him alone.
- **Antitoxin: one dose left.** The whole party was infected at some point and cured by the ship's antitoxin — fast-acting, not long-lasting. The scarcity is felt. George's six stopgap doses (five for the party, one for Erlina) are the bridge.
- On the ground: **one town fully shut down, two quarantined**; the Spásistren are recruiting heavily in Grønnfjord.

## The question on the table

**How to get to Aerun.** The party is out of the Barrier Peaks with Erlina and wants a ship to **Balic**, with Harah meeting them there. On the table: a hired ship from the coast (Grønnfjord is the northernmost port, and Eustace was a pirate), **Rico** on Airlantis (willing, but he'd be fighting the **westerly trade winds** that run along the equator as a barrier between north and south), and the party's dragons (kept out of the spores so far). Frostwatch is inland and needs dogs, and they're avoiding it. Aerun runs hot, about 115°F at its worst, and the table has already started tracking water with Tic Tacs.

## Clocks the DM is running

- **Infection spread** — faster than official awareness; one town gone, two quarantined; wildlife networking; ravines impassable on foot. **Paused, not stopped**, while the Lighthouse is busy with George — *for now*.
- **Erlina's exposure** — her old pressure suit (her "diving suit") gets her up through the Lighthouse but no further; she wears the ship-made medical uniform and leaves the lake with a dose, a spore-blaster, and no more protection than the party has.
- **George's holding action** — half-cured on his Eddie-assisted anti-fungals ([his page](../NPCs/George-Decay.md)); the doses hold the line but don't finish the job, and the Lighthouse is now actively fighting him. Every day it does is a day it isn't spreading.
- **The Sere negotiation** — containment cover for the mission, and now Erlina's defiler, depend on a reconciliation nobody has explained yet.
- **Political pressure** — the sealed orders exist; the [merchant alliance](Plot-Threads/The-Merchant-Alliance.md) circles; the party is already managing information around Jak's own apparatus.
