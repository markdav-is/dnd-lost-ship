> **Chapter not yet received.** This chapter downloads from the **House Inika relay** when you enter the region. Until then, here is what the Council can tell you from Balic.

**Look for the banner:** a gold circle on black.

## What we can tell you from here

- **Gulg** lies deep in the Crescent Forest, behind a wall that is alive: a hedge of interwoven thorn-trees grown so tight a halfling's fist won't pass.
- **Population:** about 8,000. The smallest of the seven cities, and it likes that.
- **House Inika** trades small, fast, and expensive: gems, furs, feathers, kola, and spices.
- **There is no open market.** A merchant deals with one assigned house factor, who barters for the whole city and drives a famously brutal bargain.

## In the region

- **Gulg:** the forest city behind a living wall of thorns.
- **The Crescent Forest:** the last great wood, and two cities that want it.
- **Ogo:** the halfling chief's pyramid under the canopy.

## Getting there

**140 miles east of Balic** by the Ring Road, by way of Altaruk and Amber Valley: about a week with a walking caravan. Gulg hangs off the road by a forest track, because the road does not enter the wood.
