# First Mark, Midreach, and Saltbone

The three towers on [the Long Reach](The-Long-Reach.md) — squat stone waystations built on the firmest shoals in the open silt, and the only fixed points between Raam and Balic.

## The three

- **First Mark** — the northern station, a day out from Raam's shelf. Busiest of the three: last chance to turn back, first chance to fix what the crossing has already broken.
- **Midreach** — the middle of nowhere, in the middle of the crossing. A rope-yard, a cistern, a barracks, and a beacon. The crews here are the most senior and the strangest.
- **Saltbone** — the southern station, before the run down to Balic's crest. Named for what the marker crews kept digging up when they sank the foundations, and nobody has ever wanted to know more than that.

## What they are

Each is the same kit: a tower with a **beacon fire** on top, a **cistern**, a **rope-yard** for repairs, and barracks for the marker crews who maintain the lane. All Guild property, all permanently staffed, all resupplied by the convoys that stop at them.

## Traveler's notes

- **A convoy that misses a waystation is a convoy in trouble.** The stations are spaced for a day's run; overshooting one means a night on the open silt.
- Beacons are answered, always, by anyone who can see them. This is the one rule of the silt that even smugglers keep.
- The crews are worth talking to — they see everything that crosses, remember all of it, and are bored enough to tell you. They will not, however, discuss the northwest.
