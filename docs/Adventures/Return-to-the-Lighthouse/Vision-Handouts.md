# Vision Handouts

*Print and cut apart. Everyone at the door rolls a d6. Hand each player the card for their roll. Print extra copies, because several players can get the same card.*

| d6 | Card |
| :-: | :--- |
| 1 | The desert |
| 2–3 | The connected world |
| 4 | The headache |
| 5 | The faint |
| 6 | The clear head |

---

## The connected world

![Vermoon laced with gold](../../.attachments/vision_world_web.jpg)

**You saw:** gold threads joining everything. Rabbits whose hearts beat with yours. Animals walking together without fear. A town of laughing people, their glowing ears linked to every living thing. The whole world, breathing as one.

**You feel:** the spores aren't the enemy. Let them spread and everyone will feel what everyone else feels. No more cruelty, no more loneliness. A paradise. Why would anyone stop that?

---

## The desert

![A grey desert of black glass spires](../../.attachments/lh_door_desert.jpg)

**You saw:** the tower split open on a scorching grey desert. Black glass spires. Hot wind full of grit. Something huge moving under the sand. The light and the spores were pouring out of it.

**You feel:** that desert is in Aerun, and it's the root of all this. The spores can only be stopped there. You have to go, and soon.

---

## The headache

**You saw** nothing, but something from the tower tried to get into your head.

**Rules:** take 1d6 psychic damage, and you have disadvantage on Intelligence and Wisdom checks until a short rest.

---

## The faint

**You saw** nothing. The world tilted and you woke up in the moss with a nosebleed.

**Rules:** you're prone and unconscious for 1 minute, or until someone uses an action to shake you awake or you take damage.

---

## The clear head

**Nothing happened to you.** Some of your friends may have frozen, laughed at nothing, or fallen over.
