# Current Status

Snapshot of the campaign state for session prep. **Last updated 2026-10-07, after The Awakened Ship session 3 (played 2026-10-06): the froghemoth is dead, the party crossed the bridge to the island, and the Lighthouse is still shining.**

## Where things stand

- The **Council of Frostwatch has happened as played**: Valerius severed her implant, revealed the true spread, and [Harah](../NPCs/Harah-Tabr.md) **named Rajaat** — the thousand-year quarantine, the second source, planetary triage.
- **The party chose the Cure path** and set out for the ship **by dogsled convoy on the glacier route behind [Korrin's](../NPCs/Korrin.md) lead sled** (the spore-choked ravines are still impassable — the sledmaster's route avoids them). **Five went**: Shhhmeowmeow, Eustace, Musty-Jo, Beyond-Ce, Robin ([The Party](The-Party.md)). **Harah stayed at Frostwatch**; Gobbledegook is no longer with the party.
- **Played so far:** [The Whiteout](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Whiteout.md), [The Breach](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Breach.md), [The Lab at S42](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Lab-at-S42.md), and session 3 of [The Awakened Ship](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship.md) ([Adventure Log](Adventure-Log.md)). The party took the drop tube to the garden, banished a teleporting fiend, killed the horrid plant with fire and defoliant, and crossed by the bridge. Beyond-Ce's *darkness* stopped the Lighthouse beam only while she held it; lightning did nothing to the tower. **The froghemoth is dead**, killed on the shore by the bridge. They chose the frog over the antenna, and the antenna and the big door are still on the list. **They stopped on the island at the foot of the Lighthouse.** **Next:** [The tower in full and the door](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Lighthouse.md#the-tower-in-full): awe, then the Wisdom save and the visions ([Vision Handouts](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Lighthouse/Vision-Handouts.md)). Then the hatch, Erleena's lab and the turn, optionally [The Jammed Override](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship/The-Jammed-Override.md) / [The Antenna](../Adventures/The-Awakened-Ship/The-Antenna.md), and then **[The Send-Off](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship/The-Send-Off.md)**.
- **Conditions carried into next session:** none recorded. The party took about 26 damage across the frog fight and no infection saves were rolled; spells spent in session 3 include Fly (4th), Fireball, two *Aganazzar's scorcher*, *darkness*, *charm monster*, *banishment*, *bless*, *call lightning*, the shark summon, a Divine Smite and 3 sorcery points. No rest since. **Doses not yet taken.**
- **Erleena is unreachable from outside** (but the party has now spoken to her on the internal comm from George's console) — *"No signal. I'm sorry, your number cannot be completed."* The ship's hyper-dimensional fields filter RF — the same void that blinds Jak's network — so nothing reaches her sealed levels from outside the hull. Inside the hull she is in daily contact with George and Eddie. The party carries her a month of outside news, the council's above all; she asks for it before they can offer.
- **Where the arc lands:** Eddie and George have already told Erleena the outside news. What moves her is the party's **desert vision**: she recognises the Sea of Silt from her internship dig in Aerun's northern desert and **decides to leave with the party for Aerun**. **George stays** on the ship with Eddie, holding the Lighthouse's attention. With the spore-blasters, closing the big door and getting out are easy ([Return to the Lighthouse](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Lighthouse.md)).
- **The second reason to go:** the **fire city under Eldorado** — a buried elemental city Erleena has seen only from its gate room and never dug, and a backstory thread of Shhhmeowmeow's. Unrelated to the infection ([The Elemental Cities](../World/The-Elemental-Cities.md)).
- **Defiler containment is in motion — through Harah, uneasily.** The council wants the [Order of the Sere](../World/Factions/The-Order-of-the-Sere.md) to keep the spread tamped down during the mission. But reaching them means travel, and **they will not talk to Harah**: *"They wouldn't necessarily trust me… It's a long story. Meet me at the bar later."* Erleena's second ask now makes that meeting unavoidable.
- **Robin recruited** — the occupation's infirmary medic ([her page](The-Party/Robin-Wood.md)) — now a full **PC** (new player).
- **The party is withholding the network-corruption theory.** After Copper's warning and the infected-wildlife descent, they deliberately kept the invisible-infection details out of official channels — *"the people we're talking to will have a glowing ear"* — and debated reaching Jak directly with information for him alone.
- **Antitoxin: one dose left.** The whole party was infected at some point and cured by the ship's antitoxin — fast-acting, not long-lasting. The scarcity is felt. George's six stopgap doses (five for the party, one for Erleena) are the bridge.
- On the ground: **one town fully shut down, two quarantined**; the Spásistren are recruiting heavily in Grønnfjord.

## The question on the table

The direction is chosen — now it has to survive contact: get Erleena and her lab off the ship and across the sea before the spread outruns everyone, with [Eddie awake](../Adventures/Return-to-the-Ship.md) and the Lighthouse fighting back. The council framing stands as triage, not a vote: seal/purge remain the fallbacks if the cure fails ([The Lighthouse Dilemma](Plot-Threads/The-Lighthouse-Dilemma.md)). How the party gets to Aerun is the next module's problem.

## Clocks the DM is running

- **Infection spread** — faster than official awareness; one town gone, two quarantined; wildlife networking; ravines impassable on foot. **Paused, not stopped**, while the Lighthouse is busy with George — *for now*.
- **Erleena's exposure** — her old pressure suit (her "diving suit") gets her up through the Lighthouse but no further; she wears the ship-made medical uniform and leaves the lake with a dose, a spore-blaster, and no more protection than the party has.
- **George's holding action** — half-cured on his Eddie-assisted anti-fungals ([his page](../NPCs/George-Decay.md)); the doses hold the line but don't finish the job, and the Lighthouse is now actively fighting him. Every day it does is a day it isn't spreading.
- **The Sere negotiation** — containment cover for the mission, and now Erleena's defiler, depend on a reconciliation nobody has explained yet.
- **Political pressure** — the sealed orders exist; the [merchant alliance](Plot-Threads/The-Merchant-Alliance.md) circles; the party is already managing information around Jak's own apparatus.
