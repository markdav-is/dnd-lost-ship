# The Watching Herd

*Return to Frostwatch, descent encounter 2 — dread / possible combat.*

![The Watching Herd](../../.attachments/enc_watching_herd.jpg)

Twelve mountain deer stand in a clearing directly in the hoversled's path. They do not move. They do not flinch at the sound of the sled. Every head is turned toward the party, and every pair of eyes is filmed with white.

## Behavior

- Party goes around → the deer **rotate in unison** to keep watching.
- Party stops → the herd takes **one slow step forward**, then stops.
- Any deer attacked, or the party lingers **3+ rounds** → the herd charges.

## Infected Deer (Elk stats + modifications)

- **Spore Burst (Recharge 5–6):** when the deer takes damage — 10-ft radius cloud, **DC 13 CON** or poisoned 1 minute.
- **Hive Awareness:** the entire herd shares a single initiative count and acts simultaneously.

## Running it

**DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Nature):** the movements are too coordinated, too purposeful. They are not acting like animals. **They are acting like scouts.**

If George Decay is present: *"Distributed intelligence. The mold doesn't just infect — it networks. Oh my. That is... considerably more alarming than I had hoped."*
