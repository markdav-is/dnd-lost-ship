# Yaramuke and the Black Oasis

Ruins on the Road of Kings, roughly halfway between Urik and Raam. A city stood here. It does not now, and the manner of its ending is the reason travelers camp elsewhere.

## At a glance

- **What happened** Yaramuke quarreled with Urik over quarrying rights on the Black Crown. The quarrel was settled by the destruction of the city — completely, in a single working, with a thoroughness that people a thousand years later still lower their voices about.
- **The oasis** The pool at the heart of the ruins was **poisoned by whatever ended the city, and remains poisoned.** Drink and a chill settles into you and you sicken; many do not recover. It looks perfectly clear.
- **The treasure** The old queen's hoard is supposed to lie beneath her palace. You are welcome to work out which pile of rubble that was.

## Traveler's notes

- **Do not drink. Do not water animals. Do not camp near the pool.** The ruins are also reputed to be haunted by those who ignored the first three instructions, and the reputation is old and consistent.
- Caravans pass Yaramuke without stopping as a matter of course, even when it costs them an hour of daylight.
- Whatever else it is, the site is the plainest lesson on the continent about what the great powers can do when a trade dispute goes badly. Everyone who walks the Road of Kings receives that lesson without being told.
