# Rico

Engineer and recurring fixer from the previous campaign — the hands-on counterpart to all of Vermoon's inscrutable ancient machinery. **Status: off-screen; whereabouts currently unstated.**

## History with the party

- First worked with the party at **Dry Tide**, on the car — the same job where they unknowingly met the disguised [Blue Dragon](/World/The-Dragons).
- Solved the **Airlantis fuel problem**: worked out how to synthesize fuel for the flying city, giving the party effectively unlimited operation of their base ([The Elemental Cities](/World/The-Elemental-Cities)).

## Using Rico now

- The natural NPC to call when the party wants **Airlantis airborne again**, a teleporter coaxed back to life, or salvaged ship technology reverse-engineered — a friendlier, healthier engineering resource than the ship's dying scientists.
- Where has Rico been during Jak's two-year ascendancy? An engineer who understands the ancients' infrastructure is exactly who the Administration — or the [noble alliance](/Campaign/Plot-Threads/The-Noble-Alliance) — would want on payroll. Which side got there first is an open hook.
