## The campaign in one paragraph

The **Wayward Friends** reunited at Jak Bjornsson's summons, sailed south, survived a fungal siege at Frostwatch Hold, and entered the alien starship buried in the Barrier Peaks — the void Jak cannot see. Over a rapid multi-day survey they cleared Level 1 (medical/crew), the Level 2 theater (mind flayers), the Garden Level (froghemoth, deadly flora), and the lower reactor/cargo deck, recovering **Erleena Riser** alive beneath the **Lighthouse** — the broadcast source of the russet mold. They escaped via the cargo purge and returned to a Frostwatch now occupied by the Spásistren, where a secret council put three paths on the table: containment, orbital purge, or a return trip to find a cure.
