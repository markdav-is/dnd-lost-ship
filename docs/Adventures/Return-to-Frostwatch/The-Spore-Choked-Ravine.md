# The Spore-Choked Ravine

*Return to Frostwatch, descent encounter 1 — atmospheric hazard.*

![The Spore-Choked Ravine](../../.attachments/enc_spore_ravine.jpg)

A narrow mountain pass completely choked with thick, orange-yellow spore cloud — vision obscured beyond 10 feet, settled into the ravine like fog.

## Mechanics

- **Breathing the air:** **DC 15 CON** or **poisoned 1 hour** with vivid hallucinations — the ship's walls around them, the clicking of intellect devourers, companions' faces as distorted alien masks. Fail by 5+ → also **frightened** of a random party member for the duration.
- **Piloting:** **DC 14 DEX** (land vehicles / tinker's tools apply) or the sled clips a wall — everyone aboard **DC 12 DEX** or 1d8 bludgeoning and prone.
- **Flying over:** possible — the ravine is 200 ft long, 80 ft deep; the cloud fills it to within 20 ft of the rim.

## Running it

Primarily atmospheric. Lean into the hallucinations — let players describe what their characters see. The takeaway: **the infection is airborne now**, spreading through the natural environment.
