# Arrival at Frostwatch

*Return to Frostwatch, Act II — roleplay scenes at the occupied hold.*

## Read-aloud: first sight of the hold

![First sight of the occupied hold](../../.attachments/enc_frostwatch_arrival.jpg)

> *Frostwatch Hold rises from the mountain shelf like a clenched fist. The damage from the previous siege has been repaired — new timber on the gatehouse, fresh mortar on the outer wall, the scorched sections of the great hall rebuilt in pale stone that hasn't yet weathered to match the old. Someone has been busy.*
>
> *But the banners are wrong. The old Frostwatch pennants — the grey wolf on white — have been replaced by the seal of the Administrator: an anchor and compass rose intertwined with the mystical symbols of the Temple of Nord. And at every post, at every gate, at every corner of the battlements, stand women in simple, flowing robes with weapons that are anything but simple. Their eyes are calm. Their posture is perfect. Behind each left ear, a faint blue light pulses steadily.*
>
> *In the stables, you can see the tops of military wagons. Heavy ones. The kind that carry things that are not meant to be seen.*
>
> *One of the guards sees you approaching. She does not raise an alarm. She simply turns and walks inside, presumably to inform someone. The gates remain open.*

## The occupation at a glance

~30 Spásistren: 20 initiates (guards/labor) · 8 Sisters (tactical command) · 1 High Sister (Valerius, arrived two days ago, interviewing locals). They are cataloguing supplies, interviewing locals, and unpacking wagons of **alchemical incendiaries, containment equipment, and surveying instruments**. They explain nothing: *"Those matters will be addressed at the council tomorrow."* Polite. Immovable. Never lying — simply not answering.

## Scene: Copper's warning

![Copper's warning](../../.attachments/enc_copper_warning.jpg)

Copper finds the party within the hour — a man who has not slept in three days.

> *He pulls you into the shadow of the granary, away from the nearest guard post. His eyes are red-rimmed and darting.*
>
> *"You made it. Good. Good." He doesn't sound relieved — he sounds like a man confirming a calculation. "Listen to me carefully, because I'm only going to say this once and I need you to believe me."*
>
> *He leans in close. "The spores, the mold, the creatures — that's the part we can see. That's the part that shows up on the body, that we can cut out and burn. But there's something else. Something that doesn't leave marks. I've been watching the locals since the first attack, and some of them are... different. Not infected. Not physically. But they think differently. They make decisions they wouldn't have made before. They protect the infection without knowing they're doing it."*
>
> *He glances toward the nearest Spásistren guard. "And now these women arrive, all connected through that network in their heads, and I'm thinking — what if the infection doesn't need spores? What if it can ride a signal? What if it's already inside the network and nobody knows it yet?"*
>
> *He straightens up and smooths his coat. "I'm probably wrong. I'm usually wrong about the worst things. But I'm right about the worst things often enough that you should listen."*

## Scene: Korrin in the stables

Korrin stands between his dogs and two initiates examining them with clinical detachment. Dogs agitated; his hand on his axe.

- He does not trust the Sisters — their interviews felt like *assessment*: deciding who is expendable. They will not touch his dogs.
- He has overheard: a "council" is coming; the wagons hold **"cleansing materials."**
- *"They're not here to help us. They're here to manage us. There's a difference."*

The party gets comfortable quarters for the night. In the morning: the summons to the Great Hall at the second bell → [The Council of Frostwatch](The-Council-of-Frostwatch.md).
